2. After fixed weights,ask which locations are difficult

The XPINN experiment used chosen coefficients on its interface losses. But within a residual term, some locations can remain much harder to fit. Should every point keep the same emphasis?

Self-adaptive PINNs attach trainable masks to individual points. We will compare local attention with a single shared weight, then check what neither method can see between sampled points.

中文讲稿

XPINN 实验为接口损失选择了固定系数。但同一个残差项里，有些位置可能一直更难拟合。每个点应该始终获得相同强调吗？

自适应 PINNs 为每个点附上可训练掩码。我们把局部注意力与单个共享权重比较，再检查两者在采样点之间都看不到什么。

3. Rightward flow meets a narrow warm layer at the end

Picture a steady rightward flow with a cold left boundary and a warm right boundary. U is dimensionless temperature, and x is position from zero to one.

The narrow rise near the right end is a boundary layer. Arrows illustrate advection in this steady problem. They are not a transient temperature simulation.

中文讲稿

想象一个稳定向右的流动，左边界冷，右边界温暖。u 是无量纲温度，x 是零到一的位置。

右端附近很窄的温度上升区，就是边界层。箭头表示这个稳态问题中的对流，不是瞬态温度模拟。

较小的 ε 使右端边界层很窄。精确指数形状只用于验证，训练没有内部温度标签。

5. Change a point’s emphasis,not its location

X i denotes residual point i. Our main controls use the same sixteen fixed midpoint locations. None of these points moves during training.

M i is the loss multiplier attached to that point. Increasing it makes a residual more costly, but does not insert a new measurement or sample a missing region.

x i 表示第 i 个残差点。主要对照使用相同的十六个固定中点，训练期间位置不变。

m i 是这个点对应的损失乘子。增大它会让该处残差代价更高，却不会加入新测量，也不会采样缺失区域。

N theta is a width-thirty-two tanh network. Theta collects its weights and biases, ninety-seven parameters in total. The displayed construction fixes both endpoint temperatures exactly.

Every control uses this same architecture. A correct endpoint does not certify the interior equation, so we still evaluate the residual away from training points.

每种对照都使用相同架构。正确端点不证明内部方程准确，所以仍要在训练点之外评估残差。

7. Square positive parameters to form nonnegative masks

mi=λi2,ri=εuθ,xx(xi)−uθ,x(xi)m_i=\lambda_i^2,\quad r_i=\varepsilon u_{\theta,xx}(x_i)-u_{\theta,x}(x_i)mi​=λi2​,ri​=εuθ,xx​(xi​)−uθ,x​(xi​)

Lambda i is the adaptive parameter, and its square is the nonnegative multiplier. R i is the differential-equation residual at location x i.

All lambdas start at one and remain positive in these runs. These masks are not probabilities, and their magnitude is not a confidence estimate.

中文讲稿

λ i 是自适应参数，平方后得到非负乘子。r i 是位置 x i 的微分方程残差。

这些运行把所有 λ 初始化为一，并始终保持为正。掩码不是概率，数值大小也不是可信度估计。

8. The network descends while masks update in the opposite direction

min⁡θmax⁡λ>0L,L=1N∑iλi2ri2\min_\theta\max_{\lambda>0}L,\quad L=\frac1N\sum_i\lambda_i^2r_i^2θmin​λ>0max​L,L=N1​i∑​λi2​ri2​

N counts the residual points. The temperature network takes a descent step to reduce L. The mask parameters take an ascent step on that same objective.

Why the opposite directions? A stubborn residual makes its multiplier increase, pressing the network to address it. This is a finite-budget min-max procedure, not a guarantee of a stable global saddle point.

中文讲稿

N 是残差点数。温度网络沿下降方向减小 L，掩码参数则对同一个目标做上升更新。

为什么方向相反？难以消除的残差会推动乘子增大，促使网络处理该处。这是有限预算的极小极大过程，不保证稳定全局鞍点。

9. Squared residuals drive the mask ascent

∂L∂λi=2λiri2N\frac{\partial L}{\partial\lambda_i}=\frac{2\lambda_i r_i^2}{N}∂λi​∂L​=N2λi​ri2​​

For a positive lambda, the displayed derivative is nonnegative. A larger squared residual gives a stronger instantaneous gradient for that mask.

Our optimiser is Adam, so momentum and adaptive scaling also affect the update. A final mask records training history; it is not simply the current residual plotted again.

中文讲稿

当 λ 为正时，显示的导数非负。更大的残差平方，为该掩码提供更强的瞬时梯度。

这里使用 Adam，动量与自适应缩放也影响更新。最终掩码记录了训练历史，并不等于把当前残差重新画一遍。

10. Start with equal masks,then observe how they diverge

Unit initial masks make the initial residual objective identical across our three sixteen-point controls. Their neural initial parameters are paired within each seed.

This differs from the paper’s random mask initialisations. We also omit its optional L-BFGS phase and Gaussian-process interpolation, and adapt only interior residual masks because our endpoints are hard constraints.

中文讲稿

单位初始掩码让三个十六点对照的初始残差目标一致。同一种子内部，神经初始参数也完全配对。

这不同于原文的随机掩码初始化。我们省略可选 L-BFGS 阶段与高斯过程插值；由于端点采用硬约束，只适应内部残差掩码。

The shared control learns just one lambda for all sixteen points. It can strengthen the residual term overall, but cannot choose one location over another.

Comparing it with pointwise masks helps separate scale growth from spatial allocation. It is a diagnostic control, not a claim that the two Adam systems have identical optimisation dynamics.

12. Two optimisers use the same backward pass

loss = mean((lam**2)*(residual**2))
network_optim.zero_grad(); mask_optim.zero_grad()
loss.backward()
network_optim.step()  # Adam descent
mask_optim.step()     # Adam(maximize=True)

One backward pass supplies gradients to both parameter groups. Adam descends for the network and ascends for the masks. Reversing only the mask direction is essential.

网络学习率为零点零零三，掩码学习率为零点零零五。下载实现包含完整求导与记录代码。

19. A mask cannot see a location that was never sampled

r(xi)=0  for all i  ⇏  r(x)=0  everywherer(x_i)=0\;\text{for all }i\;\not\Rightarrow\;r(x)=0\;\text{everywhere}r(xi​)=0for all i⇒r(x)=0everywhere

Imagine a smooth compact bump placed entirely between two sampled locations. It and its first two derivatives can vanish at every training point.

Adding that bump can preserve every sampled residual while changing the field between points. This is a function-space counterexample, not a claim that our particular network exactly represents that bump.

中文讲稿

想象在两个采样位置之间放一个平滑紧支撑小鼓包。它及前两阶导数可以在所有训练点上为零。

加入这个鼓包，可以保持所有采样残差不变，却改变点间的场。这是函数空间反例，不是说当前网络能够精确表示这个鼓包。

20. Field error and held-out residuals need not rank models the same way

We compare against the exact field at one thousand and one locations, and check residuals at nine hundred and ninety-seven shifted positions. Neither set supplies interior training labels.

Some sparse fits have relatively small field errors but substantial held-out residuals. A denser fit can show the opposite ranking at this budget. Report both quantities instead of selecting the one that favours a method.

中文讲稿

在一千零一个位置与精确场比较，并在九百九十七个偏移位置检查残差。两组位置都不提供内部训练标签。

有些稀疏拟合的场误差较小，点间残差却明显偏大。这个预算下，密集拟合还可能呈现相反排名。应同时报告，而不是挑选有利于某种方法的指标。

We use hard endpoints, unit initial masks and no L-BFGS or Gaussian-process interpolation. All failed main-budget outcomes remain downloadable, alongside the separately labelled longer diagnostic.

22. If sampled residuals vanish,can larger masks detect an unseen bump?

∂λiL=0when ri=0\partial_{\lambda_i}L=0\quad\text{when }r_i=0∂λi​​L=0when ri​=0

Suppose every sampled residual is exactly zero, but a smooth error bump lies between the points. What does the mask gradient tell us?

It is zero at all those locations, so mask growth has no signal to expose the bump. Use independent checks and informative sampling. This is why adaptive weighting and adaptive sampling solve different problems.

中文讲稿

假设每个采样残差都严格为零，但点之间有平滑误差鼓包。掩码梯度会告诉我们什么？

这些位置的梯度全为零，掩码增长没有信号来揭示鼓包。需要独立验证和有效采样。因此，自适应权重与自适应采样解决不同问题。
