1. Paper 06: gPINN and spatial residual gradients

Temporal weighting organised when residuals receive emphasis, so we now ask what information is enforced at each spatial point. We will connect a heated-rod equilibrium to coordinate derivatives of the Poisson residual and a gradient-enhanced objective. Finally, twelve CPU runs will compare temperature, slopes, independent residuals and a denser ordinary-PINN control.

中文讲稿

时间加权安排了何时强调残差，现在追问每个空间点约束什么信息。先把受热细杆平衡连接到 Poisson 残差的坐标导数与梯度增强目标。最后用十二次 CPU 运行比较温度、斜率、独立残差，以及更稠密普通 PINN 对照。

2. From temporal order to physics between samples

The previous presentation changed which times receive training emphasis, but every residual was still checked at finitely many locations. That leaves a spatial question about what happens between those locations. A small mismatch at sampled points need not control how rapidly the mismatch varies nearby.

This paper adds derivatives of the residual with respect to input coordinates. It is called a gradient-enhanced PINN, or gPINN. We will distinguish that spatial derivative from the parameter gradients studied earlier, then test the added information and its computational cost.

中文讲稿

上一篇改变了训练关注哪些时刻，但残差仍只在有限位置检查。于是还需要追问这些位置之间的空间行为。采样点失配很小，并不一定能控制附近失配变化有多快。

这篇论文加入残差对输入坐标的导数，称为梯度增强 PINN，简称 gPINN。先区分这个空间导数与之前研究的参数梯度，再检验增加的信息及其计算成本。

3. Start with a rod containing heating and cooling

To give the spatial problem a physical picture, consider a rod with fixed temperatures at its two ends. Internal heaters and coolers change its equilibrium temperature profile. The animation ramps the source through a family of equilibria, rather than integrating a heat transient.

Colour and the gold curve represent temperature u along position x. The endpoints remain fixed while the interior profile develops several spatial scales. This lets us ask whether a network captures not only temperature values but also their spatial slopes.

中文讲稿

为了给空间问题一个物理图像，考虑两端温度固定的细杆。内部加热与冷却装置改变平衡温度曲线。动画逐步改变源项，展示一组平衡状态，不是在积分热瞬态。

颜色与金线表示沿位置 x 的温度 u。两端保持固定，内部曲线逐渐形成多个空间尺度。由此可以追问网络是否不仅捕捉温度值，也捕捉空间斜率。

4. How does steady heat balance give a Poisson equation?

−uxx=s(x),x∈[0,π]-u_{xx}=s(x),\qquad x\in[0,\pi]−uxx​=s(x),x∈[0,π]

The equilibrium picture becomes a Poisson equation after conductivity is normalised to one. U is the dimensionless temperature, x is position, and s is the prescribed source. Positive and negative source values represent heating and cooling in this interpretation.

The minus sign makes the curvature oppose a positive heating source. The interval runs from zero to pi, matching the original paper’s one-dimensional example. We retain this equation rather than claiming a new large thermal simulation.

中文讲稿

把导热系数归一化为一后，平衡图像变成 Poisson 方程。u 是无量纲温度，x 是位置，s 是给定源项。在这个解释中，正负源项分别表示加热与冷却。

负号使曲率与正的加热源相对。区间从零到 pi，与原论文的一维例子一致。保留这个方程，而不是声称进行了新的大型热模拟。

5. Fixed temperatures select the equilibrium state

u(0)=0,u(π)=πu(0)=0,\qquad u(\pi)=\piu(0)=0,u(π)=π

The curvature equation needs endpoint values to select a particular temperature profile. The left endpoint is held at zero and the right at pi. These are prescribed dimensionless values, not unknown coefficients to infer.

Without both values a straight-line offset could leave the source equation unchanged, as we saw in the gradient-pathology paper. Here we remove that ambiguity by enforcing the endpoints exactly. This keeps the comparison focused on interior residual information.

中文讲稿

曲率方程需要端点值，才能选出具体温度曲线。左端固定为零，右端固定为 pi。这是给定的无量纲值，不是要识别的未知系数。

如果没有两个端点值，附加直线可能不改变源项方程，这在梯度病态论文中已经见过。这里精确满足端点以去掉歧义，使比较聚焦在内部残差信息上。

6. Make the endpoint conditions hold by construction

uθ(x)=x+x(π−x)Nθ(x)u_\theta(x)=x+x(\pi-x)N_\theta(x)uθ​(x)=x+x(π−x)Nθ​(x)

The original paper’s surrogate makes the boundary treatment explicit. N theta is a freely trained network and theta denotes its parameters. The multiplier x times pi minus x vanishes at both endpoints.

Consequently, the output equals zero on the left and pi on the right for every parameter value. This hard constraint is shared by PINN and gPINN in our comparison. It supplies the boundary values without revealing any interior reference labels.

中文讲稿

原论文的替代表达式让边界处理变得明确。N theta 是自由训练的网络，theta 表示其参数。乘子 x 乘以 pi 减 x，在两个端点都为零。

因此，无论参数是什么，输出在左端等于零，右端等于 pi。比较中的 PINN 与 gPINN 使用相同硬约束。它提供边界值，不泄露任何内部参考标签。

7. Which spatial scales appear in the prescribed source?

s(x)=∑j=14jsin⁡(jx)+8sin⁡(8x)s(x)=\sum_{j=1}^{4}j\sin(jx)+8\sin(8x)s(x)=j=1∑4​jsin(jx)+8sin(8x)

With the endpoints fixed, the prescribed source determines the interior shape. The index j runs from one to four in the first sum. An additional sine component has spatial frequency eight.

These components create a temperature field with broad structure and finer variation. They are known source information in the differential equation, not measured solution gradients. The analytic solution is reserved for evaluating the trained fields and slopes.

中文讲稿

端点固定后，给定源项决定内部形状。第一个求和中的索引 j 从一到四。额外的正弦分量具有空间频率八。

这些分量让温度场同时具有宽尺度结构与细节变化。它们是微分方程中已知的源项信息，不是测得的解梯度。解析解保留用于评估训练后的场与斜率。

8. First define what the ordinary PINN minimises

rθ(x)=−uθ,xx(x)−s(x)r_\theta(x)=-u_{\theta,xx}(x)-s(x)rθ​(x)=−uθ,xx​(x)−s(x)

The physical equation now defines a residual for the neural prediction. R theta is negative network curvature minus the known source. An ordinary PINN minimises its mean square at the enforcement locations.

The equation is supposed to hold throughout the interval, but the objective checks finitely many points. That sampling distinction motivates the added derivative information. It does not mean that a standard PINN cannot produce an accurate solution.

中文讲稿

现在由物理方程定义网络预测的残差。r theta 是负的网络曲率减去已知源项。普通 PINN 在约束位置最小化它的均方。

方程应当在整个区间成立，但目标只检查有限点。这种采样区别引出额外的导数信息，不代表标准 PINN 无法产生准确解。

9. This gradient is not the parameter gradient

∇θL;∂xrθ\nabla_\theta\mathcal L\qquad;\qquad\partial_x r_\theta∇θ​L;∂x​rθ​

The word gradient now needs careful disambiguation. In the earlier balancing paper we differentiated a loss with respect to trainable parameters theta. That gradient tells the optimiser how to move the network.

Here we differentiate the residual with respect to spatial position x. This measures how the physics mismatch changes from one location to another. The optimiser still needs parameter gradients afterwards, but the added loss is defined using this coordinate derivative.

中文讲稿

现在必须区分梯度这个词的两种含义。之前的平衡论文对可训练参数 theta 求损失导数，告诉优化器怎样移动网络。

这里对空间位置 x 求残差导数，测量物理失配从一个位置到另一个位置怎样变化。之后优化器仍需要参数梯度，但新增损失使用的是这个坐标导数。

10. Use a local tangent to explain the added information

r(x+h)≈r(x)+hrx(x)r(x+h)\approx r(x)+h r_x(x)r(x+h)≈r(x)+hrx​(x)

The coordinate derivative has a simple local geometric meaning. Here h is a small spatial offset from an enforcement point. The dashed line is a tangent to an illustrative residual curve, not a saved network prediction.

For a small offset, residual value and residual slope together describe the nearby mismatch to first order. Reducing both can therefore constrain local behaviour more than reducing only the sampled value. The approximation still has a remainder, so it is not a guarantee of zero residual everywhere.

中文讲稿

坐标导数有简单的局部几何含义。h 表示从约束点出发的小空间偏移。虚线是示意残差曲线的切线，不是保存的网络预测。

对于较小偏移，残差值与残差斜率共同描述附近失配的一阶变化。因此同时降低两者，比只降低样本值提供更多局部约束。但近似仍有余项，不能保证处处残差为零。

11. Add the residual derivative to the objective

L=Lr+γLg\mathcal L=\mathcal L_r+\gamma\mathcal L_gL=Lr​+γLg​

The local argument leads to the gPINN objective. L r is the mean squared residual, and L g is the mean squared spatial residual derivative. Gamma is a chosen loss weight controlling the extra derivative term.

Both terms use the same sixteen enforcement points in the sparse comparison. No new solution or solution-slope observations are added. The weight is fixed during each run rather than controlled by the adaptive rule from the earlier gradient paper.

中文讲稿

局部解释引出 gPINN 目标。L r 是残差均方，L g 是空间残差导数均方。gamma 是所选损失权重，控制额外导数项。

稀疏比较中，两项使用相同的十六个约束点。不增加任何解值或解斜率观测。每次运行保持权重固定，而不是使用之前梯度论文的自适应规则。

12. Why does a third temperature derivative appear?

rθ,x=−uθ,xxx−sxr_{\theta,x}=-u_{\theta,xxx}-s_xrθ,x​=−uθ,xxx​−sx​

Differentiating the defined residual explains the higher derivative in the implementation. A second derivative of temperature becomes a third derivative. The known source must also be differentiated, with the same minus sign.

The source derivative is available because the forcing function is prescribed analytically. It is not the derivative of a hidden reference solution supplied as a label. This distinction is essential when judging what information the enhanced method actually receives.

中文讲稿

对已定义残差求导，就能解释实现中的高阶导数。温度二阶导数变为三阶导数。已知源项也必须求导，而且保持相同负号。

源项以解析形式给定，因此它的导数可计算。这不是把隐藏参考解的导数当成标签提供。判断增强方法实际获得了什么信息时，这一区别尤其重要。

13. What do higher derivatives require from the network?

The third derivative changes the computational requirements as well as the information used. We choose smooth hyperbolic-tangent activations because the residual needs repeated input differentiation. A piecewise-linear activation would not provide the same ordinary higher derivatives.

Creating those derivative graphs also adds cost to each optimiser step. Fewer enforcement points therefore do not automatically mean a faster CPU run. We include a denser ordinary-PINN control rather than comparing point counts alone.

中文讲稿

三阶导数不仅改变使用的信息，也改变计算要求。由于残差需要重复对输入求导，选择平滑双曲正切激活。分段线性激活不能提供相同的普通高阶导数。

创建导数计算图也增加每步优化成本。因此约束点较少，并不自动代表 CPU 运行更快。除了比较点数，还加入更稠密的普通 PINN 对照。

19. Zero sampled values and slopes are not a global proof

rdemo(x)=cos⁡2(16x)r_{\rm demo}(x)=\cos^2(16x)rdemo​(x)=cos2(16x)

A simple residual illustration explains why derivative enhancement does not remove the need for validation. At our sixteen midpoint locations, cosine of sixteen x is zero. The squared cosine residual therefore has both zero value and zero first derivative at every one of those locations.

Between the locations, the same residual reaches nonzero values. This is a logical counterexample about finite constraints, not a claim that it is the trained network’s residual. More derivative conditions provide information, but they are not a substitute for sampling and independent checks.

中文讲稿

一个简单残差示意说明，导数增强为何不能取消验证需求。在本实验的十六个中点位置，十六 x 的余弦为零。因此余弦平方残差在每个位置的值和一阶导数都为零。

但在这些位置之间，同一残差会达到非零值。这是关于有限约束的逻辑反例，不是在声称它就是训练网络的残差。更多导数条件提供信息，却不能替代采样与独立检查。

22. Which part of the paper is implemented here?

This episode implements the paper’s original one-dimensional Poisson equation and its hard-endpoint surrogate. We change the network width and depth, enforcement sets and optimiser update budget to keep the experiment local. The reported errors are our own executed outcomes.

We do not implement the inverse examples, multidimensional problems or residual-adaptive refinement in this presentation. Higher derivative graphs also cost more per step, so fewer points alone do not prove faster training. Three seeds demonstrate a local trade-off rather than a universal optimum.

中文讲稿

本篇实现了论文原始一维 Poisson 方程及其硬端点替代表达式。为保持本机可运行，修改网络宽深、约束点集与优化更新预算。报告的是自己实际运行的误差。

本篇没有实现反问题、多维问题或残差自适应加点。高阶导数计算图每步也更昂贵，因此点少不能单独证明训练更快。三个种子展示局部取舍，而不是普适最优。

23. Predict before changing the points or weight

The sparse and dense controls give a small follow-up exercise without a new simulation. First keep the network and seed fixed and predict whether more enforcement locations or a stronger derivative penalty should help this source. State which error, temperature or slope, you expect to change more.

The downloadable script exposes all four policy definitions near the run function. Make one controlled modification in a copy and use a new output filename. Compare actual training time, both validation errors and the held-out residuals before deciding that the change helped.

24. Why study where enforcement points are placed next?

Gradient enhancement changes what information is enforced at each location, but we still used fixed evenly spaced points. The independent residual profiles suggest another choice about where the limited points should go. That motivates a separate paper on residual-based adaptive sampling.

The next presentation compares sampling policies on a small analytically checkable boundary-layer problem. It keeps the candidate-search cost and total point budget visible. This separates adding derivative information from choosing more informative enforcement locations.

Teaching checks and reference answers

Ask for a prediction before running the exercise. Analytic expectations do not guarantee an optimiser outcome.

Predict before changing the points or weight

Neither more points nor a larger derivative penalty guarantees improvement under a fixed optimisation budget. The saved dense ordinary control is already accurate, and the preferred enhancement weight varies by seed. Check temperature and slope errors separately, plus held-out residual and residual-gradient errors. Extra derivatives have a computational cost, and zero residual values and slopes at finitely many points are not a global certificate.

先预测改变点数与权重的影响

在固定优化预算下，更多点和更大导数惩罚都不保证改善。保存的稠密普通对照已经很准确，较好的增强权重也随种子改变。分别检查温度和斜率误差，以及独立残差和残差梯度误差。额外求导有计算成本；有限点处残差值和斜率为零，不是全局正确性的证明。
