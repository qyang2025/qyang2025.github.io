1. Paper14: Bayesian PINNs and posterior uncertainty

First connect a noisy heated rod to a Bayesian neural model. Then explain the full posterior and replay genuine CPU sampling. Finally inspect coefficient intervals,chain diagnostics and the limits of conditional uncertainty before moving to hidden flow.

先把含噪受热杆连接到贝叶斯神经模型。再解释完整后验并回放真实 CPU 采样。最后检查系数区间、链诊断与条件不确定性的边界，再衔接隐藏流动。

2. Local representations change predictions; posteriors describe uncertainty

The previous paper changed how a solution is represented in space. Its different random starts produced different trained curves, but that variability was not automatically Bayesian uncertainty.

This paper assigns probabilities to neural weights and an unknown physical coefficient. We will perform actual posterior sampling in a tiny problem and keep the numerical diagnostics visible.

中文讲稿

上篇改变了解在空间中的表示。不同随机起点产生不同训练曲线，但这种变动不会自动成为贝叶斯不确定性。

本篇给神经权重与未知物理系数赋予概率。我们在极小问题中进行真实后验采样，并展示数值诊断。

3. Can noisy temperature and source data identify conductivity?

Consider a unit rod in steady thermal balance with zero temperature at both endpoints. Position is x, temperature relative to the endpoints is u, and the positive constant k is unknown.

We observe a few noisy temperatures and noisy source values. Moving heat arrows are only a steady-flow illustration; our experiment does not solve a transient heating process.

中文讲稿

考虑两端温度为零、处于稳态热平衡的单位杆。x 是位置，u 是相对端点的温度，正常数 k 未知。

我们观测少量含噪温度与热源值。移动热流箭头只是稳态示意，实验不求解瞬态加热过程。

4. The equation connects temperature shape and the unknown coefficient

−kuxx=f,u(0)=u(1)=0-k u_{xx}=f,\quad u(0)=u(1)=0−kuxx​=f,u(0)=u(1)=0

F denotes the source, and the subscript x x means the second spatial derivative. In this synthetic experiment the validation reference is sine pi x and the true k is zero point seven.

The reference coefficient and exact field are reserved for validation, not supplied as parameter labels. Noisy source and temperature measurements constrain their relationship through this equation.

f 表示热源，下标 x x 表示空间二阶导数。合成实验的验证参考为 sin πx，真实 k 为零点七。

参考系数和精确场只用于验证，不作为参数标签。含噪热源与温度测量通过方程约束两者关系。

5. Treat every neural weight as an unknown random quantity

uθ=x(1−x)Nθ(x),k=eηu_\theta=x(1-x)N_\theta(x),\quad k=e^\etauθ​=x(1−x)Nθ​(x),k=eη

Theta contains ten weights and biases of a three-hidden-unit tanh network. The factor x times one minus x enforces both endpoint values exactly.

Eta is the logarithm of k, so exponentiation keeps conductivity positive. We sample all eleven unknowns together rather than only perturbing a final fitted coefficient.

中文讲稿

θ 包含三隐单元双曲正切网络的十个权重和偏置。x 乘一减 x 因子使两端值严格为零。

η 是 k 的对数，因此指数映射保证导热率为正。一起采样全部十一个未知量，而不是只扰动最终拟合系数。

6. Priors express modelling assumptions, not extra observations

η∼N(0,1),θj∼N(0,sj2)\eta\sim\mathcal N(0,1),\quad\theta_j\sim\mathcal N(0,s_j^2)η∼N(0,1),θj​∼N(0,sj2​)

The parameters have independent Gaussian priors. Nine neural parameters use standard deviation one point five, the output offset uses three, and eta uses one.

These choices affect the posterior and must be stated. The prior for eta is centred at zero, corresponding to median conductivity one, rather than secretly centred on the known synthetic truth.

中文讲稿

参数采用独立高斯先验。九个神经参数的标准差为一点五，输出偏置为三，η 为一。

这些选择会影响后验，必须说明。η 的先验以零为中心，对应导热率中位数一，没有暗中以合成真值为中心。

7. The noise model determines how measurement discrepancies are interpreted

yu=uθ(xu)+ϵu,yf=−kuθ,xx(xf)+ϵfy_u=u_\theta(x_u)+\epsilon_u,\quad y_f=-k u_{\theta,xx}(x_f)+\epsilon_fyu​=uθ​(xu​)+ϵu​,yf​=−kuθ,xx​(xf​)+ϵf​

Y u and y f are the measured temperature and source values at their sensor locations. Epsilon denotes independent zero-mean Gaussian measurement noise.

We compare low noise with standard deviations zero point zero three and zero point one, and high noise with zero point one and zero point three. Both cases reuse the same standard-normal draws before scaling, making the comparison paired.

中文讲稿

y u 和 y f 是传感位置的温度与热源测量，ε 表示独立零均值高斯测量噪声。

低噪声的两个标准差分别为零点零三和零点一，高噪声为零点一和零点三。两者在缩放前复用相同标准正态样本，构成配对比较。

8. The posterior combines prior assumptions and likelihood

p(θ,η∣y)∝p(y∣θ,η) p(θ) p(η)p(\theta,\eta\mid y)\propto p(y\mid\theta,\eta)\,p(\theta)\,p(\eta)p(θ,η∣y)∝p(y∣θ,η)p(θ)p(η)

Y collects both observation sets. The likelihood measures compatibility with the stated noise model, while the prior weights the allowed parameter configurations.

The posterior is conditional on this architecture, equation, prior and noise assumptions. It does not automatically include unknown model error or a wrong physical equation.

中文讲稿

y 收集两组观测。似然衡量参数与所设噪声模型的相容程度，先验为参数配置赋予权重。

后验以这个网络、方程、先验和噪声假设为条件，不会自动包含未知模型误差或错误物理方程。

9. A loss-shaped potential is used to sample a distribution

U=12∥q/s∥2+12∥(u−yu)/σu∥2+12∥(−kuxx−yf)/σf∥2U=\tfrac12\|q/s\|^2+\tfrac12\|(u-y_u)/\sigma_u\|^2+\tfrac12\|(-ku_{xx}-y_f)/\sigma_f\|^2U=21​∥q/s∥2+21​∥(u−yu​)/σu​∥2+21​∥(−kuxx​−yf​)/σf​∥2

Q collects theta and eta, s contains their prior scales, and sigma denotes the known measurement standard deviations. Constants independent of q can be omitted from this sampling potential.

Minimising this expression finds a posterior mode, not the whole posterior. We use that mode only to prepare a sampler, then retain actual Markov-chain draws.

中文讲稿

q 收集 θ 与 η，s 包含先验尺度，σ 是已知测量标准差。与 q 无关的常数可以从采样势能中省略。

最小化该式只找到后验众数，不是完整后验。我们只用众数准备采样器，随后保留真实马尔可夫链样本。

10. Use NUTS sampling, not repeated fits labelled as a posterior

Hamiltonian Monte Carlo uses derivatives of the log posterior to propose coordinated parameter moves. NUTS adapts the trajectory length and is the HMC variant used in this implementation.

Four separately initialised chains are run sequentially on one CPU thread for each noise level. Each keeps five hundred draws after four hundred warmup transitions; four optimiser restarts would not be equivalent.

中文讲稿

哈密顿蒙特卡洛利用对数后验导数，提出协调参数移动。NUTS 自适应轨迹长度，是本实现使用的 HMC 变体。

每个噪声水平使用四条独立初始化、单 CPU 线程顺序运行的链。每链预热四百次后保留五百个样本；四次优化重启并不等价。

11. Change sampling coordinates without replacing the posterior by a Gaussian

q=qMAP+Lzq=q_{\rm MAP}+Lzq=qMAP​+Lz

Q M A P is the fitted posterior mode, L is a fixed invertible matrix based on a regularised Hessian there, and z is the sampler coordinate. This rescales local curvature for more efficient exploration.

We still evaluate the original nonlinear potential at every mapped q. The Jacobian determinant is constant, so it does not alter relative probabilities; this is not a Laplace or Gaussian posterior approximation.

中文讲稿

q MAP 是拟合后验众数，L 是基于该点正则化 Hessian 的固定可逆矩阵，z 是采样坐标。该变换调整局部曲率尺度以改善探索。

每个映射 q 仍计算原始非线性势能。雅可比行列式为常数，不改变相对概率；这不是 Laplace 或高斯后验近似。

16. Check chain behaviour before trusting posterior summaries

R^k≈1.020  or  1.011,ESSk≈256  or  319\widehat R_k\approx1.020\;\text{or}\;1.011,\quad\mathrm{ESS}_k\approx256\;\text{or}\;319Rk​≈1.020or1.011,ESSk​≈256or319

R hat compares variation across and within split chains. The effective sample size,or E S S,accounts for correlation between retained draws,so two thousand draws do not imply two thousand independent observations.

The displayed diagnostics are for conductivity under low and high noise. They remain above a strict one point zero one R hat target,so these small chains should be presented as limited posterior estimates,not certified final inference.

中文讲稿

Rhat 比较拆分链之间与链内部的变化。有效样本量 ESS 考虑保留样本之间的相关性，因此两千个样本不等于两千个独立观测。

显示的是低、高噪声下导热系数诊断。Rhat 仍高于严格的一点零一目标，所以小链只能作为有限后验估计，不能宣称推断已获认证。

17. Observable agreement does not prove full weight-space exploration

Different hidden-unit permutations and sign patterns can represent similar functions. Our weight diagnostics remain large,even when temperature-centre and conductivity diagnostics are much closer.

This does not prove that all unexplored modes are harmless symmetries. We report the weight-space limitation and avoid claiming that these short chains fully explore the global neural posterior.

中文讲稿

不同隐单元排列与符号组合可以表示相似函数。即使温度中心和导热系数诊断更接近，权重诊断仍然很大。

这不证明所有未探索模式都是无害对称性。必须报告权重空间限制，避免声称这些短链充分探索了全局神经后验。

18. Failures and diagnostic retries belong in the experiment record

One initial low-noise chain had a divergent transition,and a high-noise attempt reached the ninety-second CPU cap. We retained those records rather than silently hiding them.

Diagnostic retries used target acceptance zero point nine nine and a one-hundred-and-eighty-second cap. Final retained chains have no divergences,but that alone does not establish convergence or eliminate the remaining R hat limitations.

中文讲稿

最初一条低噪声链有发散转移，一次高噪声尝试触发九十秒 CPU 上限。我们保留这些记录，没有暗中隐藏。

诊断重跑采用零点九九目标接受率与一百八十秒上限。最终保留链没有发散，但这一点不证明收敛，也不消除仍存在的 Rhat 限制。

19. Pointwise field intervals are not whole-curve coverage

[Q0.025(u(x)),Q0.975(u(x))][Q_{0.025}(u(x)),Q_{0.975}(u(x))][Q0.025​(u(x)),Q0.975​(u(x))]

Q denotes a posterior quantile at a fixed spatial location. Connecting those intervals gives the blue band,but it is not automatically a ninety-five-percent simultaneous band for an entire function.

The fraction of one synthetic reference curve inside that band is only a descriptive check. Statistical calibration would require repeated datasets and a clearly defined coverage experiment.

中文讲稿

Q 表示固定空间位置的后验分位数。连接这些区间得到蓝带，却不会自动成为整条函数百分之九十五的同时覆盖带。

一条合成参考曲线落入带内的点比例，只是描述性检查。统计校准需要重复数据集与明确定义的覆盖实验。

20. Uncertainty is conditional on assumptions and computation

We sample all eleven parameters in a genuine nonlinear Bayesian neural model. However,noise scales are assumed known,the equation is assumed correct,and the network is tiny.

We do not reproduce the paper’s wide architectures,variational comparisons or large benchmarks. Short CPU chains illustrate the mechanism while leaving explicit convergence and model-adequacy limitations.

中文讲稿

我们在真实非线性贝叶斯神经模型中采样全部十一个参数。但是噪声尺度假设已知，方程假设正确，网络也极小。

没有复现原文宽网络、变分比较或大型基准。短 CPU 链展示机制，同时明确保留收敛与模型充分性限制。

21. Do more sampler draws make the physics more certain?

Pause and distinguish two actions: doubling retained sampler draws,or adding an informative temperature sensor. Which one changes the posterior distribution defined by the observations?

More draws improve numerical estimation of the same target if the chain explores it well. A new informative observation can change the target; neither action fixes a wrong equation or an unidentified parameter automatically.

中文讲稿

停一下，区分两个操作：把保留采样数加倍，或者增加一个有信息的温度传感器。哪一个会改变由观测定义的后验分布？

如果链探索良好，更多样本会改善同一目标的数值估计。新的有效观测能够改变目标；两者都不会自动修正错误方程或不可辨识参数。

22. Next: how much hidden physics does an image actually contain?

Bayesian inference describes uncertainty conditional on a stated model and measurements. It cannot create information that those measurements do not contain.

The next paper asks whether visible dye can reveal hidden velocity. A small transport example will show why excellent concentration reconstruction may still leave a flow component undetermined.

Teaching checks and reference answers

Ask for a prediction before running the exercise. Analytic expectations do not guarantee an optimiser outcome.

Do more posterior draws narrow physical uncertainty?

Not necessarily. More well-explored Markov-chain draws reduce Monte Carlo error in estimating a fixed posterior,not the uncertainty of the posterior itself. A new informative observation can change that posterior. Neither action automatically corrects an inadequate likelihood,wrong equation or unidentifiable direction. Inspect observable and weight diagnostics; zero divergences alone is not a convergence certificate.

p(q∣y)≠p(q∣y,ynew)p(q\mid y)\ne p(q\mid y,y_{\rm new})p(q∣y)=p(q∣y,ynew​)

更多后验采样会缩小物理不确定性吗？

不一定。更多充分探索的马尔可夫链样本降低固定后验估计中的蒙特卡洛误差，不直接缩小后验本身的不确定性。新的有效观测可以改变后验。两者都不能自动修正不足的似然、错误方程或不可辨识方向。应检查可观测量与权重诊断，零发散本身不是收敛证书。
