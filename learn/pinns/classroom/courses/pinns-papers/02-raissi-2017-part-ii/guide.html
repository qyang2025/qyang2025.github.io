Protocol

Truth: lambda1=1, lambda2=0.05; x in [-1,1], t in [0,0.5]. The synthetic generator uses a sine initial profile and zero endpoints, but those are not supplied as extra training labels. The optimiser fits 256 scattered observations and residual tests at the same coordinates, matching the co-location arrangement of the paper. A 2–24–24–24–1 smooth network plus two scalar coefficient variables has 1299 parameters. Initial coefficients are 0.5 and 0.1, with positive diffusion enforced through softplus. Adam 2400 steps plus capped L-BFGS, float64, four CPU threads, network seed 11. Three runs are capped at 90 training seconds each; startup and verification add overhead.

The teaching viscosity is larger and the time window shorter than the original benchmark. The network, data count and optimiser schedule also differ. Ground truth is used only to generate synthetic observations and evaluate results, never in the fitting objective. No reference derivatives, initial labels or endpoint labels are given to the optimiser.

1. Identify Burgers coefficients from observations

Part I turned a Burgers equation with known coefficients into a PINN, so today we use Part II to identify unknown coefficients. First we will ask what information observations provide, then connect the field and coefficients through one training objective. Finally, we will inspect actual parameter trajectories, a noise comparison and sensor placement to judge the evidence for identification.

中文讲稿

第一篇把已知系数的 Burgers 方程写成了 PINN，现在我们用第二部分论文识别未知系数。先问观测提供了什么，再把场和系数连入同一个训练目标。最后检查真实参数轨迹、噪声对照和传感器位置，判断识别结果是否有依据。

2. From predicting a field to identifying its law

Part I gave us a field prediction when the equation coefficients were known, so the next unresolved question is what to do when they are not. In a forward problem, known coefficients help determine the field. An inverse problem starts with observed field values and asks which coefficients explain their evolution.

The second box changes the scientific question rather than simply increasing network size. Part II makes the coefficients trainable alongside the network. Today we will inspect that dependency, run a small Burgers experiment and test what the observations can actually identify.

中文讲稿

第一篇在系数已知时预测了一个场，但如果系数未知，该怎么办？在正问题中，已知系数帮助确定场。反问题从观测场值出发，追问哪些系数能解释这些观测的演化。

第二个框改变的是科学问题，而不是简单增大网络。第二部分让方程系数和网络一起参与训练。本课将检查这种依赖关系，运行小型 Burgers 实验，并检验观测究竟能识别什么。

To identify a law from data, first picture the phenomenon that the data describe. Position x is horizontal, the dimensionless velocity-like field u is vertical, and t denotes time. The cyan dashed curve is the starting profile, while the gold curve shows its evolving shape.

5. Which coefficients are unknown?

Classroom display: narration-controlled physical diagram or saved-result replay.

Measurements give field values, so we still need a model of how those values evolve. The subscripts t, x and double x mean time change, spatial slope and spatial curvature. Lambda one multiplies nonlinear transport, while lambda two multiplies diffusion.

In this dimensionless teaching problem both coefficients are unknown scalars, and diffusion is assumed positive. The negative sign comes from moving diffusion to the left side of the balance law. The animated profile and the equation describe the same evolving field, not two independent models.

在本无量纲教学问题中，两者都是未知标量，同时假设扩散为正。负号来自把扩散项移到平衡方程左侧。动画轮廓和公式描述的是同一个演化场，不是两个独立模型。

6. What do network weights and physical coefficients do?

uθ(x,t),λ=(λ1,λ2)u_\theta(x,t),\qquad\lambda=(\lambda_1,\lambda_2)uθ​(x,t),λ=(λ1​,λ2​)

The unknown equation introduces a second group of trainable quantities, so we should separate their roles. Theta is the vector of network weights and biases, which shapes a smooth field prediction u theta. It is not a single physical coefficient.

Lambda is the pair of physical coefficients shared by every coordinate in this model. Changing theta can change the whole predicted function, while changing lambda changes how its derivatives must balance. Joint training must therefore find a compatible field and coefficient pair.

中文讲稿

未知方程引入了第二类可训练量，因此先区分各自作用。theta 是网络权重和偏置组成的向量，用来塑造光滑场预测 u theta。它不是一个物理系数。

lambda 是这个模型在所有坐标上共享的一对物理系数。改变 theta 可以改变整个预测函数，改变 lambda 则改变这些导数应满足的平衡。联合训练要找到相互兼容的场和系数。

7. How do candidate coefficients enter the physics test?

fθ,λ=uθ,t+λ1uθuθ,x−λ2uθ,xxf_{\theta,\lambda}=u_{\theta,t}+\lambda_1u_\theta u_{\theta,x}-\lambda_2u_{\theta,xx}fθ,λ​=uθ,t​+λ1​uθ​uθ,x​−λ2​uθ,xx​

Separating the roles is useful only if we connect them computationally, so now we form the residual. Automatic differentiation supplies coordinate derivatives of the same smooth network. We combine those derivatives with candidate lambda values to obtain f, the local equation mismatch.

The second box is that candidate balance at one coordinate. Its value depends on both theta and lambda, so a residual loss can send gradients into both groups. A small residual by itself still cannot tell us whether the predicted field matches the observations.

中文讲稿

只有在计算上把两类量连起来，这种区分才有用，因此现在构造残差。自动微分给出同一光滑网络的坐标导数。把这些导数与候选 lambda 值组合，就得到局部方程失配 f。

第二个框表示某个坐标上的候选平衡。它同时依赖 theta 和 lambda，所以残差损失能把梯度传到两类量。残差小本身仍不能说明预测场符合观测。

8. First require the field to explain the observations

Lu=1N∑i=1N∣uθ(xi,ti)−yi∣2\mathcal L_u=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left|u_\theta(x_i,t_i)-y_i\right|^2Lu​=N1​i=1∑N​∣uθ​(xi​,ti​)−yi​∣2

The physical test needs an observational anchor, so we first measure field mismatch. N is the number of observations, and i selects one coordinate pair and its measured value. Subtract y i from the network prediction at that same position and time.

Square the difference and average over the N samples to obtain L u. This term directly constrains theta, because the field network is u theta. With this architecture it does not directly use lambda, a distinction that will matter in our control experiment.

中文讲稿

物理检验需要观测作为锚点，因此先计算场值失配。N 是观测总数，i 选取其中一个坐标对及测量值。用该位置、该时间的网络预测减去 y i。

将差平方，并对 N 个样本取平均，就得到 L u。这一项直接约束 theta，因为场网络是 u theta。在这个结构下，它没有直接使用 lambda，这一点会在对照实验中变得很重要。

9. Where do we test the equation?

Lf=1N∑i=1N∣fθ,λ(xi,ti)∣2\mathcal L_f=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left|f_{\theta,\lambda}(x_i,t_i)\right|^2Lf​=N1​i=1∑N​∣fθ,λ​(xi​,ti​)∣2

The field loss anchors values, but the inverse formulation still needs equation tests. In this Burgers example from Part II, the residual is evaluated at the same coordinates as the observations. We do not silently add a second set of independent collocation points.

Square those residuals and average them to obtain L f. The observed values are used by the data term, while their coordinates are reused by the physics term. Our implementation follows that arrangement with two hundred fifty-six observations and two hundred fifty-six co-located equation tests.

中文讲稿

场值损失锚定了数值，但反问题还需要方程检验。在第二部分的 Burgers 算例中，残差就在观测坐标上计算。本实现不会悄悄增加另一组独立配点。

把这些残差平方后取平均，就得到 L f。观测值用于数据项，观测坐标则被物理项再次使用。本实现沿用这种安排，使用二百五十六个观测和同位置的二百五十六次方程检验。

10. How do we update both groups of unknowns?

min⁡θ,λ  Lu(θ)+Lf(θ,λ)\min_{\theta,\lambda}\;\mathcal L_u(\theta)+\mathcal L_f(\theta,\lambda)θ,λmin​Lu​(θ)+Lf​(θ,λ)

We now have two connected tests, so joint training minimises their sum over theta and lambda. The data term resists field predictions that disagree with measurements. The physical term resists derivative balances that disagree with the candidate equation.

Both terms update the network, while the physical term also updates the coefficients. This is a coupled nonlinear optimisation problem, even though the coefficients appear linearly when the field is fixed. Neither a low total loss nor a small data error guarantees correct coefficient estimates.

中文讲稿

现在已有两个相互关联的检验，所以联合训练对 theta 和 lambda 最小化两项损失之和。数据项抵制不符合测量的场预测。物理项抵制不符合候选方程的导数平衡。

两项都更新网络，而物理项还更新系数。即使固定场时系数在线性形式中出现，整体仍是耦合的非线性优化问题。总损失小或数据误差小，都不能保证系数估计正确。

11. How do we keep diffusion positive?

λ2=softplus⁡(β)=log⁡(1+eβ)\lambda_2=\operatorname{softplus}(\beta)=\log(1+e^\beta)λ2​=softplus(β)=log(1+eβ)

Joint training can change a coefficient in the wrong direction, so we encode a known physical restriction. Beta is an unconstrained scalar, and the softplus function maps it smoothly to a positive lambda two. The formula is the logarithm of one plus the exponential of beta.

The optimiser updates beta rather than clipping lambda two after each step. This positivity assumption is an explicit teaching implementation choice, not knowledge of the true value. Lambda one remains unconstrained, and the observations must determine the coefficient magnitudes.

中文讲稿

联合训练可能把系数改到不合理的方向，因此显式加入已知的物理限制。beta 是一个无约束标量，softplus 函数把它光滑地映射为正的 lambda 二。公式就是一加 beta 的指数，再取对数。

优化器更新 beta，而不是每步之后截断 lambda 二。正性假设是明确说明的教学实现选择，不是提前知道真值。lambda 一保持无约束，系数大小仍必须由观测决定。

14. Do coefficients move if the physics term is removed?

∂Lu∂λ=0\frac{\partial\mathcal L_u}{\partial\lambda}=0∂λ∂Lu​​=0

The code reveals a precise control experiment: remove only the physical term. In this architecture the data loss contains theta but never uses lambda. Its partial derivative with respect to the coefficient variables is therefore zero, and autograd reports unused gradients.

The recorded control leaves the coefficients at 0.5 and 0.1. A different method could fit derivatives and then regress coefficients, but that is not this particular data-only objective. This control tests the implemented gradient dependency, not every possible data-driven identification method.

中文讲稿

代码给出了一个很清楚的对照：只移除物理项。在当前结构中，数据损失包含 theta，却从未使用 lambda。因此对系数变量的偏导数为零，自动微分会报告这些梯度未被使用。

已记录的对照把系数留在 0.5 和 0.1。另一种方法可以先拟合导数再回归系数，但那不是这里这个纯数据目标。这个对照检验的是实际实现中的梯度依赖，而不是否定所有数据驱动识别方法。

These three curves come from three different coefficient pairs but the same odd starting profile and symmetric domain. The white sensor sits at the centre, x equal to zero, where all three fields remain zero.

A sensor only at that location therefore records the same history for different physical coefficients. No optimiser can distinguish those alternatives from those readings alone. The experiment needs observations with informative spatial and temporal variation, rather than simply more iterations.

中文讲稿

一次成功的合成拟合，不保证任何传感器布局都包含所需信息。这三条曲线来自三组不同系数，但使用相同的奇对称初值和对称区域。白色传感器位于中心，也就是 x 等于零，三种场在那里始终为零。

因此，只在这个位置测量，会为不同物理系数记录同样的历史。仅凭这些读数，任何优化器都无法区分这些候选情况。实验需要具有信息的时空变化，而不是单纯增加迭代次数。

22. What does this successful run not prove?

Observation design limits what can be inferred, and our experiment has further limits too. It uses synthetic scattered data, a smoother viscosity and a short time window. We did not reproduce the original low-viscosity benchmark, its reported precision or its other PDE examples.

The clean and noisy runs took 16.6 and 15.0 seconds on this computer, while the control took 4.4 seconds. Their computational costs are not identical, and one seed cannot support universal speed or accuracy rankings. Unknown model terms, biased sensors and uncertainty calibration remain separate research questions.

中文讲稿

除了观测设计的限制，本实验还有其他边界。它使用合成散布数据、较光滑的黏性和较短时间区间。没有复现原文小黏性基准、原文精度或其他方程算例。

本机干净数据和噪声运行分别用时 16.6 和 15.0 秒，对照用时 4.4 秒。三者计算代价不同，单个种子不能支持普遍的速度或精度排名。未知模型项、有偏传感器和不确定性校准仍是另外的研究问题。

23. Change only the noise level and predict the effect

The limits suggest a controlled next exercise rather than a larger simulation. Predict how the two coefficient estimates might change if noise grows from one percent to five percent while the observation coordinates and network remain fixed. Do not assume that their relative errors will grow by the same factor.

Run the downloadable script with noise set to zero point zero five and keep the same per-run time cap. It writes a new trace file and reports both coefficient and field errors. Compare results and budget flags before changing anything else, and do not overwrite the published experiment record.

24. Why should the next paper study gradient imbalance?

Part II has shown how coefficients enter training, but we have not explained why all loss terms should learn at compatible rates. Data mismatch and derivative-based physics can produce gradients of very different sizes. Adding more physical information can therefore also make optimisation harder.

The next planned paper studies gradient pathologies and balancing strategies using another small CPU example. We will inspect the competing gradients before testing a remedy. That next presentation is planned, while this one has implemented and independently checked the coefficient-identification example.
