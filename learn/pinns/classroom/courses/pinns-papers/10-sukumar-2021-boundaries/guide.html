1. Paper 10: distance functions and exact boundary construction

After weak residuals, we turn to satisfying boundary values by construction. First connect a heated disk to a smooth geometric factor. Then inspect automatic derivatives and twelve CPU controls, separating exact rim values from interior accuracy.

中文讲稿

弱残差之后，我们转向通过构造满足边界值。先把受热圆盘与光滑几何因子连接起来。再检查自动导数和十二次 CPU 对照，区分圆周精确值与内部精度。

2. After testing the equation, how do we satisfy its boundary?

The VPINN lecture changed how we tested the equation inside the domain. It used a simple factor to satisfy endpoint temperatures.

This paper asks how geometry can supply such a factor on a curved boundary. We will separate exact boundary values from the accuracy of the learned interior field.

中文讲稿

上篇 VPINN 改变了内部方程的检查方式。它用一个简单因子满足杆两端的温度。

本篇追问，几何形状怎样为曲线边界提供这样的因子。我们将区分边界值精确满足与内部温度场的学习精度。

3. Start with a heated disk and its rim temperature

Imagine a thin circular plate with unit conductivity and a uniform internal heat source. U is its normalised steady temperature, while x and y locate a point in the unit disk.

The rim is maintained at a prescribed temperature that increases slightly from left to right. Moving markers show outward heat flux; they illustrate a steady state, not a transient numerical simulation.

中文讲稿

想象一块单位导热系数的薄圆盘，内部有均匀热源。u 是归一化稳态温度，x 和 y 表示单位圆盘内的位置。

圆周温度已知，并从左向右略微升高。移动标记表示向外的热流；这是稳态示意，不是瞬态数值模拟。

4. One disk, four training constructions

−Δu=4,x2+y2<1,u∣∂Ω=g=0.2x-\Delta u=4,\quad x^2+y^2<1,\qquad u|_{\partial\Omega}=g=0.2x−Δu=4,x2+y2<1,u∣∂Ω​=g=0.2x

Delta is the Laplacian, the sum of the second derivatives with respect to x and y. The source is four, and the boundary symbol denotes the circle where x squared plus y squared equals one.

G is the prescribed rim temperature, here zero point two x. All four controls solve this same problem without using interior reference temperatures as training labels.

中文讲稿

Δ 是拉普拉斯算子，表示对 x 和 y 的二阶导数之和。热源为四，边界符号表示 x 平方加 y 平方等于一的圆周。

g 是给定圆周温度，这里取零点二 x。四个对照都求解同一问题，不把内部参考温度当作训练标签。

5. Soft constraints add boundary error to the loss

uθ=g+Nθ,L=LPDE+λLBCu_\theta=g+N_\theta,\qquad L=L_{\mathrm{PDE}}+\lambda L_{\mathrm{BC}}uθ​=g+Nθ​,L=LPDE​+λLBC​

N theta is the neural correction and theta denotes its trainable parameters. The first loss averages squared equation residuals at interior points.

The second loss checks the correction at sampled rim points, and lambda weights that penalty. We compare lambda equal to one and one hundred, without claiming either is universally optimal.

中文讲稿

N θ 是神经网络修正，θ 表示待训练参数。第一个损失在内部采样点平均方程残差的平方。

第二个损失在圆周采样点检查修正，λ 是惩罚权重。比较权重一与一百，但不把任何一个说成普遍最优。

6. Sampled boundary checks are not an exact boundary identity

The soft controls see sixty-four equally spaced boundary locations during training. A small loss there is evidence at those points, not an algebraic statement about every boundary location.

We evaluate another two hundred and fifty-seven angularly shifted rim points. This independent check can reveal boundary error between the training locations.

中文讲稿

软约束训练只使用六十四个等间距圆周位置。那里损失很小，说明这些点表现较好，不是对所有边界位置的代数保证。

另用角度错开的二百五十七个圆周点验证。这个独立检查能够暴露训练位置之间的边界误差。

7. Make the neural correction vanish on the rim

uθ=g⏟known lift+φNθ⏟zero on rimu_\theta=\underbrace{g}_{\text{known lift}}+\underbrace{\varphi N_\theta}_{\text{zero on rim}}uθ​=known liftg​​+zero on rimφNθ​​​

The known lift g extends the prescribed boundary values into the disk. Our simple choice zero point two x is defined everywhere and already matches the rim.

Phi is a geometric factor that equals zero on the entire rim. Multiplying the learned correction by phi makes its boundary contribution zero for any network weights.

中文讲稿

已知延拓 g 把给定边界值扩展到圆盘内部。这里简单选择处处有定义的零点二 x，它已经匹配圆周。

φ 是在整个圆周都为零的几何因子。把学习修正乘以 φ，就让任何网络权重下的边界修正都为零。

8. It is the boundary value that is exact

φ∣∂Ω=0⟹uθ∣∂Ω=g\varphi|_{\partial\Omega}=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad u_\theta|_{\partial\Omega}=gφ∣∂Ω​=0⟹uθ​∣∂Ω​=g

At a rim point, phi is zero, so the trial temperature equals g regardless of theta. This is an algebraic boundary identity, up to floating-point round-off in evaluation.

Inside the disk, phi is positive and the neural correction still has to solve the equation. Exact boundary enforcement alone does not give an exact interior solution.

中文讲稿

在圆周位置，φ 为零，因此无论 θ 如何，试探温度都等于 g。这是代数边界恒等式，数值计算时仍受浮点舍入影响。

在圆盘内部，φ 为正，神经修正仍需要满足方程。边界精确施加本身，并不带来精确的内部解。

9. A smooth approximate distance factor for a circle

φ=R2−∥x−xc∥22R,R=1,xc=0\varphi=\frac{R^2-\|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_c\|^2}{2R},\qquad R=1,\quad\mathbf{x}_c=\mathbf{0}φ=2RR2−∥x−xc​∥2​,R=1,xc​=0

The paper gives this circular approximate distance function, with radius R and centre vector x c. For our unit disk it becomes one minus x squared minus y squared, divided by two.

The gold diameter curve is smooth through the centre, positive inside and zero at both ends. It is not the exact distance everywhere; its useful features are boundary behaviour and differentiability.

中文讲稿

论文给出这个圆形近似距离函数，R 是半径，x c 向量是圆心。对单位圆盘，它就是一减 x 平方减 y 平方，再除以二。

金色直径曲线在圆心处光滑，内部为正，两端为零。它并非处处等于真实距离；有用之处是边界行为与可微性。

10. Normalisation concerns behaviour near the boundary

∂νφ=1 on the rim,ν=−n\partial_\nu\varphi=1\ \text{on the rim},\qquad \boldsymbol\nu=-\mathbf n∂ν​φ=1 on the rim,ν=−n

The vector n is the outward unit normal, so nu is the inward unit normal. On the unit circle, the gradient of phi points inward with unit magnitude.

Therefore the inward normal derivative is one on the boundary. This first-order normalisation does not say phi equals the true distance throughout the domain.

中文讲稿

向量 n 是外单位法向，ν 则是内单位法向。在单位圆周上，φ 的梯度指向内部，长度为一。

因此它沿内法向的导数在边界上为一。这个一阶归一化，并不是说 φ 在整个区域都等于真实距离。

11. Why is the true distance not automatically a better choice?

d=1−x2+y2,d(x,0)=1−∣x∣d=1-\sqrt{x^2+y^2},\qquad d(x,0)=1-|x|d=1−x2+y2​,d(x,0)=1−∣x∣

D is the exact distance to the rim from inside the unit disk. Its pink diameter profile has the same zero endpoints but a cusp at the centre.

The gold quadratic factor removes that cusp while preserving the required boundary values. Strong-form automatic differentiation cares about this interior smoothness, not just geometric distance accuracy.

中文讲稿

d 是单位圆盘内部到圆周的真实距离。粉色直径曲线同样在两端为零，但圆心处有尖点。

金色二次因子去掉这个尖点，同时保留需要的边界值。强形式自动微分关心这样的内部光滑性，而不仅是距离是否精确。

12. Second derivatives expose the centre singularity

Δφ=−2,Δd=−1/r (r>0),r=x2+y2\Delta\varphi=-2,\qquad \Delta d=-1/r\ (r>0),\quad r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}Δφ=−2,Δd=−1/r (r>0),r=x2+y2​

R is radial distance from the centre, and the plotted magnitudes use a logarithmic vertical scale. The smooth factor has constant Laplacian minus two, while the true distance has minus one over r away from zero.

At the centre the classical second derivative of the true distance is undefined. We keep this failure visible rather than silently replacing the distance by a smoothed or clamped formula.

中文讲稿

r 是到圆心的径向距离，图中幅值的纵轴采用对数尺度。光滑因子的拉普拉斯恒为负二，真实距离在非零位置则为负一除以 r。

圆心处真实距离的经典二阶导数无定义。我们保留这个失败，不悄悄把距离换成平滑或截断后的公式。

13. Hard boundaries do not remove derivatives from the equation

Δ(φN)=φΔN+2∇φ⋅∇N+NΔφ\Delta(\varphi N)=\varphi\Delta N+2\nabla\varphi\cdot\nabla N+N\Delta\varphiΔ(φN)=φΔN+2∇φ⋅∇N+NΔφ

The Laplacian of a product contains the network curvature, the cross-gradient term and the geometry curvature. Nabla is the spatial gradient, and the dot denotes its inner product.

We differentiate the complete trial function with automatic differentiation. Dropping geometry derivatives would train a different equation even if the boundary values looked correct.

中文讲稿

乘积的拉普拉斯包含网络曲率、梯度交叉项与几何曲率。∇ 表示空间梯度，点号表示内积。

我们对完整试探函数自动微分。即使边界值看起来正确，丢掉几何导数也会让训练求解另一条方程。

14. Temperature values and normal heat flux are different conditions

∂νuθ=∂νg+(∂νφ)Nθon the rim\partial_\nu u_\theta=\partial_\nu g+(\partial_\nu\varphi)N_\theta\quad\text{on the rim}∂ν​uθ​=∂ν​g+(∂ν​φ)Nθ​on the rim

On the rim, the normal derivative still depends on the network through the derivative of phi. Enforcing the temperature value does not automatically prescribe its normal heat flux.

The paper develops further constructions for other boundary types and complex geometries. Our small experiment implements only this smooth disk with a Dirichlet temperature boundary.

中文讲稿

圆周上，法向导数仍通过 φ 的导数依赖网络。精确施加温度值，不会自动指定法向热流。

论文还为其他边界类型和复杂几何发展了相应构造。我们的小实验只实现这个光滑圆盘的狄利克雷温度边界。

15. Pair initial weights across four constructions

Both soft controls use g plus the raw network and differ only in boundary penalty weight. They share all initial parameter weights with the two hard constructions for each seed.

The hard controls use either the smooth factor or the deliberately nonsmooth true distance. Equal parameter weights do not mean equal initial temperature functions, because multiplying factors changes the function.

中文讲稿

两个软约束都用 g 加原始网络，只改变边界惩罚权重。每个种子下，它们与两个硬构造共享全部初始参数权重。

硬构造分别用光滑因子或故意保留非光滑性的真实距离。相同参数权重不代表相同初始温度函数，因为乘法因子改变了函数。

16. A small experiment that runs on this CPU

We use a two-input tanh network with two hidden layers of sixteen units and three hundred and thirty-seven parameters. Adam performs two thousand updates for each of three seeds and four constructions.

There are one hundred and twenty-eight fixed interior points and sixty-four rim training points. The ninety-second per-run cap is recorded, and all twelve runs finish the full update budget.

中文讲稿

采用两个输入、两个十六单元隐藏层的双曲正切网络，共三百三十七个参数。三个种子、四种构造，每次 Adam 做两千步更新。

内部固定一百二十八点，圆周训练六十四点。记录每次九十秒上限，十二次运行都完成了完整更新预算。

17. Construct temperature before differentiating it

g = 0.2 * xy[:, :1]
phi = (1 - xy.square().sum(1, keepdim=True))/2
u = g + phi * net(xy)
r = -laplacian(u, xy) - 4
loss = r.square().mean()

The code first forms the known lift and smooth geometric factor, then multiplies the network correction. X y is the coordinate array, and square followed by sum computes squared radius.

The residual differentiates the complete temperature before subtracting the source four. No boundary penalty is needed for this hard construction, but the interior residual must still be optimised.

中文讲稿

代码先形成已知延拓与光滑几何因子，再乘网络修正。xy 是坐标数组，平方后求和得到半径平方。

残差对完整温度求导，再减热源四。这个硬构造不需要边界惩罚，但内部残差仍需要优化。

18. Use the reference solution only for validation

u∗(x,y)=0.2x+1−x2−y2,−Δu∗=4u^*(x,y)=0.2x+1-x^2-y^2,\qquad -\Delta u^*=4u∗(x,y)=0.2x+1−x2−y2,−Δu∗=4

The star marks an independent analytic reference, not another learned prediction. Its two second derivatives are each minus two, giving the required source four.

On the unit circle the quadratic correction vanishes and only zero point two x remains. Interior values from this expression are used for error measurement, never for training.

中文讲稿

星号表示独立解析参考，不是另一个学习预测。它的两个二阶导数各为负二，因此给出需要的热源四。

单位圆周上二次修正消失，只剩零点二 x。这个表达式的内部值用于测量误差，从不用于训练。

22. The centre must appear in validation

The fixed interior training design excludes the centre, with its closest radius equal to zero point zero six two five. A finite training residual therefore cannot certify that the true-distance ansatz is differentiable at the centre.

We explicitly evaluate that missing location and retain the non-finite derivative flag. Separate checks use shifted residual points, shifted rim points and the full-disk reference grid.

中文讲稿

固定内部训练设计避开圆心，最近半径为零点零六二五。因此有限训练残差不能证明真实距离构造在圆心可微。

我们专门检查这个缺失位置，并保留非有限导数标记。另外还用错开的残差点、圆周点与全圆盘参考网格分别检查。

23. Separate the paper idea from this CPU adaptation

This lesson retains the paper’s geometric trial-function idea and its circular approximate distance factor. It changes the source, known lift, network size and training budget for a small reproducible comparison.

We do not implement general geometry assembly, Neumann or Robin constructions, higher-order equations or the original large benchmarks. Training times include checkpoint audits and are not a time-to-accuracy speedup claim.

中文讲稿

本讲保留论文的几何试探函数思想与圆形近似距离因子。为小型可复现对照，改变了热源、已知延拓、网络规模与训练预算。

没有实现一般几何组装、诺依曼或罗宾构造、高阶方程或原始大型基准。计时包含检查点验证，不用来宣称达到同等精度的加速比。

25. Next: why do different losses learn at different rates?

We have separated boundary identities, geometric smoothness and learned interior accuracy. Boundary construction is one way to avoid a competition between residual and boundary penalties.

The next paper studies that competition through the neural tangent kernel. It asks why different loss components can converge at different rates even when they belong to the same physical problem.

中文讲稿

我们已经区分边界恒等式、几何光滑性与内部学习精度。边界构造提供一种避免残差惩罚与边界惩罚竞争的方式。

下一篇用神经切线核研究这种竞争。它追问，同一物理问题中的不同损失分量为什么会以不同速率收敛。

If the known lift changes from0.2x to ax, what remains exact and what still needs validation?

For any known constant a, the reference ax+1-x²-y² has source4 because the linear lift has zero Laplacian. On the unit circle the quadratic correction vanishes, so g+phi*N matches ax for every network parameter value. This boundary identity does not guarantee the learned interior field is accurate. The raw exact-distance factor still has a centre cusp; changing the lift cannot repair its missing second derivative.
