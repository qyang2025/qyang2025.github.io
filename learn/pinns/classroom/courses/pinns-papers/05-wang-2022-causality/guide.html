Protocol and limits

Undamped oscillator u_t=omega*v, v_t=-omega*u, omega6, t=[0,1], initial (1,0). Analytic reference (cos(omega*t),-sin(omega*t)); normalised energy u²+v²=1. Hard initial ansatz: (1,0)+t*N_theta(t). This replaces the paper’s soft initial-loss handling and is applied to every policy. No interior reference labels, energy penalty, validation-guided checkpoint choice or physical time integration. Tanh network 1–24–24–24–2, 1298 parameters, four CPU threads, float64. Sixty-four midpoint residual times, eight ordered bins of eight points, mean across both residual components. Adam rate0.001, seeds11/22/33 paired across policies.

Uniform weights; fixed epsilon1; annealed epsilon[.01,.1,1,10,100]. All weights use detached nonnegative exclusive prefix sums; w_first=1. Objective is mean(w*binLoss), not normalisation by sum(w). Annealed epsilon advances when minimum weight exceeds .99, checked every100 updates after at least200 at that epsilon. Unlike the full original algorithm, final-criterion attainment is recorded but does not terminate training early: all runs share a 6000-update ceiling and 90-second cap. Actual completed updates differ in capped runs; this is not an equal-update or controlled timing ranking. Initial state is hard, so residual gating has no separate initial penalty. No time-window marching or modified MLP is used.

Validation: 201 independent times with both states, maximum normalised-energy deviation, initial-state values and held-out residual at128 shifted times. All nine cases are retained. The simple baseline already works, so small error gaps do not prove a universal causal advantage. Chaotic/turbulent paper benchmarks and long-time accuracy are not reproduced.

1. Paper 05: Causal weighting and oscillator validation

The previous paper organised training across transport coefficients, so this one asks how residuals are prioritised within physical time. We will connect an animated spring oscillator to ordered residual bins and detached exponential gates. Finally, nine CPU runs will be checked against analytic trajectories, phase portraits and conserved energy.

中文讲稿

上一篇按输运系数组织训练，本篇追问物理时间内部的残差优先级。先把弹簧振子动画连接到有序残差格与脱离梯度的指数门控。最后对照解析轨迹、相图和守恒能量，检验九次 CPU 运行。

2. From coefficient curricula to temporal order

The previous paper changed the transport coefficient between training stages, but each stage still fitted the whole time interval together. This leaves a different question about learning order within one fixed physical problem. Should later-time residuals receive full emphasis before earlier dynamics are accurate.

The second box introduces temporal residual weighting. We keep the physical equation fixed and change the attention assigned to ordered time regions. This is a training-priority mechanism rather than statistical causal inference or another coefficient-identification method.

中文讲稿

上一讲在阶段之间改变输运系数，但每个阶段仍同时拟合整个时间区间。对于同一个固定物理问题，内部的学习顺序还值得追问。较早动力学尚未准确时，较晚残差是否应得到全部强调？

第二个框引入时间残差加权。保持物理方程固定，调整有序时间区域受到的关注。这是训练优先级机制，不是统计因果推断，也不是另一种系数识别方法。

3. Start by watching an undamped spring oscillator

To examine temporal order without a large simulation, picture a mass, labelled m, attached to a spring on a frictionless track. It starts displaced from equilibrium with zero velocity. The animation uses the exact oscillatory motion rather than a numerical time-stepper.

The gold mass moves back and forth while the spring stretches and compresses. There is no damping, so the amplitude should not decay. Later, checking that amplitude and the associated energy gives an independent way to judge the neural prediction.

中文讲稿

为了在无需大型模拟的条件下研究时间顺序，先看光滑轨道上标为 m 的弹簧质量块。它从偏离平衡的位置、以零速度开始运动。动画使用精确振动，不是数值时间推进。

金色质量块往返移动，弹簧伸长与压缩。模型没有阻尼，因此振幅不应衰减。后面检查振幅以及相应能量，可以独立判断网络预测。

4. How does the restoring force become an equation?

mutt+ku=0m u_{tt}+k u=0mutt​+ku=0

The moving mass gives each symbol a physical meaning. U is displacement from equilibrium, t is time, m is mass, and k is spring stiffness. The subscript t t means the second derivative in time, or acceleration.

The restoring force points against displacement and has magnitude k times u. Newton’s law therefore balances mass times acceleration with that restoring term. A dimensionless frequency, called omega, is the square root of stiffness divided by mass.

中文讲稿

运动质量块为每个符号提供物理含义。u 是相对平衡位置的位移，t 是时间，m 是质量，k 是弹簧刚度。下标 t t 表示时间二阶导数，也就是加速度。

回复力方向与位移相反，大小为 k 乘 u。因此牛顿定律把质量乘加速度与回复项相平衡。把刚度除以质量后开平方，就得到这里称为 omega 的无量纲频率。

5. Represent the motion with two first-order states

ut=ωv,vt=−ωuu_t=\omega v,\qquad v_t=-\omega uut​=ωv,vt​=−ωu

The second-order law can be rewritten using two states that are easy for a small network to output. We define v as velocity divided by omega. The displacement derivative is then omega times v.

Differentiating v gives minus omega times u, which is the second equation. We use omega equal to six over the time interval from zero to one. The initial state is displacement one and normalised velocity zero.

中文讲稿

二阶规律可以改写成两个状态，方便小网络同时输出。把 v 定义为速度除以 omega。因此位移导数就是 omega 乘 v。

对 v 求导得到负 omega 乘 u，这就是第二个方程。时间区间为零到一，omega 取六。初始状态是位移一、归一化速度零。

6. Build the initial values into the network expression

uθ=1+tN1(t),vθ=tN2(t)u_\theta=1+tN_1(t),\qquad v_\theta=tN_2(t)uθ​=1+tN1​(t),vθ​=tN2​(t)

The oscillator begins from a known state, so we enforce that state by construction in every compared method. N one and N two are the two unconstrained outputs of the same network with parameters theta. Multiplication by time makes their contribution vanish at time zero.

The first state therefore starts at one and the second at zero for any network parameters. This hard-initial-value ansatz is an explicit adaptation from the paper’s soft initial-loss treatment. It supplies the initial state but does not reveal the reference trajectory at later times.

中文讲稿

振子从已知状态出发，因此在所有比较方法中都用构造方式满足初始值。N 一与 N 二是同一个参数为 theta 的网络的两个自由输出。乘以时间，使它们在零时刻的贡献消失。

因此，无论网络参数是什么，第一个状态都从一开始，第二个都从零开始。这个硬初值表达式是相对论文软初值损失的明确改动。它提供初始状态，但不会泄露后续参考轨迹。

7. Use the analytic solution to check trajectory and phase

u∗(t)=cos⁡(ωt),v∗(t)=−sin⁡(ωt)u_*(t)=\cos(\omega t),\qquad v_*(t)=-\sin(\omega t)u∗​(t)=cos(ωt),v∗​(t)=−sin(ωt)

An exact reference makes this a small reproducible experiment rather than a simulation project. U star is cosine of omega time, and v star is negative sine of omega time. They satisfy the two first-order equations and the prescribed initial state.

We use these reference values only for independent checks on two hundred and one times. They do not appear as interior labels in the optimiser objective. A visually plausible displacement must also have the correct phase and velocity to match this two-state reference.

中文讲稿

精确参考使它成为小型可复现实验，而不是大型模拟项目。u 星是 omega 乘时间的余弦，v 星是相应正弦的负值。它们满足两个一阶方程和给定初始状态。

只在二百零一个时刻用参考值进行独立检查。它们不作为内部标签进入优化目标。位移图像看起来合理，还必须具有正确相位与速度，才符合这个双状态参考。

8. First place residuals into ordered temporal bins

ℓi=12mean⁡t∈Ii∥rθ(t)∥22\ell_i=\frac12\operatorname{mean}_{t\in I_i}\|r_\theta(t)\|_2^2ℓi​=21​meant∈Ii​​∥rθ​(t)∥22​

To prioritise time, the residual measurements must first have an explicit order. I subscript i denotes the i th temporal bin, and ell i is its average squared equation mismatch. R theta contains the two oscillator residual components.

Our sixty-four midpoint times are grouped into eight ordered bins with eight points each. The implementation also averages over the two state components. These bins organise the training objective; they are not physical time-integration steps or separate neural networks.

中文讲稿

要安排时间优先级，残差测量必须先有明确顺序。I 下标 i 表示第 i 个时间格，ell i 是其中的平均方程失配平方。r theta 含两个振子残差分量。

把六十四个中点时刻分成八个有序格，每格八点。实现还在两个状态分量上取平均。这些格用于组织训练目标，不是物理时间积分步，也不是各自独立的网络。

9. How does a uniform objective treat different times?

Luniform=18∑i=18ℓi\mathcal L_{\rm uniform}=\frac18\sum_{i=1}^{8}\ell_iLuniform​=81​i=1∑8​ℓi​

The baseline gives every temporal bin the same weight. It averages all eight bin losses at every optimiser update. This is a clear control for asking what changes when temporal priority is added.

Uniform weighting does not prevent the optimiser from learning the correct trajectory. In fact the measured baseline is already accurate on this simple oscillator. We will avoid turning the paper’s motivation into a claim that all uniform training must fail.

中文讲稿

基线给每个时间格相同权重。每次优化更新都平均八个时间格的损失。这个清楚的对照用于判断加入时间优先级究竟改变了什么。

均匀加权并不阻止优化器学出正确轨迹。事实上，本地实测基线在这个简单振子上已经准确。不能把论文的动机变成所有均匀训练都会失败的主张。

10. How does a later bin see earlier mismatch?

Si=∑j<iℓjS_i=\sum_{j<i}\ell_jSi​=j<i∑​ℓj​

Temporal priority needs a summary of what remains unresolved earlier in time. S i is the sum of bin losses with indices smaller than i. The current bin and every future bin are excluded from this prefix sum.

For the first bin the sum is empty and therefore zero. This ordering must follow physical time rather than an arbitrary shuffled list. Otherwise the same formula would prioritise a random sequence instead of earlier dynamics.

中文讲稿

时间优先级需要概括较早时刻还有多少失配。S i 是索引小于 i 的时间格损失之和。当前格以及所有未来格都不进入这个前缀和。

第一个格的求和为空，因此是零。这个顺序必须遵循物理时间，而不能使用任意打乱的列表。否则，相同公式安排的只是随机序列，而不是较早动力学。

11. Turn preceding mismatch into a soft gate

wi=exp⁡(−ϵSi)w_i=\exp(-\epsilon S_i)wi​=exp(−ϵSi​)

The prefix sum becomes a temporal weight through an exponential. Epsilon is a positive causality parameter controlling how steeply the gate closes. When earlier mismatch is large, a later bin receives less weight.

The first weight is exactly one because its prefix is zero. These are soft priorities, not a proof that every later residual is completely disabled. The weights also need not increase monotonically during training, because earlier losses can rise as the network changes.

中文讲稿

通过指数函数，把前缀和转换成时间权重。epsilon 是正的因果参数，控制门控关闭得多快。较早失配很大时，较晚时间格的权重就降低。

第一个权重恰好为一，因为它的前缀为零。这是软优先级，不代表所有较晚残差都会完全关闭。训练中权重也不必单调增大，因为网络变化时较早损失可能反而上升。

12. The weighted objective still averages over all bins

Lcausal=18∑i=18wiℓi\mathcal L_{\rm causal}=\frac18\sum_{i=1}^{8}w_i\ell_iLcausal​=81​i=1∑8​wi​ℓi​

The gates now change the residual contributions entering the optimiser. Each bin loss is multiplied by its temporal weight, and the result is averaged over the eight bins. The denominator remains eight, not the sum of the weights.

This distinction matters because renormalising by the weight sum defines a different objective. Our initial values are already exact by construction, so there is no separate initial penalty in any policy. The comparison isolates temporal residual weighting under that stated adaptation.

中文讲稿

门控现在改变进入优化器的残差贡献。每个格损失乘以自己的时间权重，再对八个格取平均。分母仍是八，不是权重之和。

这一区别很重要，因为除以权重总和会定义另一个目标。初始值已由构造精确满足，因此所有策略都没有单独初值惩罚。比较是在这个明确改动下，单独检验时间残差加权。

13. Why stop gradients through the gate calculation?

prefix = cumsum(losses.detach())
previous = cat([zero, prefix[:-1]])
weights = exp(-epsilon*previous)
loss = mean(weights*losses)

The weighted objective requires one important implementation detail. The controller uses detached bin losses when computing the prefix and weights. Gradients still flow through the current residual losses multiplied by those fixed weights.

The paper also stops gradients through the gate calculation. Differentiating through the exponential instead would introduce extra terms, defining a different update rule. The full script explicitly preserves the zero prefix of the first bin.

中文讲稿

加权目标还有一个关键实现细节。控制器计算前缀与权重时，使用已脱离计算图的格损失。梯度仍会通过被固定权重相乘的当前残差损失传播。

论文也会停止通过门控计算的梯度。若对指数本身继续求导，就会引入额外项，得到不同更新规则。完整脚本明确保留第一个格为零的前缀。

14. How do fixed and annealed epsilon differ?

ϵ:0.01→0.1→1→10→100\epsilon:0.01\to0.1\to1\to10\to100ϵ:0.01→0.1→1→10→100

The gate definition raises a choice about its steepness. We compare uniform weights, fixed epsilon equal to one, and an annealed sequence of epsilon values. Annealing starts gently and permits later increases in sensitivity to preceding mismatch.

The sequence is shown here, but the transitions are triggered by measured weights rather than evenly spaced training stages. This differs from the coefficient curriculum in the previous paper. The oscillator equation and its frequency remain unchanged throughout this process.

中文讲稿

门控定义带来了陡峭程度的选择。比较均匀权重、epsilon 固定为一，以及 epsilon 退火序列。退火从较温和的门控开始，再允许提高对先前失配的敏感度。

这里显示参数序列，但转移由测得权重触发，而不是等长训练阶段。这与上一篇系数课程不同。整个过程中振子方程与频率都保持不变。

15. What does a weight close to one tell us?

min⁡iwi>0.99\min_i w_i>0.99imin​wi​>0.99

The annealed controller needs an explicit transition criterion. When the minimum temporal weight exceeds zero point nine nine, every preceding cumulative training loss is small relative to the current epsilon. We check this every hundred updates after at least two hundred updates at that epsilon.

The criterion advances epsilon in this adaptation rather than ending the whole experiment. At the last epsilon we record when it is met but retain the common training ceiling. It is a training diagnostic, not a rigorous bound on trajectory error at unseen times.

中文讲稿

退火控制器需要明确的转移标准。当最小时间权重大于零点九九时，相对于当前 epsilon，所有先前累积训练损失都较小。每个 epsilon 至少更新两百次，再每百步检查一次。

在本改编中，这个标准用于推进 epsilon，而不是结束整个实验。到最后一个 epsilon 时记录满足时刻，但仍保留共同训练上限。它是训练诊断，不是对未见时刻轨迹误差的严格界。

16. How do nine runs stay small and transparent?

The three weight policies are now fully defined, so we test each with three paired seeds. The network has one thousand two hundred and ninety-eight parameters and sixty-four residual times. Every case uses double precision and four CPU threads.

All cases have a ceiling of six thousand Adam updates and a ninety-second training cap. Capped runs can finish different numbers of updates, especially under concurrent local workload. We publish actual steps and gate transitions, so these timings are not a controlled speed ranking.

中文讲稿

三种权重策略已完整定义，现在分别用三个配对种子检验。网络有一千二百九十八个参数，以及六十四个残差时刻。每种情形都使用双精度与四个 CPU 线程。

所有情形都设六千步 Adam 上限与九十秒训练上限。触发上限的运行可能完成不同更新次数，尤其在本机并发工作时。公开实际步数与门控转移，因此这些时间不能用于受控速度排名。

20. Energy supplies another independent physical check

The phase portrait suggests a scalar check that is easy to interpret physically. The normalised energy is the sum of squared displacement and scaled velocity. In this undamped model its reference value remains one at every time.

These curves show energy from the final learned states, not from the analytic animation. Energy is never added as a training penalty in these comparisons. A low weighted residual should therefore be judged together with trajectory error and energy drift.

22. How far can a small oscillator result take us?

This experiment makes detached temporal weighting, prefix ordering and epsilon transitions concrete. It does not reproduce the paper’s chaotic Lorenz, Kuramoto-Sivashinsky or turbulent-flow benchmarks. We also do not reproduce its modified MLP or time-window marching implementation.

Our hard initial state and fixed training ceiling are deliberate departures, with every cap disclosed. A minimum weight close to one is not an accuracy certificate, and a small oscillator error does not predict long-time chaotic performance. These limits keep the CPU demonstration useful and honest.

中文讲稿

这个实验把脱离梯度的时间加权、前缀顺序和 epsilon 转移变得具体。它没有复现论文中的混沌 Lorenz、Kuramoto-Sivashinsky 或湍流算例，也未复现改进 MLP 与逐时间窗实现。

硬初始状态与固定训练上限是有意改动，所有触发上限的情况都会披露。最小权重接近一不是精度证书，小振子误差也不能预测长期混沌表现。明确这些边界，才能让 CPU 演示既有用又诚实。

24. Connect the five papers into one research route

The series now connects a forward Burgers PINN, inverse coefficient identification, gradient statistics, coefficient curricula and temporal residual weighting. Each mechanism changes a different part of the learning problem. They should not be treated as interchangeable switches that always improve accuracy.

A useful next application starts with a clearly posed physical question, an independent reference and a local compute budget. Then choose whether the main difficulty is identification, competing objectives, problem continuation or temporal evolution. That is a stronger research habit than collecting advanced model names without controls.
