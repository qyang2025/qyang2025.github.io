1. Paper 03: Gradient pathologies and loss balancing

Part II identified physical coefficients, so this presentation asks how competing losses influence shared network updates. We will connect a loaded-string picture to Poisson constraints, then explain gradient statistics and weight controllers. Finally, twelve actual CPU runs will test the controllers and expose their limitations.

中文讲稿

第二篇识别了物理系数，本篇追问多个损失怎样影响共享网络的更新。先从受载细弦图像连接到 Poisson 约束，再解释梯度统计与权重控制器。最后用十二次真实 CPU 运行检验控制器，并展示它们的适用边界。

2. Why study training after writing the physics?

Part II connected observations and unknown coefficients through a common objective, but it did not guarantee that each objective would influence training appropriately. The same network must respond to data and derivative-based physics. Today we inspect the update signals rather than assuming that adding the losses is enough.

The next box is about optimisation, not another unknown material coefficient. A boundary mismatch and a physics mismatch can pull shared parameters at different scales. We will measure those gradients, test a published balancing rule, and retain a case where that rule performs poorly.

中文讲稿

第二篇用共同目标连接了观测和未知系数，但没有保证各个目标都能适当地影响训练。同一个网络必须同时响应数据和基于导数的物理约束。今天先检查更新信号，而不是认为把损失相加就足够。

第二个框讨论的是优化，不是另一个未知材料系数。边界失配与物理失配可能以不同尺度拉动共享参数。本课会测量梯度、检验论文的平衡规则，并保留它表现不好的案例。

3. Start with the equilibrium of a loaded string

Before differentiating a network, picture a taut string with prescribed end positions. A distributed transverse load bends it away from the straight line between its ends. The animation increases the load through a family of static equilibria, so it is not a transient simulation.

The gold curve is displacement u along position x, and the blue marks represent the applied load. With a normalised constant tension, load is proportional to negative curvature. This physical picture leads to a one-dimensional Poisson equation and makes the endpoint constraints meaningful.

中文讲稿

在对网络求导前，先想象一根端点位置已给定的张紧细弦。分布横向载荷使它偏离两端之间的直线。动画逐步增大载荷，展示一组静态平衡形状，不是瞬态模拟。

金线表示沿位置 x 的位移 u，蓝色标记表示外加载荷。张力归一化为常数后，载荷与负曲率成正比。这个物理图像引出一维 Poisson 方程，也解释了端点约束的意义。

4. Describe the physics through curvature

uxx(x)=g(x),x∈[0,1]u_{xx}(x)=g(x),\qquad x\in[0,1]uxx​(x)=g(x),x∈[0,1]

The loaded string motivates the equation, so let us name its variables before using them. Here x is the dimensionless position, u is transverse displacement, and g is the prescribed curvature source. The subscript x x means the second derivative with respect to position.

The second box is known input rather than a neural prediction. For this experiment we prescribe g of x as minus sixteen times sine of four x. The neural network must reproduce the corresponding curvature without being given interior solution labels.

中文讲稿

受载细弦给出了方程的背景，现在先说明变量。x 是无量纲位置，u 是横向位移，g 是给定的曲率源项。下标 x x 表示对位置求二阶导数。

第二个框是已知输入，而不是网络预测。本实验给定 g 等于负十六乘以四 x 的正弦。网络必须再现相应曲率，但不会获得内部解值标签。

5. Which solution do the endpoints select?

u(0)=0,u(1)=sin⁡4u(0)=0,\qquad u(1)=\sin 4u(0)=0,u(1)=sin4

The equation specifies curvature, but a shape also needs its end positions. The left endpoint has zero displacement. The right endpoint is prescribed as sine of four, which is negative and is deliberately not another zero boundary.

Together these conditions select the analytic solution sine of four x. We use that formula to verify predictions on a separate grid. During training, only its two endpoint values and the prescribed source are supplied.

中文讲稿

方程规定了曲率，但一条形状还需要端点位置。左端位移为零。右端指定为四的正弦，它是负数，特意不是另一个零边界。

这些条件共同选出解析解，即四 x 的正弦。我们用解析式在独立网格上检验预测。训练只获得两个端点值和给定源项。

6. Correct curvature can still give the wrong shape

u~=u+ax+b⟹u~xx=uxx\widetilde u=u+a x+b\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\widetilde u_{xx}=u_{xx}u=u+ax+b⟹uxx​=uxx​

The endpoints matter because the equation alone does not distinguish every possible shape. In this expression, a and b are arbitrary constants describing a straight-line offset. Its second derivative is zero.

The modified field therefore satisfies exactly the same source equation while generally missing the endpoints. This schematic is a concrete reason to inspect both the residual and the boundary error. A small physics residual alone cannot certify the boundary-value solution.

中文讲稿

端点很重要，因为仅靠方程无法区分所有形状。这里 a 和 b 是任意常数，用来描述一条附加直线。直线的二阶导数为零。

因此，修改后的场满足完全相同的源项方程，却通常不满足端点。这个示意图具体说明了为何必须同时检查残差和边界误差。物理残差小，不能单独证明边值问题已解对。

7. Connect both requirements to one objective

L=Lr+wLb\mathcal L=\mathcal L_r+w\mathcal L_bL=Lr​+wLb​

The two constraints now become losses for one network with parameters theta. L r is the mean squared curvature residual at interior test points. L b is the mean squared displacement mismatch at the two endpoints.

The scalar w controls the contribution of boundary fitting. It is a loss weight, not the Burgers coefficient lambda from Part II. The authors use lambda for loss weights, but we rename it w to avoid mixing an optimisation setting with a physical property.

中文讲稿

现在把两个约束写成同一个参数为 theta 的网络的损失。L r 是内部测试点上的曲率残差均方。L b 是两个端点的位移失配均方。

标量 w 控制边界拟合的贡献。它是损失权重，不是第二篇 Burgers 方程的物理系数 lambda。论文用 lambda 表示损失权重，这里改用 w，以免混淆优化设置和物理属性。

8. Training responds to gradients, not just loss values

gr=∇θLr,gb=∇θLbg_r=\nabla_\theta\mathcal L_r,\qquad g_b=\nabla_\theta\mathcal L_bgr​=∇θ​Lr​,gb​=∇θ​Lb​

The shared objective raises the question of how each requirement changes the parameters. The symbol nabla theta means differentiation with respect to the network parameters. We call the residual gradient g r and the boundary gradient g b.

Both are vectors with one coordinate for each trainable scalar. Equal loss values need not produce equally sized gradients. To diagnose an imbalance, we measure the two gradient vectors separately before combining them.

中文讲稿

共同目标引出一个问题：每项要求怎样改变参数？nabla theta 表示对网络参数求导。把残差梯度记为 g r，把边界梯度记为 g b。

两者都是向量，每个可训练标量对应一个坐标。损失值相等，并不意味着梯度大小相等。诊断失衡时，要先分别测量两个梯度，再把它们组合。

9. How do the two signals move the network?

θn+1=θn−η(gr+wgb)\theta_{n+1}=\theta_n-\eta\bigl(g_r+w g_b\bigr)θn+1​=θn​−η(gr​+wgb​)

The gradient vectors make the role of the loss weight explicit. Here n counts training updates, and eta is the learning rate. For simple gradient descent, the proposed parameter movement combines g r with w times g b.

Our experiment uses Adam, which further transforms the combined gradient with its moment estimates. The displayed equation explains why weighting changes the signal entering the optimiser. It is not a claim that every Adam step follows this exact straight-vector formula.

中文讲稿

梯度向量让损失权重的作用变得明确。n 表示训练更新次数，eta 是学习率。普通梯度下降把 g r 与 w 倍的 g b 组合起来，形成参数变化。

本实验使用 Adam，它还会用动量估计进一步变换合成梯度。显示的方程用于解释权重如何改变进入优化器的信号，不表示每一步 Adam 都严格遵循这个直向量公式。

10. How do we summarise many gradient coordinates?

Gr=max⁡j∣(gr)j∣,Gb=1P∑j=1P∣(gb)j∣G_r=\max_j |(g_r)_j|,\qquad G_b=\frac1P\sum_{j=1}^{P}|(g_b)_j|Gr​=jmax​∣(gr​)j​∣,Gb​=P1​j=1∑P​∣(gb​)j​∣

A single network has many gradient coordinates, so we need explicit statistics rather than an impression from a loss curve. The index j labels a trainable scalar, and P is the total number of such scalars. G r is the largest absolute residual-gradient coordinate.

G b is the mean absolute boundary-gradient coordinate. Our implementation includes all trainable scalars, placing zero in a coordinate when the residual does not depend on that parameter. These maximum and mean statistics differ from the Euclidean norms shown in later diagnostic plots.

中文讲稿

一个网络有许多梯度坐标，因此需要明确统计量，不能凭损失曲线的外观判断。j 标记一个可训练标量，P 是这些标量的总数。G r 是残差梯度坐标的最大绝对值。

G b 是边界梯度坐标绝对值的平均数。实现中包含全部可训练标量，若残差与某参数无关，相应坐标记零。这些最大值和平均值，与后面诊断图里的欧几里得范数不同。

11. Which ratio sets the proposed boundary weight?

w^=GrGb\widehat w=\frac{G_r}{G_b}w=Gb​Gr​​

The measured statistics lead directly to the paper’s ratio rule. W hat means a proposed new weight rather than the weight already applied. A small G b relative to G r suggests increasing the boundary contribution.

The denominator is the unweighted boundary-gradient statistic in the displayed rule. This does not force the two vector norms to be equal, and it does not fix opposing gradient directions. If G b becomes extremely small, the proposed weight can also become extremely large.

中文讲稿

测得的统计量直接引出论文的比值规则。w 帽表示建议的新权重，而不是已经使用的权重。当 G b 相对 G r 很小时，这个规则建议增大边界贡献。

显示规则的分母是未加权边界梯度的统计量。它既不强制两个向量范数相等，也不能解决梯度方向相反的问题。如果 G b 极小，建议权重也可能变得极大。

12. Why smooth the proposed weight?

w←(1−α)w+αw^w\leftarrow(1-\alpha)w+\alpha\widehat ww←(1−α)w+αw

A large or noisy proposal motivates smoothing before the next optimiser update. Alpha is a mixing fraction between zero and one. A larger alpha gives more influence to the new proposal, so the paper’s alpha of zero point nine is a fast update.

We recompute the proposal every ten Adam steps, a frequency discussed in the paper. Between those checks the weight is held fixed. The weight is a detached controller value, so back-propagation does not differentiate through the controller itself.

中文讲稿

建议值可能很大或有波动，因此在下次优化更新前先做平滑。alpha 是零到一之间的混合比例。alpha 越大，新建议的影响越大，所以论文的零点九属于较快更新。

本实验每十步 Adam 重新计算建议值，这也是论文讨论过的更新频率。两次检查之间保持权重不变。权重作为已脱离计算图的控制器数值，不对控制器本身反向传播。

13. First differentiate each objective separately

gr = gradients(Lr, params)
gb = gradients(Lb, params)
Gr = gr.abs().max()
Gb = gb.abs().mean()

The ratio has precise ingredients, so let us connect it to the executed code. We obtain the residual gradient and the boundary gradient from separate autograd calls. Retaining the graph allows the final combined objective to be differentiated afterwards.

The helper replaces an unused parameter gradient with a matching zero tensor before flattening. It then detaches the diagnostic vectors. This preserves the same parameter-coordinate convention in both statistics without treating an absent derivative as missing data.

中文讲稿

比值的组成已经明确，现在把它连接到实际代码。分别通过 autograd 调用获得残差和边界梯度。保留计算图，允许之后再对合成目标求导。

辅助函数先把未使用参数的梯度替换为同形零张量，再展平向量。随后把诊断向量脱离计算图。这样两个统计量采用同一套参数坐标，而不是把不存在的导数当成缺失数据。

14. Controller and network updates are separate steps

proposal = Gr / max(Gb, 1e-12)
w = (1-alpha)*w + alpha*proposal
loss = Lr + w*Lb
loss.backward(); optimizer.step()

After measuring the gradients, the controller proposes and smooths a new weight. The tiny denominator floor is a numerical safeguard, not a replacement for checking the trajectory. The displayed compact code omits the additional bounds that the full script records explicitly.

Only then do we form the combined objective and update the network. This order matters when interpreting the saved gradient statistics. A plotted weight must refer to the same update policy that produced the recorded prediction.

中文讲稿

测量梯度以后，控制器建议并平滑一个新权重。分母的小下限只是数值保护，不能替代轨迹检查。这里的紧凑代码省略了完整脚本中明确记录的额外限幅。

之后才形成合成目标并更新网络。解释保存的梯度统计时，这个顺序很重要。图中的权重必须来自实际产生该预测的同一更新策略。

15. What does the guarded variant change?

1≤w^clip≤100,α=0.11\leq\widehat w_{\rm clip}\leq100,\qquad\alpha=0.11≤wclip​≤100,α=0.1

The direct ratio can grow dramatically, so we also test an explicitly modified controller. This guarded version clips the proposal between one and one hundred. It mixes the proposal with alpha equal to zero point one.

Those are engineering choices for this experiment, not the paper’s unchanged Algorithm One. The ratio case also has a much wider numerical bound, from one millionth to one million. We publish every bound activation, because frequent clipping means the bounds materially define the method.

中文讲稿

直接比值可能剧烈增大，因此还检验一个明确修改过的控制器。限幅版本把建议值截在一到一百之间。它用零点一的 alpha 混入新建议。

这些是本实验的工程选择，不是论文未修改的算法一。比值版本也有宽得多的数值范围，即百万分之一到一百万。我们公开每次限幅，因为频繁触发意味着边界实质上定义了方法。

The controllers are defined, so now we compare them on identical small problems. Each seed creates the same initial network for all four policies. We use sixty-four fixed interior points, two endpoint labels, and four thousand Adam steps.

The four policies are unit weight, fixed weight one hundred, the ratio rule with wide safeguards, and the guarded variant. Three seeds give twelve runs, each below ninety training seconds on four CPU threads. The small network has one thousand two hundred and seventy-three parameters, and no interior solution labels.

18. What happens as the boundary gradient shrinks?

The poor field prediction makes it necessary to inspect the controller inputs. These bars show the Euclidean norms of the residual gradient, raw boundary gradient and weighted boundary gradient in the ratio run. Bar heights use a logarithmic scale, so each order of magnitude receives equal space.

They are diagnostics, not the maximum and mean used to compute the ratio. The weighted boundary signal can greatly exceed the raw one while the field remains inaccurate. Matching one statistic is therefore not equivalent to satisfying both physical requirements.

22. What does this counterexample actually support?

The measurements support a careful conclusion rather than a slogan. Gradient statistics are useful diagnostics, but their controller can be fragile when a denominator becomes tiny. A remedy must be checked against a simple baseline and independent physical errors.

This is not a refutation of the paper’s two-dimensional results, because our equation, network, data and arithmetic differ. We also did not implement its improved gated architecture or compute a Hessian spectrum. Those separate claims should not be inferred from these runs.

中文讲稿

测量支持的是谨慎结论，而不是口号。梯度统计是有用诊断，但当分母很小时，相应控制器可能脆弱。改进措施必须与简单基线和独立物理误差比较。

这不是对论文二维结果的否定，因为方程、网络、数据和数值精度都不同。我们也未实现论文的改进门控结构，或计算 Hessian 谱。因此不能从这些运行推断那些不同主张。

24. Why study gradually increasing difficulty next?

We have now learned to inspect optimisation signals without assuming a particular controller must win. Another difficulty remains: a network may fail when the physical problem itself becomes harder, even if the objective looks reasonable. That motivates a separate look at failure modes and curriculum training.
