1. Paper15: Hidden fluid mechanics from a visible tracer

First connect visible tracer motion with the hidden transport equation. Then replay matched CPU controls with noise and missing gradient information. Finally distinguish concentration fit from velocity identification and bridge to inverse design.

中文讲稿

先把可见示踪剂运动与隐藏输运方程连接。再回放带噪声与缺失梯度信息的 CPU 对照。最后区分浓度拟合与速度识别，并衔接逆设计。

2. After uncertainty, ask what the observations can identify

The Bayesian lesson described uncertainty conditional on a model and its measurements. But what if the observations cannot distinguish two different physical explanations at all?

This paper uses visible dye or smoke to infer hidden flow. We will test that idea in a small two-dimensional transport problem, including a case where one velocity component is genuinely invisible.

中文讲稿

上篇用后验描述给定模型与观测下的不确定性。可是，如果观测根本分不清两种不同的物理解释呢？

本篇从看得见的染料或烟雾推断隐藏流动。我们用一个小型二维输运问题检验这个想法，也保留一个确实看不见某个速度分量的例子。

The reference flow moves rightwards and upwards while molecular diffusion smooths the pattern. The arrow shows the known reference for teaching; its velocity is never supplied as a training label.

先看这个彩色图案在周期区域里移动。颜色表示零到一之间的归一化被动浓度 c，不是温度，也不是速度大小。

4. Separate the measured field from the hidden quantities

X and y are spatial coordinates, and t is time. Our sensors give concentration values at selected positions and times, with no direct velocity observations.

U and v denote the two flow-velocity components. The original method also reconstructs pressure in more general flows, whereas this CPU example fixes constant pressure and learns only two constant velocity coefficients.

中文讲稿

x 与 y 是空间坐标，t 是时间。传感器只给选定位置、时刻的浓度值，没有直接速度观测。

u 与 v 表示两个流速分量。原方法还在更一般的流动中重建压力，而这个 CPU 示例固定常压力，只学习两个常数速度系数。

5. Advection and diffusion jointly change the concentration image

ct+ucx+vcy=κ(cxx+cyy)c_t+u c_x+v c_y=\kappa(c_{xx}+c_{yy})ct​+ucx​+vcy​=κ(cxx​+cyy​)

The left-hand side combines time change with transport along the concentration gradients. Subscripts mark derivatives, and kappa is the known molecular diffusivity, zero point zero two in our chosen units.

The right-hand side describes diffusion through the spatial Laplacian. The flow moves the pattern, while diffusion smooths it; confusing those effects can bias a velocity estimate.

中文讲稿

左边把时间变化与沿浓度梯度的输运组合起来。下标表示求导，κ 是已知分子扩散率，在本实验单位中取零点零二。

右边用空间拉普拉斯描述扩散。流动搬运图案，扩散使它平滑，混淆这两种作用会影响速度估计。

6. The original method also constrains the hidden fluid field

ut+(u ⁣⋅ ⁣∇)u=−∇p+νΔu,∇ ⁣⋅ ⁣u=0\boldsymbol u_t+(\boldsymbol u\!\cdot\!\nabla)\boldsymbol u=-\nabla p+\nu\Delta\boldsymbol u,\quad\nabla\!\cdot\!\boldsymbol u=0ut​+(u⋅∇)u=−∇p+νΔu,∇⋅u=0

Bold u denotes the velocity vector, p is pressure and nu is kinematic viscosity. The original framework couples tracer transport with momentum conservation rather than treating arbitrary image motion as a fluid solution.

The divergence constraint expresses incompressibility. In our constant-flow family all spatial and temporal velocity derivatives vanish, so these fluid equations hold by construction rather than being learned through an additional loss.

中文讲稿

粗体 u 表示速度向量，p 是压力，ν 是运动黏度。原框架把示踪剂输运与动量守恒耦合，而不是把任意图像运动都当作流体解。

散度约束表达不可压缩性。在本实验的常流速族中，速度的空间与时间导数均为零，所以这些流体方程通过构造满足，而不是通过额外损失学习。

7. Restrict the model family so the CPU can test it completely

u=(a,b),p=0,(a∗,b∗)=(0.8,0.35)\boldsymbol u=(a,b),\quad p=0,\quad (a^*,b^*)=(0.8,0.35)u=(a,b),p=0,(a∗,b∗)=(0.8,0.35)

A and b are the trainable constant velocity coefficients, with the star marking the reference values. Pressure zero is a chosen constant gauge, not an inferred pressure field.

This reduction keeps the hidden-transport mechanism while avoiding a large fluid simulation. It cannot demonstrate recovery of vortices, wall shear or general pressure distributions.

中文讲稿

a 与 b 是待训练的常数速度系数，星号表示参考值。压力取零是选定的常数基准，不是推断得到的压力场。

这个简化保留隐藏输运机制，也避免大型流体模拟。它不能展示涡流、壁面剪切或一般压力分布的恢复。

8. Generate concentration observations from a checkable pattern

We sample forty-eight concentration positions at each of three times: zero, one half and one. The smooth periodic reference is transported analytically, so generating these observations needs no expensive numerical flow solve.

Two hundred and fifty-six additional points test the transport equation during training. A separate set of one thousand points evaluates field and residual errors afterwards.

中文讲稿

在零、二分之一和一这三个时刻，各采样四十八个浓度位置。平滑周期参考通过解析式输运，因此生成这些观测不需要昂贵的数值流场求解。

另外二百五十六个点在训练中检查输运方程。训练之后，再用独立的一千个点评价场与残差误差。

9. Respect periodicity and the concentration range

cθ=0.5+0.5tanh⁡Nθ(sin⁡x,cos⁡x,sin⁡y,cos⁡y,2t−1)c_\theta=0.5+0.5\tanh N_\theta(\sin x,\cos x,\sin y,\cos y,2t-1)cθ​=0.5+0.5tanhNθ​(sinx,cosx,siny,cosy,2t−1)

Theta collects the concentration-network weights and biases. Periodic sine-cosine inputs make opposite spatial edges agree without an extra boundary penalty.

The final tanh mapping bounds the predicted concentration between zero and one. Those useful constructions do not reveal the hidden velocity automatically; the observations and transport residual must still constrain it.

中文讲稿

θ 表示浓度网络的权重与偏置。周期正弦余弦输入让相对空间边缘自动一致，不需要额外边界惩罚。

最后的双曲正切映射把预测浓度限制在零到一之间。这些有用构造并不会自动揭示隐藏速度，仍要靠观测与输运残差提供约束。

10. Let the velocity parameters participate in the transport residual

c = model(points)
cx, cy, ct = spatial_time_grad(c, points)
r = ct + model.a*cx + model.b*cy
r = r - 0.02*(cxx + cyy)
loss = 20*mse(c_data, labels) + mean(r*r)

The network supplies concentration and its space-time derivatives. A and b are ordinary trainable parameters inside the residual, so the physics term can send gradients to them.

The displayed derivative helper abbreviates explicit automatic differentiation in the downloaded code. No direct velocity label appears in this loss.

中文讲稿

网络提供浓度及其时空导数。a 与 b 是残差里的普通待训练参数，因此物理项可以把梯度传给它们。

显示的导数辅助函数，是下载代码中显式自动求导的缩写。这个损失里没有直接速度标签。

The remaining fifteen hundred updates include physics, except in the explicitly labelled data-only control. We use sixty-four-bit arithmetic and two CPU threads; the browser will only replay saved results.

15. Noise changes inference and calls for repeated starts

Adding concentration noise moves the recovered velocities and increases concentration error. We retain all three starting identifiers, so the displayed conclusion does not depend on selecting one favourable run.

Our concentration error is normalised by the true fluctuation around one half. Using the entire positive field in the denominator would let the constant background hide errors in the moving pattern.

中文讲稿

加入浓度噪声会改变恢复速度，并增大浓度误差。保留全部三个随机起点，因此结论不依赖挑选某一次好结果。

浓度误差用真实场相对二分之一背景的波动归一化。如果用整个正值场作分母，常数背景就会掩盖运动图案中的误差。

16. Motion along a stripe can leave its image unchanged

cy=0⟹vcy=0c_y=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v c_y=0cy​=0⟹vcy​=0

This concentration pattern varies with x but not y. Imagine sliding a vertical stripe upwards: its concentration image does not change, so the observation cannot report that motion.

The same fact appears in the equation: the y derivative is zero, removing v from the transport residual. A very accurate stripe fit can therefore coexist with a completely undetermined vertical velocity.

中文讲稿

这个浓度图案随 x 变化，却不随 y 变化。想象把一条竖直条纹向上移动：浓度图像不变，观测因此无法报告这个运动。

同一事实也出现在方程中：y 导数为零，使 v 从输运残差中消失。因此，极准确的条纹拟合也可以同时具有完全无法确定的竖直速度。

17. One concentration gradient constrains one velocity projection

[cx  cy][uv]=−ct+κΔc[c_x\;c_y]\begin{bmatrix}u\\v\end{bmatrix}=-c_t+\kappa\Delta c[cx​cy​][uv​]=−ct​+κΔc

At one space-time point, the transport equation provides one scalar constraint on two velocity components. It measures the projection of velocity onto the concentration gradient.

Motion tangent to the local concentration contour is invisible to that individual constraint. Multiple positions and times with differently oriented gradients can provide complementary information in our constant-velocity family.

中文讲稿

在单个时空位置，输运方程只为两个速度分量提供一个标量约束。它测量的是速度在浓度梯度方向上的投影。

沿着局部等浓度线的运动，对这一条约束不可见。在常速度模型族中，不同位置和时刻、方向不同的梯度，能够提供互补信息。

18. Complementary gradient directions identify two constant velocities

G=1N∑j=1N∇cj∇cjTG=\frac1N\sum_{j=1}^{N}\nabla c_j\nabla c_j^{\mathsf T}G=N1​j=1∑N​∇cj​∇cjT​

G is the two-by-two Gram matrix of concentration gradients across our diagnostic points, and N counts those points. Two positive eigenvalues indicate information in two independent directions for this constant-coefficient problem.

The stripe matrix has a zero eigenvalue because every y gradient vanishes. This is a diagnostic inside our reduced family, not a theorem that any general flow is uniquely recoverable from a single tracer.

中文讲稿

G 是诊断点上浓度梯度构成的二乘二 Gram 矩阵，N 是点数。在这个常系数问题中，两个正特征值表示两个独立方向都包含信息。

条纹矩阵有一个零特征值，因为所有 y 梯度都为零。这是简化模型族内的诊断，不是说任意一般流场都能由单个示踪剂唯一恢复。

19. Check concentration, velocity and equation residuals separately

We evaluate the learned concentration on independent space-time points and compare velocity against the synthetic reference used only for validation. We also check the transport residual at one thousand held-out points.

These tests answer different questions. A small residual can accompany an unidentifiable velocity, and a good concentration fit does not prove that hidden physical quantities are correct.

中文讲稿

在独立时空点上评估学习浓度，并把速度与只用于验证的合成参考比较。另在一千个未训练位置检查输运残差。

这些检查回答不同问题。小残差可能伴随无法辨识的速度，良好浓度拟合也不能证明隐藏物理量正确。

20. Retain hidden transport without claiming full flow reconstruction

Our constant velocities and constant pressure satisfy the unforced fluid equations by construction. The experiment learns concentration and two coefficients; it does not learn a spatial velocity field, pressure gradients or vascular flow.

The original paper treats substantially richer flows. Our compact example is designed to expose the inverse mechanism and its missing-information failure case within a reproducible CPU budget.

中文讲稿

常速度与常压力通过构造满足无外力流体方程。实验学习浓度和两个系数，没有学习空间速度场、压力梯度或血管流动。

原论文处理丰富得多的流动。这个小示例旨在可复现 CPU 预算内，揭示逆问题机制与信息缺失的失败情形。

21. If concentration is uniform, can it reveal the flow?

∇c=0⟹u ⁣⋅ ⁣∇c=0\nabla c=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boldsymbol u\!\cdot\!\nabla c=0∇c=0⟹u⋅∇c=0

Pause and predict what happens if the tracer concentration is constant everywhere. Both spatial gradients vanish, so neither velocity component appears in its transport observation.

Better optimisation cannot manufacture the missing information. Design informative tracer patterns or obtain independent measurements, then test identifiability before trusting a hidden-flow estimate.

中文讲稿

停一下，预测当浓度处处相同时会怎样。两个空间梯度都为零，两个速度分量都不再出现在输运观测中。

更好的优化无法制造缺失信息。应设计有信息的示踪图案，或获取独立测量，再先检查可辨识性，最后相信隐藏流动估计。

22. Finally, move from identifying physics to designing it

We have asked what a physical system is doing and what observations can reveal. Inverse design changes the question: which admissible material arrangement should we choose to achieve a desired performance?

The final paper introduces hard constraints and augmented Lagrangians. A small thermal design experiment will show why an attractive neural objective must still be verified by an independent physical solve.

我们已经问过物理系统在做什么，以及观测能揭示什么。逆设计改变了问题：应该选择哪种允许的材料安排，才能达到期望性能？

Teaching checks and reference answers

Ask for a prediction before running the exercise. Analytic expectations do not guarantee an optimiser outcome.

Can uniform concentration identify either constant velocity?

No. With both concentration gradients zero, the advection term vanishes for every velocity. The transport observation cannot distinguish any two constant velocities. A small concentration error or residual therefore cannot certify velocity accuracy. Add spatially informative tracer structure or independent measurements; repeated optimisation cannot supply missing information.

均匀浓度能确定任一常速度吗？

不能。两个浓度梯度都为零时，对所有速度，平流项均为零。输运观测无法区分任意两个常速度，因此浓度误差或残差很小也不能证明速度准确。需要有空间结构的示踪剂或独立测量；重复优化无法提供缺失的信息。
