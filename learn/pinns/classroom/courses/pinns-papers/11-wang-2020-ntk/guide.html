1. Paper 11: NTKs and unequal training rates

Boundary construction avoids one loss competition, so we now study that competition when penalties remain. First connect a heated rod to parameter sensitivities and the empirical kernel. Then derive trace weighting and inspect nine CPU runs, checking where constant-kernel intuition breaks down.

中文讲稿

边界构造避免了一种损失竞争，本篇研究保留惩罚时的这种竞争。先从受热杆连接到参数敏感度与经验核。再推导迹加权并检查九次 CPU 运行，观察常核直觉在哪些地方失效。

2. After boundary construction, revisit training rates

The previous paper made boundary values exact through a geometric trial function. When we instead train boundary penalties, different loss components can still improve at different rates.

This paper studies that interaction using the neural tangent kernel, abbreviated N T K. We will connect parameter sensitivities, kernel spectra and actual CPU training without assuming a small network behaves like an infinitely wide one.

中文讲稿

上篇通过几何试探函数精确满足边界值。如果改用边界惩罚训练，不同损失分量仍可能以不同速率改善。

本篇用神经切线核，也就是 NTK，研究这种相互作用。我们将连接参数敏感度、核谱与实际 CPU 训练，不把小网络当作无限宽网络。

4. Fix the physical problem and reference solution

−uxx=π2sin⁡(πx),u(0)=u(1)=0,u∗=sin⁡(πx)-u_{xx}=\pi^2\sin(\pi x),\quad u(0)=u(1)=0,\qquad u^*=\sin(\pi x)−uxx​=π2sin(πx),u(0)=u(1)=0,u∗=sin(πx)

The heat source is pi squared times sine of pi x, and the negative second derivative balances that source. The star marks the analytic reference, which satisfies both endpoint values.

All controls learn the temperature directly with soft endpoint penalties. Reference interior values measure error after training and never enter the training loss.

热源为圆周率平方乘正弦圆周率 x，温度二阶导数的负值与它平衡。星号表示满足两端温度的解析参考。

所有对照都直接学习温度，并用软惩罚约束端点。内部参考值只在训练之外测量误差，从不进入训练损失。

5. Two objectives change the same parameter vector

L=λb2mean⁡(ub2)+λr2mean⁡(r2),r=−uxx−fL=\frac{\lambda_b}{2}\operatorname{mean}(u_b^2)+\frac{\lambda_r}{2}\operatorname{mean}(r^2),\quad r=-u_{xx}-fL=2λb​​mean(ub2​)+2λr​​mean(r2),r=−uxx​−f

The boundary subscript b identifies the two endpoint temperatures, and lambda b weights their squared error. Theta denotes every trainable weight and bias of the temperature network.

The residual r measures the interior equation mismatch, with f denoting the known source. Lambda r weights this second objective, which changes the same theta rather than a separate network.

中文讲稿

下标 b 表示两个端点温度，λ b 加权它们的平方误差。θ 表示温度网络全部待训练权重与偏置。

残差 r 测量内部方程不匹配，f 是已知热源。λ r 加权这个第二目标；它改变同一个 θ，而不是另一个网络。

6. Put mean normalisation into the error vector

e=[ub/Nbr/Nr],Nb=2,Nr=24e=\begin{bmatrix}u_b/\sqrt{N_b}\\r/\sqrt{N_r}\end{bmatrix},\qquad N_b=2,\quad N_r=24e=[ub​/Nb​​r/Nr​​​],Nb​=2,Nr​=24

E stacks the two boundary errors after division by the square root of their count. This scaling makes their squared vector norm equal the mean squared boundary error.

The twenty-four interior residuals are scaled by the square root of twenty-four. Recording this normalisation is essential because kernel traces and numerical loss weights depend on it.

中文讲稿

e 先堆叠两个除以数量平方根的边界误差。这个缩放让对应向量范数的平方等于边界误差平方均值。

二十四个内部残差则除以根号二十四。必须记录这个归一化，因为核的迹与数值损失权重都依赖它。

7. The Jacobian describes parameter sensitivity

Jij=∂ei∂θjJ_{ij}=\frac{\partial e_i}{\partial\theta_j}Jij​=∂θj​∂ei​​

J is the Jacobian of the normalised error vector with respect to network parameters. Row i collects how error i changes under small changes of every parameter theta j.

Comparing two rows asks whether those errors respond along similar parameter directions. Their inner product creates the coupling measured by the kernel.

中文讲稿

J 是归一化误差向量对网络参数的雅可比矩阵。第 i 行汇集误差 i 对每个参数 θ j 的微小变化响应。

比较两行，就是问这两个误差是否沿相近参数方向响应。两行的内积形成核所测量的耦合。

8. Draw sensitivity inner products as a matrix

K=JJ⊤,Kij=⟨Ji,Jj⟩K=JJ^\top,\qquad K_{ij}=\langle J_i,J_j\rangleK=JJ⊤,Kij​=⟨Ji​,Jj​⟩

K is the empirical neural tangent kernel at the current finite-network parameters. Each displayed cell is the inner product between two rows of J, using our normalised boundary and residual outputs.

Gold cells are positive and blue cells negative, with brightness indicating magnitude. Individual negative entries do not contradict positive semidefiniteness of the complete Gram matrix.

中文讲稿

K 是当前有限网络参数下的经验神经切线核。每个格子都是 J 的两行内积，使用我们归一化后的边界与残差输出。

金色格子为正，蓝色为负，亮度表示幅值。某些元素为负，并不违背整个 Gram 矩阵的半正定性。

9. Both diagonal and cross blocks matter

K=[KbbKbrKrbKrr]K=\begin{bmatrix}K_{bb}&K_{br}\\K_{rb}&K_{rr}\end{bmatrix}K=[Kbb​Krb​​Kbr​Krr​​]

K b b couples boundary errors to boundary errors, while K r r couples residuals to residuals. Their traces summarise squared parameter sensitivities within each group.

The off-diagonal blocks couple boundary and residual errors through shared parameters. Separate trace statistics are useful summaries, but they do not remove this interaction or predict every individual mode.

中文讲稿

K b b 耦合边界误差，K r r 耦合内部残差。它们的迹概括每一组参数敏感度平方的总和。

非对角块通过共享参数，把边界误差和残差连接起来。分别统计迹很有用，但不会取消这种相互作用，也不能预测每个独立模式。

10. Gradient flow turns parameter updates into error dynamics

θ˙=−J⊤e⟹e˙=−Ke\dot\theta=-J^\top e\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dot e=-K eθ˙=−J⊤e⟹e˙=−Ke

For unit weights and continuous gradient flow, the parameter velocity is minus J transpose times e. The dot denotes differentiation with respect to optimisation time, not physical heat-transfer time.

Applying the chain rule gives error velocity equal to minus K times e. This identity uses the current kernel, which may change as a finite network trains.

中文讲稿

对单位权重的连续梯度流，参数变化率是负的 J 转置乘 e。点号表示对优化时间求导，不是热传导的物理时间。

应用链式法则，误差变化率等于负的 K 乘 e。这里使用当前核；有限网络训练时，这个核可能发生变化。

11. What do large and small eigenvalues mean?

Kqi=μiqi,ci(τ)=ci(0)e−μiτKq_i=\mu_i q_i,\qquad c_i(\tau)=c_i(0)e^{-\mu_i\tau}Kqi​=μi​qi​,ci​(τ)=ci​(0)e−μi​τ

Q i is a unit eigenvector, mu i its kernel eigenvalue, and c i the error coefficient along that direction. If the kernel stays fixed, that coefficient decays exponentially in optimisation time tau.

Large positive eigenvalues give faster decay, while zero eigenvalues leave corresponding errors unchanged in this frozen model. The blue curve shows the initial empirical spectrum, with a display floor for very small values.

中文讲稿

q i 是单位特征向量，μ i 是对应核特征值，c i 是误差沿该方向的系数。如果核保持不变，这个系数随优化时间 τ 指数衰减。

较大的正特征值带来更快衰减，零特征值则让相应误差在冻结模型中保持不变。蓝线展示初始经验谱，极小值采用显示下限。

12. Trace summarises sensitivity, not the whole convergence story

tr⁡K=∥J∥F2=∑iμi\operatorname{tr}K=\|J\|_F^2=\sum\nolimits_i\mu_itrK=∥J∥F2​=∑i​μi​

The trace is the sum of diagonal entries and equals the squared Frobenius norm of J. It is also the sum of kernel eigenvalues, so it can be computed without a full eigendecomposition.

Two kernels with equal trace can still have very different small eigenvalues and error projections. Equalising trace statistics therefore does not equalise every convergence mode.

中文讲稿

迹是对角元素之和，等于 J 的 Frobenius 范数平方。它也等于核特征值之和，因此不必完整特征分解就能计算。

两个核即使迹相同，较小特征值与误差投影仍可能不同。因此平衡迹统计，并不等于平衡每个收敛模式。

13. Use traces to weight the loss components

λb=Tb+TrTb,λr=Tb+TrTr\lambda_b=\frac{T_b+T_r}{T_b},\qquad\lambda_r=\frac{T_b+T_r}{T_r}λb​=Tb​Tb​+Tr​​,λr​=Tr​Tb​+Tr​​

T b and T r denote the traces of our mean-normalised boundary and residual blocks. These ratios adapt the paper’s trace rule to the exact loss convention used in this experiment.

Each weighted block then has the same trace at the time weights are computed. We compare keeping these initial weights fixed with refreshing them every four hundred updates.

中文讲稿

T b 和 T r 表示我们按均值归一化后的边界块与残差块的迹。这些比值把论文的迹规则应用到本实验明确的损失约定。

在计算权重的时刻，两组加权块就有相同的迹。我们比较固定初始权重与每四百步刷新权重。

14. Inspect weighted dynamics in symmetric coordinates

z=D1/2e,z˙=−Az,A=D1/2KD1/2z=D^{1/2}e,\qquad\dot z=-Az,\quad A=D^{1/2}KD^{1/2}z=D1/2e,z˙=−Az,A=D1/2KD1/2

D is diagonal with positive boundary and residual weights, and z is the correspondingly scaled error. For weights held constant, this coordinate change gives the symmetric matrix A.

A is positive semidefinite because it is still a Jacobian Gram matrix after row scaling. For a frozen A, explicit gradient descent needs a sufficiently small step; positive weights alone do not guarantee nonlinear training stability.

中文讲稿

D 是由正的边界与残差权重组成的对角矩阵，z 是相应缩放后的误差。权重固定时，这个坐标变化给出对称矩阵 A。

A 仍是雅可比行缩放后的 Gram 矩阵，因此半正定。冻结 A 后，显式梯度下降仍需要足够小的步长；正权重本身并不保证非线性训练稳定。

The adaptive policy refreshes detached trace weights every four hundred updates. We run seeds eleven, twenty-two and thirty-three, keeping final checkpoints rather than selecting the best validation result.

自适应策略每四百步刷新不参与反向传播的迹权重。运行种子十一、二十二、三十三，保留最终检查点，不挑选验证集上最好的结果。

16. The kernel comes from output derivatives with respect to parameters

Jb = param_jacobian(ub) / sqrt(Nb)
Jr = param_jacobian(r) / sqrt(Nr)
Tb, Tr = Jb.square().sum(), Jr.square().sum()
wb, wr = trace_weights(Tb, Tr)  # detached
loss = (wb*mean(ub**2) + wr*mean(r**2))/2

The code differentiates boundary outputs and equation residuals with respect to all trainable parameters. Dividing rows by square roots of sample counts matches the mean losses exactly.

Squared Jacobian entries provide the two traces, and detached ratios become loss weights. The small teaching implementation saves complete kernels for inspection, although weights themselves need only the traces.

中文讲稿

代码把边界输出和方程残差对全部待训练参数求导。行除以样本数平方根，与均值损失严格对应。

雅可比元素平方的总和给出两个迹，分离梯度后的比值成为损失权重。小型教学实现还保存完整核用于观察，但计算权重本身只需要迹。

23. What does this lesson establish?

We independently check the Gram construction, loss normalisation and saved numerical errors. The experiment demonstrates a finite-network comparison with disclosed sampling, step size and all three seeds.

It does not verify the paper’s infinite-width theorem, resolve spectral bias or establish equal convergence of every loss mode. The next paper will change input representations to address frequency learning more directly.

24. Predict normalisation effects before changing the residual count

J^r=Jr/Nr,Tr=∥J^r∥F2\widehat J_r=J_r/\sqrt{N_r},\qquad T_r=\|\widehat J_r\|_F^2Jr​=Jr​/Nr​​,Tr​=∥Jr​∥F2​

If every residual point is duplicated with the same multiplicity, predict the trace of the mean-normalised residual block. Its trace stays unchanged because the repeated squared rows are divided by the increased count.

New spatial locations can change both sensitivities and constraint information, so they are not equivalent to duplicates. Save any modified experiment separately and compare field error, boundary error and kernel drift.

如果每个残差点都按相同次数重复，先预测均值归一化残差块的迹。重复行平方的增加被样本数量增加抵消，因此迹不变。

新的空间位置可能改变敏感度和约束信息，所以不等同于重复样本。修改实验时另存记录，比较场误差、边界误差与核变化。

25. Why change the input representation next?

Trace weights changed the aggregate influence of boundary and residual objectives, while small kernel modes and finite-network drift remained. Weighting alone is not a complete treatment of frequency learning.

The next paper examines Fourier features and eigenvector bias in multiscale learning. We will again use a small CPU example, asking which spatial patterns the representation makes easier to learn.

Teaching checks and reference answers

Ask for a prediction before running the exercise. Analytic expectations do not guarantee an optimiser outcome.

Does duplicating every residual point change the mean-normalised block trace, and does it add physical information?

With k identical copies of every row, the squared Jacobian sum grows by k while the mean-normalisation denominator also grows by k, so the residual block trace is unchanged. Copies add no new constraint locations or parameter-sensitivity directions. The expanded Gram matrix has a different dimension, including redundant modes, so do not compare average eigenvalues without tracking dimension. New spatial locations can change the trace and the constraint span. Any changed run must use a separate output file and independent field, boundary and residual checks.
