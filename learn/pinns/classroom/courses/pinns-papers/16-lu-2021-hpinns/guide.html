1. Paper16: Hard constraints and physics-informed inverse design

First define a heated-rod design problem and distinguish exact constructions from trained constraints. Then compare penalties and augmented Lagrangians through saved CPU experiments. Finally verify every design with independent physics and connect the sixteen-paper series.

先定义受热杆设计问题，区分精确构造与训练约束。再通过 CPU 检查点比较罚项与增广拉格朗日。最后用独立物理验证候选设计，并串联十六篇系列。

2. From inferring a system to choosing a better one

Hidden fluid mechanics asked what a system is doing and what observations can identify. Inverse design instead asks which admissible system we should build.

The final paper studies hard constraints and constrained optimisation. Our small heated rod will expose a particularly useful trap: a neural objective can look excellent while its predicted physics is wrong.

中文讲稿

隐藏流体力学问系统在做什么，以及观测能确定什么。逆设计则问，我们应该建造哪种允许的系统。

最后一篇研究硬约束与约束优化。小型受热杆会揭示一个很有价值的陷阱：神经目标可以极好看，预测物理却是错的。

3. Place limited material to reduce temperature

Look at the rod. Heat enters all along it, while both ends stay at zero temperature. Where should the better conductor go?

Colour shows rho, the local fraction of conducting material. Moving it changes how heat escapes. The arrows illustrate steady heating, not a transient simulation.

先看这根杆。热量沿整根杆输入，而两端温度保持为零。更好的导热材料，应该放在哪里呢？

颜色表示局部高导热材料比例，记作 ρ。移动这些材料，就改变了热量离开的方式。箭头表示稳态加热，不是瞬态模拟。

4. Every candidate design must satisfy the same physical balance

−ddx ⁣(k(ρ)ux)=1,u(0)=u(1)=0-\frac{d}{dx}\!\left(k(\rho)u_x\right)=1,\quad u(0)=u(1)=0−dxd​(k(ρ)ux​)=1,u(0)=u(1)=0

Start with heat moving along the rod. X is position, u is temperature, and k is local conductivity. A subscript x means a spatial derivative. The one on the right is our uniform heat source.

Now change the material. K changes with position, so it must stay inside the derivative. Taking it outside would describe a different rod.

中文讲稿

先顺着杆里的热量来看。x 是位置，u 是温度，k 是局部导热率。下标 x 表示空间求导。右边的一，是均匀热源。

现在，换一下材料分布。k 会随位置变化，所以必须留在导数里面。把它拿到外面，描述的就不是同一根杆了。

5. Material is bounded and its total amount is limited

k=0.2+1.8ρ,0<ρ<1,∫01ρ dx=0.4k=0.2+1.8\rho,\quad0<\rho<1,\quad\int_0^1\rho\,dx=0.4k=0.2+1.8ρ,0<ρ<1,∫01​ρdx=0.4

Rho is the fraction of the more conductive material. The linear mixing law places conductivity between zero point two and two in the selected units.

The integral fixes the total material budget at zero point four. Without a budget, filling the entire rod with the best conductor would trivialise this particular objective.

中文讲稿

ρ 是高导热材料的比例。线性混合规律把导热率限制在所选单位中的零点二到二之间。

积分把总材料预算固定为零点四。没有用量限制，整根杆全部使用最好导体，就会让这个目标变得平凡。

6. The objective is physical mean temperature

J(ρ)=∫01uρ(x) dxJ(\rho)=\int_0^1u_\rho(x)\,dxJ(ρ)=∫01​uρ​(x)dx

J denotes the design objective, and the subscript rho reminds us that temperature must solve the equation for that material. With a uniform source, this is also thermal compliance.

A neural field can estimate J only if it accurately represents that physical solution. Lowering the network output alone does not establish that the designed rod really performs better.

中文讲稿

J 表示设计目标，下标 ρ 提醒我们，温度必须是该材料对应的方程解。在均匀热源下，这也是热柔度。

只有准确代表该物理解，神经场才能估计 J。单纯降低网络输出，不能证明设计后的杆确实更好。

7. Hard constraints hold by construction

uθ=x(1−x)softplus⁡(Nθ(x))u_\theta=x(1-x)\operatorname{softplus}(N_\theta(x))uθ​=x(1−x)softplus(Nθ​(x))

N theta is our trainable network; theta collects its weights and biases. Look at the factor x times one minus x. What happens at either end? It becomes zero, so both endpoint temperatures are exact.

Softplus keeps the interior prediction nonnegative. But have we enforced heat balance inside the rod? Not yet. A correct boundary is only part of the problem.

中文讲稿

N θ 是待训练网络，θ 收集权重和偏置。看这个 x 乘一减 x 的因子。在任一端点，它会变成什么？零，所以两端温度严格满足要求。

softplus 保证内部预测非负。但杆里面的热平衡，也被保证了吗？还没有。正确边界，只完成了问题的一部分。

8. Use two design variables for a small auditable experiment

ρ=σ(d0+6tanh⁡(d1)cos⁡(2πx))\rho=\sigma(d_0+6\tanh(d_1)\cos(2\pi x))ρ=σ(d0​+6tanh(d1​)cos(2πx))

Sigma is the logistic function, d zero controls overall material amount, and d one controls a symmetric spatial redistribution. Tanh limits the cosine coefficient to a bounded range.

This is a restricted two-parameter family, not arbitrary topology optimisation. Together with a width-twenty-four temperature network, the model has seventy-five trainable parameters.

中文讲稿

σ 是逻辑函数，d 零控制总体材料量，d 一控制对称空间再分配。双曲正切把余弦系数限制在有界范围内。

这是受限的双参数族，不是任意拓扑优化。加上宽度二十四的温度网络，模型共有七十五个待训练参数。

9. Track physics and budget errors separately

rθ=−(kuθ,x)x−1,g=⟨ρ⟩−0.4r_\theta=-(k u_{\theta,x})_x-1,\quad g=\langle\rho\rangle-0.4rθ​=−(kuθ,x​)x​−1,g=⟨ρ⟩−0.4

R theta measures violation of the differential equation at the collocation points. G measures the deviation of the quadrature-averaged density from the required material amount.

A small g certifies only the budget check at those quadrature points. It does not establish a small physics residual or an accurate neural temperature.

中文讲稿

r θ 测量配置点上的微分方程违背程度。g 测量求积平均材料比例相对要求用量的偏差。

小 g 只说明这些求积点上的预算检查通过，不代表物理残差小，也不代表神经温度准确。

10. Fixed penalties trade off the objective and constraints

L=Jθ+β⟨rθ2⟩+γg2L=J_\theta+\beta\langle r_\theta^2\rangle+\gamma g^2L=Jθ​+β⟨rθ2​⟩+γg2

Beta and gamma are fixed weights on physics and budget violations. We compare a weak physics penalty with a much stronger penalty under the same training budget.

The weak model can reduce its reported temperature while tolerating a wrong equation. A strong penalty may improve feasibility but also change the optimisation landscape and leave little progress on design.

中文讲稿

β 与 γ 是物理和预算违背的固定权重。在相同训练预算下，比较弱物理惩罚与强得多的惩罚。

弱惩罚模型可以容忍错误方程，同时降低自己报告的温度。强惩罚可能改善可行性，也会改变优化地形，使设计进展有限。

11. An augmented Lagrangian remembers persistent violations

LA=Jθ+⟨λr⟩+μ2⟨r2⟩+λVg+μ2g2L_A=J_\theta+\langle\lambda r\rangle+\frac\mu2\langle r^2\rangle+\lambda_V g+\frac\mu2g^2LA​=Jθ​+⟨λr⟩+2μ​⟨r2⟩+λV​g+2μ​g2

Lambda denotes one multiplier per residual point, lambda V is the budget multiplier, and mu is the positive penalty parameter. The linear multiplier terms accumulate information about constraint violations.

Our teaching loop uses mu equal to ten and updates multipliers every two hundred and fifty parameter steps. This is a small joint primal-update adaptation, not the original paper’s full benchmark optimiser.

中文讲稿

λ 是每个残差点的乘子，λ V 是预算乘子，μ 是正惩罚参数。线性乘子项累积约束违背的信息。

教学循环取 μ 为十，每两百五十个参数更新步更新一次乘子。这是小型联合原变量更新实现，不是原论文完整基准优化器。

12. Multiplier updates provide feedback, not a guarantee

λ←λ+μr,λV←λV+μg\lambda\leftarrow\lambda+\mu r,\quad\lambda_V\leftarrow\lambda_V+\mu gλ←λ+μr,λV​←λV​+μg

After each outer interval, a persistent positive residual increases its multiplier, changing the next optimisation objective. The budget multiplier follows the same signed-feedback idea.

The neural and design parameters make this problem nonconvex. Finite updates and multiplier feedback do not guarantee a globally optimal or perfectly feasible result.

中文讲稿

每个外层间隔之后，持续为正的残差增大对应乘子，改变下一轮优化目标。预算乘子也采用相同的带符号反馈。

神经参数和设计参数使问题非凸。有限次更新与乘子反馈，不保证全局最优，也不保证完美可行。

20. A restricted-family sweep is a reference, not a global proof

We sweep one hundred and twenty-one cosine coefficients across the allowed design family. For each, bisection adjusts the offset to satisfy the material budget, followed by a finer independent physical solve.

This gives a useful numerical reference inside our symmetric family. It does not prove a global optimum over all possible material distributions or reproduce the original topology-design benchmarks.

中文讲稿

在允许设计族中扫描一百二十一个余弦系数。每个系数都用二分法调节偏置，满足材料预算，再进行更细独立物理求解。

这给出对称模型族内的实用数值参考。不证明所有材料分布中的全局最优，也不复现原始拓扑设计基准。

21. Hard constraints, feasibility and optimality are separate questions

Our endpoint, positivity and material-bound constraints hold by construction. Physics and volume are still trained constraints, and their residuals must be evaluated independently.

Even a feasible design need not be optimal. This seventy-five-parameter rod teaches the mechanism and verification discipline; it is not an optical hologram, a Stokes-flow topology design or a general convergence guarantee.

中文讲稿

端点、正值和材料界限通过构造成立。物理与用量仍是训练约束，必须独立评估残差。

即使设计可行，也不一定最优。这个七十五参数的杆讲解机制与验证方法，不是光学全息图、Stokes 流拓扑设计，也不是一般收敛保证。

22. Near-zero objective and exact endpoints: should we trust it?

Suppose a network reports almost zero average temperature and exact endpoint values. Would you accept the material design immediately, or first ask whether its interior temperature solves the heated-rod equation?

Solve the same candidate independently, verify its material amount and compare physical objectives. A wrong neural score does not automatically mean the candidate design is bad, but it does invalidate the claimed score.

中文讲稿

假设网络报告平均温度几乎为零，而且端点准确。你会立即接受材料设计，还是先问内部温度是否满足受热杆方程？

对同一候选做独立求解，检查材料用量，再比较物理目标。错误神经分数不自动表示候选设计差，却确实使该分数失去依据。

23. Close the series with physically verifiable conclusions

Across sixteen papers, we moved from forward and inverse equations through optimisation, sampling, boundaries, representations, uncertainty, hidden flow and design. These methods solve different bottlenecks rather than forming a universal ranking.

For your own problem, state the unknowns, identify the available information and choose a small falsifiable experiment. Keep unsuccessful controls, verify the physical claim independently, and only then decide whether a larger computation is justified.

中文讲稿

十六篇论文从正反问题走过优化、采样、边界、表示、不确定性、隐藏流动与设计。这些方法针对不同瓶颈，不构成通用排名。

面对自己的问题，先说明未知量、辨认已有信息，再选择一个可证伪的小实验。保留失败对照，独立验证物理结论，最后才决定是否值得扩大计算。

Teaching checks and reference answers

Ask for a prediction before running the exercise. Analytic expectations do not guarantee an optimiser outcome.

Does a near-zero neural objective establish a good physical design?

No. Exact endpoints and positive temperature do not enforce the interior differential equation. Independently solve the same material distribution and compare its actual objective, material budget and residuals. A false neural score invalidates the score, but does not alone establish that the material design is poor: our weak-penalty candidates can improve the independently verified objective despite inaccurate neural fields.
