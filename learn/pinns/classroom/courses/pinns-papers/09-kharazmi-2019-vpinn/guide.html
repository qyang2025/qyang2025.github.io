1. Paper 09: VPINNs and weak-form residuals

The cPINN lecture connected physical interfaces, so we now ask how the equation itself should be tested. First derive a weak residual using a heated rod and test functions. Then run twelve CPU controls and distinguish missing tests, inaccurate integration and actual field error.

中文讲稿

上篇 cPINN 连接了物理接口，本篇进一步追问应当怎样检查方程本身。先从受热杆与测试函数推导弱残差。再通过十二次 CPU 对照，区分测试不足、积分不准与实际场误差。

2. After connecting interfaces, why change the residual?

The cPINN episode connected neighbouring material models, but each local equation still required pointwise second derivatives. This paper changes how we test that equation.

We will weight the residual, integrate it, and transfer one derivative to a known test function. Then a small controlled experiment will expose both the benefit and the information we can lose.

中文讲稿

上篇 cPINN 连接相邻材料模型，但局部方程仍需要逐点二阶导数。本篇改变检查方程的方式。

给残差加权并积分，再把一个导数转移到已知测试函数。随后用小型受控实验观察这个形式带来的便利与可能丢失的信息。

3. Start with a heated rod cooled at both ends

Both ends of this single-material rod are held at the reference temperature, while a known distributed source heats its interior. U is the normalised temperature above that reference and x is position along the rod.

Heat flows outward towards the cold ends, with its direction changing near the temperature maximum. The moving dots illustrate steady heat flow, not a time-stepping simulation.

中文讲稿

这根单材料杆的两端保持参考温度，内部由已知分布热源加热。u 是高于参考值的归一化温度，x 是沿杆的位置。

热量流向两端，流动方向在温度峰值附近改变。移动点表示稳态热流，不是随时间推进的数值模拟。

4. State the same physical problem for every method

−uxx=f(x),x∈(−1,1),u(−1)=u(1)=0-u_{xx}=f(x),\quad x\in(-1,1),\quad u(-1)=u(1)=0−uxx​=f(x),x∈(−1,1),u(−1)=u(1)=0

We use unit conductivity, so the steady heat equation is minus the second spatial derivative of temperature equals f. F denotes the prescribed heat source, and both endpoint temperatures are zero.

Our teaching source is two plus zero point two times pi squared times sine of pi x. The independent reference is one minus x squared plus zero point two times sine of pi x; it is used for validation, never as an interior training label.

中文讲稿

取单位导热系数，稳态热方程就是温度空间二阶导数的负值等于 f。f 表示给定热源，两端温度为零。

教学热源取二加零点二乘圆周率平方再乘正弦圆周率 x。独立参考解是一减 x 平方再加零点二乘正弦圆周率 x；它只用于验证，不作内部训练标签。

5. Strong form checks the equation at individual locations

rθ(x)=−uθ,xx(x)−f(x)r_\theta(x)=-u_{\theta,xx}(x)-f(x)rθ​(x)=−uθ,xx​(x)−f(x)

Theta denotes all learned network parameters, and r theta is the strong residual. A conventional PINN evaluates this mismatch at specified spatial locations.

Our strong-form control uses thirty-two fixed midpoints and the mean squared residual. It shares the network, boundary construction, initial weights and update budget with the variational runs.

中文讲稿

θ 表示全部待学习的网络参数，r θ 是强形式残差。普通 PINN 在指定空间位置计算这个不匹配。

强形式对照使用三十二个固定中点和残差平方均值。它与变分运行共享网络、边界构造、初始权重和更新预算。

6. Test functions are weighting shapes for checking physics

Rk(θ)=∫−11rθ(x)vk(x) dxR_k(\theta)=\int_{-1}^{1}r_\theta(x)v_k(x)\,dxRk​(θ)=∫−11​rθ​(x)vk​(x)dx

A test function v k is a known weighting shape, not another temperature prediction or a learned label. R k is the signed residual integral against that shape, and k identifies which test we use.

The blue and gold curves show the first two weighting shapes. Different shapes detect different spatial patterns, so one small integral cannot certify the entire residual field.

中文讲稿

测试函数 v k 是已知的加权形状，不是另一个温度预测，也不是学习标签。R k 是残差与该形状相乘后的带符号积分，k 标识所用测试。

蓝色和金色曲线展示前两个加权形状。不同形状检查不同空间模式，因此一个积分很小不能证明整个残差场很小。

7. See what cancellation inside an integral allows

∫−11r(x)v1(x) dx=0 ⇏ r(x)=0\int_{-1}^{1}r(x)v_1(x)\,dx=0\ \not\Rightarrow\ r(x)=0∫−11​r(x)v1​(x)dx=0 ⇒ r(x)=0

For this illustrative drawing, the residual is three x and the first test is an even function. Their product is odd, so equal positive and negative areas cancel.

The displayed residual is a mathematical example, not a trained model result. Squaring the integral after projection does not undo the information lost through cancellation.

中文讲稿

这个示意图把残差取为三 x，第一个测试函数为偶函数。二者的乘积是奇函数，因此正负面积恰好抵消。

图中的残差是数学示例，不是训练结果。先投影再把积分平方，并不能恢复抵消时丢掉的信息。

8. Move one derivative onto the known test function

−∫−11uθ′′vk dx=∫−11uθ′vk′ dx−[uθ′vk]−11-\int_{-1}^{1}u_\theta''v_k\,dx=\int_{-1}^{1}u_\theta'v_k'\,dx-[u_\theta'v_k]_{-1}^{1}−∫−11​uθ′′​vk​dx=∫−11​uθ′​vk′​dx−[uθ′​vk​]−11​

Integration by parts replaces the second derivative of the network with its first derivative multiplied by the derivative of the test. The bracket is the endpoint contribution, with the right endpoint minus the left.

We choose tests that vanish at both endpoints, which makes this particular bracket zero. This step lowers the spatial derivative order required from the network; it does not remove parameter backpropagation.

中文讲稿

分部积分把网络二阶导数换成网络一阶导数与测试函数导数的乘积。方括号表示右端值减左端值的边界贡献。

选择两端都为零的测试函数，就让这里的边界项消失。这降低了对网络求空间导数的阶数，但不取消参数反向传播。

9. The weak residual still comes from the same heat equation

Rk=∫−11uθ′vk′ dx−∫−11fvk dxR_k=\int_{-1}^{1}u_\theta'v_k'\,dx-\int_{-1}^{1}f v_k\,dxRk​=∫−11​uθ′​vk′​dx−∫−11​fvk​dx

The first integral weights the temperature gradient by the derivative of the known test function. This is the gradient contribution shown on the left of the diagram.

The second integral weights the prescribed heat source by the test function itself. Subtracting these contributions gives the weak residual, a signed moment rather than an integral of squared pointwise error.

中文讲稿

第一个积分用已知测试函数的导数，对温度梯度加权。这就是示意图左边的梯度贡献。

第二个积分用测试函数本身，对给定热源加权。把这两个贡献相减，得到的是带符号的弱残差矩，不是逐点残差平方的积分。

10. Make the test shapes vanish at the endpoints

vk=Pk+1−Pk−1,vk(±1)=0v_k=P_{k+1}-P_{k-1},\qquad v_k(\pm1)=0vk​=Pk+1​−Pk−1​,vk​(±1)=0

P k denotes the Legendre polynomial of degree k. This difference of neighbouring polynomial degrees is the test family selected from the paper.

We keep its original unnormalised scaling, with derivative equal to two k plus one times P k. The tests are fixed functions; the neural trial space and polynomial test space are different, giving the Petrov–Galerkin construction.

中文讲稿

P k 表示 k 次勒让德多项式。这里选用论文中的相邻次数多项式之差作为测试函数族。

保留未归一化的缩放，导数等于二 k 加一再乘 P k。测试函数是固定的；神经网络试探空间与多项式测试空间不同，这就是 Petrov–Galerkin 构造。

11. Separate the number of tests from the number of integration nodes

K counts the test functions, so it determines how many residual patterns are checked. Increasing K asks more questions of the learned field.

Q counts the quadrature nodes used to approximate each integral. More Q can reduce integration error but cannot recover an untested pattern, which is separate from changing network capacity.

中文讲稿

K 是测试函数数目，决定要检查多少个残差模式。增加 K 是向学习场提出更多问题。

Q 是近似每个积分所用的节点数。更多 Q 可以改善积分精度，却不能检测已选测试都看不见的模式，这与改变网络容量又是不同选择。

12. Compute integrals with weighted nodes

∫−11g(x) dx ≈ ∑q=1Qωqg(xq)\int_{-1}^{1}g(x)\,dx\ \approx\ \sum\nolimits_{q=1}^{Q}\omega_q g(x_q)∫−11​g(x)dx ≈ ∑q=1Q​ωq​g(xq​)

G denotes the current integrand, x q is a quadrature location, and omega q is its integration weight. The diagram uses x to the eighth power simply to make integration error visible.

We use Gauss–Legendre nodes and weights on the full interval. Four nodes are exact only for polynomials through degree seven, so this eighth-degree example needs more nodes; neural integrands are generally not polynomials.

中文讲稿

g 表示当前被积函数，x q 是积分位置，ω q 是积分权重。图中用 x 的八次方，只为直观展示积分误差。

使用整区间上的高斯—勒让德节点和权重。四节点只保证七次以内多项式精确，因此这个八次例子需要更多节点；神经网络被积函数通常并不是多项式。

13. Form each moment first, then average its square

Lweak=1K∑k=1K∣RkQ(θ)∣2\mathcal L_{\rm weak}=\frac1K\sum_{k=1}^{K}\left|R_k^Q(\theta)\right|^2Lweak​=K1​k=1∑K​​RkQ​(θ)​2

R k with superscript Q denotes the quadrature approximation of one weak residual. First compute that weighted moment for every selected test using Q integration nodes.

Only after forming those K moments do we average their squared values. Raw strong and weak losses measure different objects and should not be compared as if they were the same accuracy metric.

中文讲稿

上标 Q 表示一个弱残差的数值积分近似。先用 Q 个积分节点，为每个已选测试计算带权残差矩。

形成这 K 个矩之后，才对它们的平方取平均。强形式和弱形式的原始损失衡量不同对象，不能把它们当作同一种精度指标比较。

14. Use the same endpoint construction in every control

uθ(x)=(1−x2)Nθ(x)u_\theta(x)=(1-x^2)N_\theta(x)uθ​(x)=(1−x2)Nθ​(x)

N theta is a small tanh network, multiplied by one minus x squared. This factor makes both endpoint temperatures exactly zero for any parameter values.

We use this simple one-dimensional convenience for every policy, rather than claiming the original paper used it in these experiments. A later paper will examine boundary constructions on more general geometries.

中文讲稿

N θ 是一个小型双曲正切网络，再乘上一减 x 平方。无论参数怎样变化，这个因子都让两端温度严格为零。

每种策略都采用这个一维便利构造，不把它说成原论文这些实验的设置。后续论文会专门研究更一般几何中的边界构造。

15. Professor demo: what the weak-form training loop computes

ux = grad(u, x, create_graph=True)
R = sum_q(w * (ux*dv - f*v))
loss = mean_k(R**2)
loss.backward(); opt.step()

Follow the five lines from the temperature trial function to the spatial derivative, the weighted moments and their squared loss. Only the test functions and quadrature weights are precomputed constants.

Backpropagation still differentiates that loss with respect to every network parameter. The downloadable program implements these steps in PyTorch on the CPU, and the following plots replay its saved checkpoints.

16. Four constraint choices, three paired seeds, one small network

Each network has three hundred and twenty-one parameters and is trained for two thousand Adam updates. We reuse the same initial weights within each seed across all four policies.

The controls are strong form at thirty-two midpoints, weak form with one or eight tests at thirty-two quadrature nodes, and eight tests at only four nodes. All twelve runs finished their update budget in roughly four to seven seconds each on four CPU threads.

中文讲稿

每个网络有三百二十一个参数，训练两千次 Adam 更新。每个种子内，四种策略复用相同初始权重。

对照分别为三十二中点强形式、三十二积分点下的一或八个弱测试，以及只有四积分点的八测试。十二次运行都完成更新预算，在四个 CPU 线程上每次约四到七秒。

17. Inspect the temperature field before trusting the training loss

The dashed line is the independent analytic temperature reference, and the coloured curves replay seed eleven. Watch how the four constraint choices lead to different learned fields.

The strong control already fits this smooth problem very accurately. Eight weak tests also give a close field, whereas one test and the underintegrated control can miss substantial physics.

虚线是独立解析温度参考，彩色曲线回放种子十一。观察四种约束选择如何得到不同学习场。

强形式对照已经很准确地拟合这个光滑问题。八个弱测试也得到接近的场，而一个测试和积分不足对照会漏掉大量物理信息。

18. Keep every seed rather than select the most attractive run

Every dot is an independently trained model, and all three seeds are retained for every policy. The vertical axis is relative field error in percent on a logarithmic scale.

The strong-form errors are approximately zero point zero one to zero point zero five percent, and the eight-test well-integrated errors are about zero point zero four to zero point one four percent. This particular experiment does not show a universal accuracy advantage for VPINNs.

中文讲稿

每个点是独立训练的模型，每种策略都保留全部三个种子。纵轴是以百分比表示、采用对数刻度的场相对误差。

强形式误差约为百分之零点零一到零点零五，八测试充分积分时约为百分之零点零四到零点一四。这个具体实验并不支持 VPINN 总有精度优势的说法。

19. One zero moment can hide substantial field error

The one-test runs reach a numerically zero training loss, yet their field errors range from roughly nine to thirty-four percent. The audit plot checks sixteen moments using a separate one hundred and twenty-eight-node rule.

A constraint can be perfectly satisfied while being insufficient to identify the solution. This is a finite-test-space limitation, not evidence that a zero loss proves the physics everywhere.

中文讲稿

单测试运行的训练损失在数值上为零，但场误差仍约为百分之九到三十四。审计图用独立的一百二十八节点规则检查十六个矩。

一个约束即使完全满足，也可能不足以确定解。这说明有限测试空间有局限，并不表示零损失能证明处处满足物理。

20. A mode the first test cannot see

∫−11w′(x)v1′(x) dx=0\int_{-1}^{1}w'(x)v_1'(x)\,dx=0∫−11​w′(x)v1′​(x)dx=0

Here w is the polynomial P three minus P one, which vanishes at both endpoints. Adding zero point three times w changes the temperature field while leaving its first weak residual unchanged.

The first residual is blind because w prime is proportional to P two, orthogonal to the P one in the first test derivative. The second test does detect the perturbation, so choosing the test space matters even with exact integration.

中文讲稿

这里 w 是三次勒让德多项式减一次多项式，在两端都为零。加上零点三乘 w 会改变温度场，却不改变第一个弱残差。

第一个残差看不见它，是因为 w 的导数正比于 P 二，与第一测试导数中的 P 一正交。第二测试能检测该扰动，所以即使积分精确，测试空间的选择仍然重要。

21. Underintegration can create incompatible discrete constraints

v4′(xq)=9P4(xq)=0(Q=4)v_4'(x_q)=9P_4(x_q)=0\qquad(Q=4)v4′​(xq​)=9P4​(xq​)=0(Q=4)

The four-node Gauss rule places every node at a zero of P four. The fourth test derivative therefore vanishes at every training node.

Its discrete source moment is nevertheless about one point three five, so no network slope values can repair that mismatch under this rule. An independent least-squares calculation gives a minimum mean squared moment loss of about three point zero five, matching all three underintegrated runs.

中文讲稿

四节点高斯规则把所有节点放在 P 四的零点。因此第四测试的导数在全部训练节点处为零。

但离散热源矩约为一点三五，所以在这个积分规则下，任何网络斜率值都无法修复该项不匹配。独立最小二乘计算得到残差矩平方均值的下限约三点零五，与三个积分不足运行一致。

22. Validate beyond the checks used during training

We compare the field and heat flux against independent analytic values and test strong residuals at shifted locations. We also recompute moments with more nodes and more test functions than the training rule.

The three hundred and one field points, two hundred and fifty-seven shifted residual points and sixteen audit tests do not choose a checkpoint. Every reported result uses the final two-thousand-update model.

中文讲稿

把场和热流与独立解析值比较，再在偏移位置检查强残差。还使用比训练更多的节点和测试函数重新计算残差矩。

三百零一个场位置、二百五十七个偏移残差位置和十六个审计测试都不用于选择检查点。报告结果全部来自最终两千更新的模型。

23. Retain the paper’s idea and state the implementation limits

We retain the paper’s Petrov–Galerkin idea, selected Legendre tests and once-integrated residual. Our source, hard endpoint construction, small tanh network and Gauss–Legendre budget are explicit teaching changes.

We do not reproduce the original shallow sine formulas, steep-layer benchmarks, two-dimensional comparisons or hp-VPINNs. Lower derivative order is a useful mechanism, but it does not by itself guarantee better accuracy or faster time to a prescribed error.

中文讲稿

保留论文的 Petrov–Galerkin 思想、所选勒让德测试和一次分部积分残差。热源、硬端点构造、小型双曲正切网络与高斯—勒让德预算都是明确的教学改变。

没有复现原始浅层正弦解析公式、陡层基准、二维比较或 hp-VPINNs。降低导数阶数是有用机制，但它本身不保证更高精度，也不保证更快达到给定误差。

24. Predict what more tests and more nodes can repair

Keep the network and update budget fixed, and predict what happens when one test uses sixty-four rather than thirty-two nodes. Then predict what a second test does to the analytic perturbation w.

Explain why these are different interventions before running new experiments. Save any exploratory record under a new filename so that the published twelve-run evidence stays unchanged.

先解释这两种干预为何不同，再运行新实验。探索记录要保存为新文件，不要覆盖已发布的十二次运行证据。

25. After residual formulations, why study boundary constructions?

This episode reduced spatial derivative order but relied on a very simple one-dimensional boundary factor. Real geometries make that construction less obvious, and soft penalties introduce their own trade-offs.

The next paper studies geometry-aware trial functions for imposing boundary conditions exactly. That is a separate design choice from the strong-versus-weak residual comparison we have just completed.

中文讲稿

本篇降低了空间求导阶数，但依赖很简单的一维边界因子。真实几何让这种构造不再明显，而软惩罚也有自己的取舍。

下一篇研究通过几何相关试探函数精确满足边界条件。它与刚完成的强弱残差比较，是不同的设计选择。

Teaching checks and reference answers

Ask for a prediction before running the exercise. Analytic expectations do not guarantee an optimiser outcome.

What can more quadrature nodes repair, and what can an additional test detect?

Increasing Q improves an approximation of the same selected moment; it cannot remove an exact null space of that test. For w=P3-P1, orthogonality gives moment1=0 and moment2=10, so adding0.3w preserves the first moment and changes the second by3. Adding test2 therefore detects this particular perturbation, but it still does not certify the complete field: P4-P2 is invisible to both tests1and2. Run K1Q64 and K2Q64 with the same seed and final update budget, recording actual errors without expecting any guaranteed optimiser outcome. Save each record under a new filename.

更多积分节点可以修复什么，额外测试又能检测什么？

增加 Q 改善的是同一测试所定义积分的近似，不能去掉该测试的精确零空间。对 w=P3-P1，正交性给出第一矩零、第二矩十，因此加上零点三乘 w 保留第一矩、让第二矩改变三。增加第二测试能发现这一个扰动，但仍不能证明整个场：P4-P2 对前两个测试都不可见。以相同种子和最终更新预算运行 K1Q64 与 K2Q64，记录实际误差，不预设优化器一定成功；分别保存新文件。
