1. Paper13: FBPINNs and local spatial representations

First explain overlapping local representations using the same multiscale temperature field as the previous lesson. Then connect coordinate scaling, complete product derivatives and equal-parameter controls. Finally replay every CPU result, separate smooth assembly from physical accuracy, and state which scheduling and scaling claims this experiment does not test.

中文讲稿

先用与上篇相同的多尺度温度场解释重叠局部表示。再连接坐标缩放、完整乘积求导与相同参数数目的对照。最后回放全部 CPU 结果，区分平滑组装与物理精度，并说明这个实验没有检验哪些调度或扩展性结论。

2. From global frequency features to local representations

The Fourier-feature lesson changed the spatial patterns preferred by one global representation. Its sensitivity to bandwidth and random initialisation motivates a different question: could smaller local representations help?

This paper introduces finite basis physics-informed neural networks, or F B PINNs. We will combine overlapping windows and local coordinates, then test the idea without assuming it must improve every small experiment.

中文讲稿

傅里叶特征这一讲改变了一个全局表示偏爱的空间模式。它对带宽与随机初始化敏感，因此引出另一个问题：较小的局部表示能否有所帮助？

本篇介绍有限基物理信息神经网络，也就是 FBPINNs。我们将组合重叠窗口与局部坐标，再做实验，不预先假定它一定改善每个小问题。

Look at the broad rise and fall, then the little ripples riding on it. Can one large network capture both comfortably? The moving dots only illustrate steady heat-flow direction; this picture is neither a transient simulation nor a trained prediction.

5. Overlap means shared contribution, not a hard cut

Each coloured curve, w i, is one local network’s window. See the strips where two colours overlap? Both neighbours contribute there, so we are not handing the solution from one network to another with a hard switch.

The windows fade to zero outside their supports rather than abruptly selecting a single model. In our construction, each window and its first two derivatives vanish at its support edges.

中文讲稿

每条彩色曲线都是一个局部网络的窗口，记作 w i。看到两种颜色重叠的那一段了吗？那里由两个邻居共同贡献，并不是从一个网络突然切换到另一个网络。

窗口在支撑范围外逐渐归零，而不是突然只选择一个模型。我们的构造让窗口及其前两阶导数在支撑边缘均为零。

6. Rescale each subdomain into its own local coordinates

C i is the centre of subdomain i, and h i is its support radius in physical coordinates. The four centres in this experiment are spread evenly along the unit rod.

Now zoom into one patch. Z i measures distance from its centre in units of that patch’s radius, stretching the patch across minus one to one. We have not erased the ripples; each small network simply has fewer of them to represent.

中文讲稿

c i 是子区域 i 的中心，h i 是物理坐标中的支撑半径。这个实验的四个中心均匀分布在单位长度的杆上。

现在，把一个小区域放大来看。z i 用这个区域的半径作为距离单位，将它展开到负一至一。小波纹没有被删掉，只是每个小网络需要处理的波纹少了一些。

7. Choose compact windows with continuous second derivatives

wi(x)=[max⁡(0,1−zi2)]3w_i(x)=\big[\max(0,1-z_i^2)\big]^3wi​(x)=[max(0,1−zi2​)]3

W i is the polynomial window used in our teaching implementation. It is positive for local coordinates between minus one and one, and exactly zero outside that interval.

The cubic power makes the function twice continuously differentiable at the support edges. This differs from the original paper’s sigmoid windows, and these windows are summed directly rather than normalised into a partition of unity.

中文讲稿

w i 是教学实现中使用的多项式窗口。它在局部坐标负一到一之间为正，在区间外严格为零。

三次幂使函数在支撑边缘连续可求二阶导数。这不同于原论文的 sigmoid 窗口；这些窗口直接相加，没有归一化为单位分解。

8. Sum local contributions, then impose the boundary constraint

vθ(x)=∑i=14wi(x)Ni(zi),uθ=x(1−x)vθv_\theta(x)=\sum_{i=1}^{4}w_i(x)N_i(z_i),\quad u_\theta=x(1-x)v_\thetavθ​(x)=i=1∑4​wi​(x)Ni​(zi​),uθ​=x(1−x)vθ​

N i is an independent trainable tanh subnetwork, theta collects all their trainable weights and biases, and v theta is the sum of all windowed local contributions. Unlike the previous Fourier architecture, these local networks do not share their trainable weights.

Multiplying the sum by x times one minus x imposes zero endpoint temperatures exactly. Smoothness follows from the complete constructed field, while satisfying the physical equation still requires training.

N i 是独立待训练的双曲正切子网络，θ 表示全部待训练权重与偏置，v θ 是全部加窗局部贡献之和。与上篇傅里叶架构不同，这些局部网络不共享待训练权重。

把总和乘以 x 与一减 x，便精确施加两端零温度。平滑性来自完整构造场，而满足物理方程仍然需要训练。

9. Window derivatives also enter the physical curvature

(wN)xx=wxxN+2wxNx+wNxx(wN)_{xx}=w_{xx}N+2w_xN_x+wN_{xx}(wN)xx​=wxx​N+2wx​Nx​+wNxx​

Here is an easy mistake: differentiate the network and forget the window. Why is that wrong? The physical contribution is their product, so its curvature includes the window’s derivatives too.

Follow the three terms in the equation: window curvature, a cross term, and network curvature. Every subscript x means a physical-coordinate derivative, including coordinate scaling. That is why our autodiff graph keeps the windows, the full sum and the endpoint factor together.

中文讲稿

这里很容易犯一个错误：只对网络求导，把窗口忘了。为什么不行呢？物理贡献是两者的乘积，所以曲率里也必须包含窗口的导数。

顺着公式看这三项：窗口的曲率、交叉项，以及网络的曲率。下标 x 都表示对物理坐标求导，也要包含坐标缩放。这就是为什么自动微分必须保留窗口、完整求和与端点因子。

10. Narrower supports amplify derivative scales

∂x=hi−1∂zi,∂xx=hi−2∂zizi\partial_x=h_i^{-1}\partial_{z_i},\qquad \partial_{xx}=h_i^{-2}\partial_{z_i z_i}∂x​=hi−1​∂zi​​,∂xx​=hi−2​∂zi​zi​​

The inverse radius appears once for a first derivative and twice for a second derivative. Therefore the same local shape can create much larger physical curvature when its support is narrowed.

Local representation benefits do not remove derivative conditioning or the need to resolve transitions with residual points. We include a narrow-overlap control to test this trade-off rather than treating narrower windows as automatically better.

中文讲稿

一阶导数包含一次半径倒数，二阶导数包含两次。因此同一个局部形状在支撑收窄后，可能产生更大的物理曲率。

局部表示的好处没有消除导数条件问题，也没有免除用残差点分辨过渡的需求。我们加入窄重叠对照来检验这个取舍，而不是认为窗口越窄越好。

12. Separate normalisation and overlap at equal parameter counts

Before comparing curves, let’s make the budget fair. One global network has ninety-seven hidden units; the local alternatives each have four independent networks with twenty-four units. Either way, the total is two hundred and ninety-two trainable parameters.

The raw-coordinate control has the same windows and initial weights as the locally normalised model, but passes the physical x directly to each network. The narrow control also normalises locally but reduces the support radius from zero point three five to zero point one four.

中文讲稿

看曲线之前，先把参数预算对齐。全局方案用一个九十七单元的网络，局部方案用四个各二十四单元的独立网络。不论哪一种，总共都是二百九十二个待训练参数。

原坐标对照与局部归一化模型共用窗口和初始权重，却直接把物理 x 输入每个网络。窄窗对照也做局部归一化，但将支撑半径从零点三五缩小为零点一四。

13. Use random seeds to check dependence on the starting point

A seed fixes the random starting draws, allowing an experiment to be reproduced in the same environment. Eleven, twenty-two and thirty-three are simply the three identifiers chosen for this study.

For each seed, the three local controls share their initial trainable weights, and all four representations receive the same points and update budget. Equal parameter counts do not imply identical initial functions, equal runtime or equivalent optimisation problems.

中文讲稿

随机种子固定随机起点的抽样，使实验能够在相同环境下复现。十一、二十二和三十三，只是本次选择的三个编号。

每个种子下，三种局部对照共享初始待训练权重，四种表示得到相同的点与更新预算。参数数目相等，不表示初始函数相同、耗时相等，或者优化问题等价。

w = clamp(1 - z*z, min=0)**3
parts = cat([w_i * net_i(z_i)], dim=1)

r = -grad(grad(u, x), x) - source(x)

The first lines form local coordinates and compact polynomial windows. The displayed contribution list abbreviates a loop over all four independent networks in the downloadable implementation.

Spatial derivatives are taken only after summing the contributions and multiplying by the hard-boundary factor. We train all networks jointly using full-batch Adam, four CPU threads and sixty-four-bit arithmetic for two thousand updates per run.

18. Window smoothness and training difficulty are different questions

Our narrow window remains twice continuously differentiable, but its transition occurs over a shorter physical distance. Large derivatives and limited sampling can still make the residual optimisation difficult.

Every model has exact endpoint values by construction, yet its interior field can still be inaccurate. We retain shifted-point residual checks and independent slope errors to avoid judging accuracy from the endpoints alone.

中文讲稿

窄窗仍然连续可求二阶导数，但过渡发生在更短的物理距离上。较大的导数与有限采样，仍可能使残差优化困难。

所有模型都通过构造精确满足端点值，内部场却仍可能不准确。我们保留偏移点残差检查与独立斜率误差，避免只看端点就判断精度。

19. The paper also allows local training schedules

The original method can activate selected subnetworks, keep trained neighbours fixed and progress across the domain. Its parallel algorithm exchanges neighbouring contributions while controlling which parameter gradients are updated.

Our small experiment uses a simpler joint global optimisation loop with every network active. It does not implement detached-neighbour parallel updates or a travelling training schedule, so its measured timings are not a parallel scalability benchmark.

中文讲稿

原方法可以激活选定子网络，固定已训练的邻居，再逐步走过整个区域。其并行算法交换邻居贡献，并控制更新哪些参数梯度。

我们的小实验采用更简单的联合全局优化循环，全部网络都激活。没有实现邻居梯度分离的并行更新，也没有移动式训练调度，因此测量耗时不是并行扩展性基准。

20. Keep the limits of this one-dimensional adaptation explicit

We retain independent local networks, smooth overlapping support and separate input normalisation. We change the window family, omit output unnormalisation and use a common hard-boundary factor for all controls.

All final checkpoints, seeds and runtimes are downloadable, including any poor outcomes. No original large-wave benchmark, finite-element comparison or universal superiority is claimed from this compact rod example.

中文讲稿

保留独立局部网络、平滑重叠支撑与单独输入归一化。改变了窗口族，省略输出反归一化，并为所有对照采用共同硬边界因子。

全部最终检查点、种子与耗时均可下载，也包含表现较差的结果。这个小型杆示例不声称复现原始大型波动基准、有限元比较，或证明普遍优越性。

21. Predict what happens when the support radius is halved

hi↦hi/2⟹hi−2↦4hi−2h_i\mapsto h_i/2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_i^{-2}\mapsto4h_i^{-2}hi​↦hi​/2⟹hi−2​↦4hi−2​

For a fixed shape in local coordinates, halving the support radius multiplies its second-derivative scale by four. This is a chain-rule prediction, not a guarantee about the trained error.

Before running that change, check whether the fixed centres still provide coverage and overlap across the rod. Save modified runs separately, then compare full-field, slope and shifted-residual errors for every seed.

中文讲稿

对局部坐标中的固定形状，支撑半径减半，会把二阶导数尺度放大四倍。这是链式法则的预测，不是对训练误差的保证。

运行这个改变前，检查固定中心能否继续覆盖并重叠整个杆。修改运行必须另存，再对每个种子比较完整场、斜率与偏移残差误差。

22. Next: move from one prediction to uncertainty

Changing the representation can change an optimiser’s result, but a single predicted curve still hides uncertainty. Repeating random initialisations measures training variability and is not automatically Bayesian uncertainty.

The next paper introduces Bayesian PINNs and actual posterior inference in a tiny problem. We will distinguish uncertainty from data, model assumptions and numerical approximation while staying within a small CPU budget.

中文讲稿

改变表示可以改变优化结果，但单条预测曲线仍隐藏了不确定性。重复随机初始化测量的是训练变动，并不会自动变成贝叶斯不确定性。

下一篇介绍贝叶斯 PINNs，并在极小问题中进行真实后验推断。我们将区分数据、模型假设与数值近似带来的不确定性，同时保持小型 CPU 预算。

What changes when a support radius halves, and can the same centres still cover the domain?

For a fixed local shape, the physical second derivative carries h to the power minus two, so halving h multiplies that scale by four. This does not predict the trained error because the learned shape can change. Coverage must be checked independently: our centres are spaced by0.25 and lie0.125 from the endpoints. Radius0.35 halved to0.175 retains coverage and overlap, while radius0.14 halved to0.07 leaves gaps. Compact windows and their first two derivatives vanish outside support, so no network can correct an uncovered interval in this ansatz.
