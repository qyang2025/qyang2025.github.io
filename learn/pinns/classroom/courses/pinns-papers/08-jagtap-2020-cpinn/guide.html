1. Paper 08: cPINN and conservative material interfaces

The sampling paper chose where to enforce one model, so we now examine how neighbouring material models should be connected. First use a composite heated rod to derive state and flux interface conditions. Then compare twelve small CPU runs, inspecting temperature, heat flow, interface jumps and global energy balance.

中文讲稿

采样论文选择检查一个模型的位置，本篇研究相邻材料模型应当怎样连接。先用复合产热杆推导状态与通量接口条件。再比较十二次小型 CPU 运行，检查温度、热流、接口差与整体能量平衡。

2. After choosing points, explain how regions connect

Residual-based sampling changed where a model was tested, but some problems contain different materials on the two sides of a joint. More enforcement points alone do not define how those materials exchange heat. This episode therefore introduces conservative interface coupling.

We will use two tiny networks on one composite rod, then remove interface conditions one at a time. The purpose is to understand the missing physics rather than claim a large-scale speedup. The experiment is a small teaching adaptation of the cPINN idea.

中文讲稿

基于残差的采样改变了检查模型的位置，但有些问题的接头两侧是不同材料。仅增加约束点，不能定义它们如何交换热量。因此，本篇引入守恒接口耦合。

在一根复合杆上使用两个很小的网络，再逐个移除接口条件。目标是理解缺少的物理，而不是声称大规模加速。这是 cPINN 思想的小型教学改编。

3. Two materials: continuous temperature, different slopes

Imagine a rod made from two perfectly bonded materials, with its left end held hot and its right end cold. Heat is also generated uniformly inside the rod. The temperature profile is continuous at the joint but bends differently in the two materials.

U denotes the normalised steady temperature and x is position along the rod. The moving markers indicate heat flow to the right, not a numerical transient simulation. Watch the change of slope at the material joint before we express it mathematically.

中文讲稿

想象两种材料完美粘接成杆，左端保持高温，右端保持低温。杆内还均匀产生热量。温度曲线在接头处连续，但两种材料内的弯曲不同。

u 表示归一化稳态温度，x 是沿杆位置。移动标记表示热量向右流动，不是数值瞬态模拟。先观察材料接头处斜率的改变，再用数学表达。

4. Define the material regions and interface location

a=12,kL=1,kR=4a=\tfrac12,\qquad k_L=1,\quad k_R=4a=21​,kL​=1,kR​=4

The joint is at position a equal to one half of the unit interval. K L and k R are the known positive conductivities in the left and right materials. We set them to one and four.

A higher conductivity can carry the same heat flux with a smaller temperature slope. This explains why continuity of temperature should not be confused with continuity of its spatial derivative. We will enforce the physical flux rather than blindly matching derivatives.

中文讲稿

接口位于单位区间的一半，记作 a。k L 与 k R 是左右材料已知的正导热系数。分别取一和四。

更高的导热系数可以用更小的温度斜率传递同一热通量。因此，温度连续不等于空间导数连续。要约束物理通量，而不是盲目匹配导数。

5. Start from energy balance on a short rod segment

qx=s,s=1q_x=s,\qquad s=1qx​=s,s=1

Let q be heat flux, defined as positive toward the right. In steady state, the increase of flux along a short segment balances its internal heat generation. S is that prescribed source per unit length, chosen as one in normalised units.

The equation says q x equals s, so the total outward flux difference across the rod is one. Steady state does not mean that flux is constant when heat is generated internally. We will use this global balance as a separate validation check.

中文讲稿

令 q 为热通量，以向右为正。稳态时，一小段杆的热通量增量平衡内部产热。s 是给定的单位长度热源，在归一化单位中取一。

方程说明 q x 等于 s，因此整杆两端的流出与流入通量之差为一。有内部产热时，稳态并不表示通量处处恒定。后面会把整体平衡作为单独验证。

6. Use heat flow to explain the sign and conductivity

qm=−kmum,x,m∈{L,R}q_m=-k_m u_{m,x},\qquad m\in\{L,R\}qm​=−km​um,x​,m∈{L,R}

Fourier’s law connects the temperature drawing with the conserved quantity. The subscript m identifies the left or right material. Heat flux is negative conductivity times the local temperature slope.

For our hot-left, cold-right profile, the temperature slope is negative while the rightward heat flux is positive. At the joint, the conductivities differ, so equal flux generally requires different slopes. This is the physical reason for using a material-aware interface condition.

中文讲稿

傅里叶定律把温度图像与守恒量连接起来。下标 m 表示左或右材料。热通量等于负的导热系数乘局部温度斜率。

在左热右冷的曲线中，温度斜率为负，而向右热通量为正。接口两侧导热系数不同，因此相同通量通常需要不同斜率。这就是使用材料相关接口条件的物理原因。

7. Enforce the local equation within each material

−kmum,xx=s-k_m u_{m,xx}=s−km​um,xx​=s

Combining energy balance with Fourier’s law gives this second-order equation within each material. Conductivity is constant inside each open subdomain. We therefore differentiate the local temperature twice and multiply by its own conductivity.

We do not pretend that the discontinuous conductivity is a globally smooth coefficient. The bulk equations apply away from the joint, while separate conditions connect the two traces there. This distinction is central to the domain-decomposition formulation.

中文讲稿

结合能量平衡与傅里叶定律，可得到各材料内部的二阶方程。每个开放子区域内导热系数为常数。因此对局部温度求两次导数，再乘各自的导热系数。

不能把不连续导热系数假装成全局光滑系数。体内方程用于接头以外的位置，另用接口条件连接两侧迹值。这是区域分解形式的关键区别。

8. First prescribe the actual exterior boundaries

uL(0)=1,uR(1)=0u_L(0)=1,\qquad u_R(1)=0uL​(0)=1,uR​(1)=0

The exterior temperatures are one at the left and zero at the right. These are physical boundary conditions for the full rod. They do not prescribe the unknown temperature at the internal joint.

Each local second-order equation still needs information from the neighbouring material. Fixing the exterior values alone leaves the two local problems disconnected. The next two conditions provide the missing physical connection.

中文讲稿

外部温度取左端一、右端零。这是整杆的物理边界条件，不是在规定内部接头的未知温度。

每个局部二阶方程仍需要邻近材料的信息。仅固定外部值，会让两个局部问题彼此断开。下面两个条件提供缺少的物理连接。

9. Perfect contact gives one temperature at the joint

uL(a)=uR(a)u_L(a)=u_R(a)uL​(a)=uR​(a)

Under our perfect-contact assumption, the two temperatures approaching the same joint must agree. U L at a is the left trace, and u R at a is the right trace. Neither is a known training label.

This condition would change if the interface had thermal contact resistance. We are explicitly not modelling that situation here. Temperature agreement also says nothing by itself about whether the two models carry a consistent amount of heat.

中文讲稿

在完美接触假设下，接近同一接头的两侧温度必须一致。u L 在 a 处是左侧迹值，u R 在 a 处是右侧迹值。两者都不是已知训练标签。

如果接口存在热接触热阻，这个条件会改变。这里明确不模拟那种情况。仅有温度一致，也不能保证两个模型传递的热量相符。

10. Without interface storage, heat cannot disappear at the joint

qL(a)=qR(a)q_L(a)=q_R(a)qL​(a)=qR​(a)

Integrate energy balance across an increasingly thin region around the joint. With no concentrated interface source or storage, the incoming and outgoing heat fluxes must agree. This is the conservative connection.

Both q values use the same positive-right orientation. If we used outward normals for the two subdomains, the equivalent condition would be a zero sum of outward fluxes. Matching bare temperature slopes instead would be wrong when the conductivities differ.

中文讲稿

在接头周围越来越薄的区域上积分能量平衡。如果没有集中接口热源或储热，进入与离开的热通量必须一致。这就是守恒连接。

两个 q 都采用向右为正的同一方向。如果改用两子区域各自外法向，等价条件就是外向通量之和为零。导热系数不同时，直接匹配温度斜率反而是错误的。

11. One missing interface law leaves an undetermined degree of freedom

qm(x)=x+Cmq_m(x)=x+C_mqm​(x)=x+Cm​

For the exact local equations, the two interface laws explain why incomplete couplings remain underdetermined. Integrating q x equal to one on the left leaves an unknown constant C L. This shifts the left heat flux while preserving its local conservation equation.

The right half has its own constant C R, with its exterior temperature supplying the other integration condition. A single interface condition relates the two flux constants but leaves one degree of freedom. Using both state and flux conditions fixes their common value in this bonded-rod problem.

中文讲稿

对于精确局部方程，两个接口定律解释了不完整耦合为何仍然欠定。在左侧对 q x 等于一积分，会留下未知常数 C L。它改变左侧热通量的偏移，却仍保留局部守恒方程。

右半段也留下自己的通量常数 C R，外部温度则提供另一条积分条件。一个接口条件只关联两通量常数，仍留下一个自由度。对于这根粘接杆，同时使用状态和通量条件才能确定它们的共同值。

12. Two small networks, one connected physical problem

uL=1+xNL(x),uR=(1−x)NR(x)u_L=1+xN_L(x),\qquad u_R=(1-x)N_R(x)uL​=1+xNL​(x),uR​=(1−x)NR​(x)

N L and N R are two independent trainable network outputs, each with two hidden layers of width sixteen. The displayed factors impose the exterior values exactly. Each network receives its own locally normalised coordinate in the implementation.

The two networks contain six hundred and forty-two trainable parameters in total. The networks learn their interface values through coupling, with flux offsets implicit in the fields rather than added as C parameters. We train them together on one CPU; this is not a demonstration of parallel acceleration.

中文讲稿

N L 与 N R 是两个独立可训练网络输出，各有两个宽度十六的隐层。图中因子精确施加外部边界值。实现中每个网络接收各自局部归一化坐标。

两个网络合计六百四十二个可训练参数。网络通过耦合学习接口值，通量偏移隐含于场中，没有另外加入 C 参数。这里在一个 CPU 上联合训练，不是在演示并行加速。

13. How does average-state coupling express temperature agreement?

uˉ=12(uL(a)+uR(a))\bar u=\tfrac12\bigl(u_L(a)+u_R(a)\bigr)uˉ=21​(uL​(a)+uR​(a))

The original cPINN formulation includes agreement with an average interface state. U bar denotes the average of the two predictions at the joint. If both predictions equal this average, they equal each other.

Summing the two squared distances to the differentiable average gives one half of the squared temperature jump. Our joint objective uses the direct jump with an explicitly chosen weight instead. This is an equivalent state-agreement idea up to scaling, not a claim to reproduce the original loss normalisation.

中文讲稿

原始 cPINN 形式包含与平均接口状态一致的约束。u 横线表示接头两侧预测的平均值。如果两个预测都等于这个平均值，它们也彼此相等。

两侧到可微平均值的平方距离之和，等于温度差平方的一半。联合目标改用明确权重的直接温度差。因此状态衔接思想在缩放意义上等价，但没有声称复现原始损失归一化。

14. First check the physics residual inside each material

Lr=12(⟨rL2⟩+⟨rR2⟩)\mathcal L_r=\tfrac12\bigl(\langle r_L^2\rangle+\langle r_R^2\rangle\bigr)Lr​=21​(⟨rL2​⟩+⟨rR2​⟩)

R m is the local residual, negative conductivity times temperature curvature minus the prescribed source. Angle brackets mean the average over the thirty-two midpoint locations in that material. L r averages the two local mean squared residuals.

No residual location lies on the material interface. More importantly, a small bulk loss cannot detect a missing interface condition by itself. Each subdomain can satisfy its own equation while the assembled rod remains physically inconsistent.

中文讲稿

r m 是局部残差，即负导热系数乘温度曲率再减给定热源。尖括号表示该材料内三十二个中点的平均。L r 再平均两侧的残差均方。

没有残差位置落在材料接口上。更重要的是，较小体内损失本身无法检测缺少的接口条件。各子区域可能满足自身方程，而拼接整杆仍然不符合物理。

15. Add the two interface jumps to supply missing information

L=Lr+γu(Δu)2+γq(Δq)2\mathcal L=\mathcal L_r+\gamma_u(\Delta u)^2+\gamma_q(\Delta q)^2L=Lr​+γu​(Δu)2+γq​(Δq)2

Delta u is the left temperature trace minus the right trace, and delta q is the corresponding heat-flux difference. Gamma u and gamma q are nonnegative penalty weights. The fully coupled run sets both weights to ten in these dimensionless variables.

A finite penalty encourages small jumps; it does not impose exact equality by construction. We will report both remaining jumps rather than declaring exact conservation from the method’s name. The network architecture, points and exterior treatment stay the same in every ablation.

中文讲稿

delta u 是左温度迹值减右迹值，delta q 是相应热通量差。gamma u 与 gamma q 是非负惩罚权重。完整耦合在这些无量纲变量中把两权重都设为十。

有限惩罚促使接口差变小，不是从结构上精确强制相等。要报告两种剩余接口差，而不能因方法名称就宣称精确守恒。每种消融都保持相同网络、点和外部处理。

16. Remove conditions one at a time to expose missing physics

The four runs are uncoupled, temperature agreement only, flux agreement only, and both conditions. Each seed starts all four from the same pair of networks. Only the two interface penalty weights change.

The incomplete variants are diagnostic ablations, not four equally valid physical models of the bonded rod. They reveal why small local residuals are insufficient. We do not need to compare different network sizes or different sampling policies to isolate this question.

中文讲稿

四种运行分别是无耦合、仅温度一致、仅通量一致，以及两条件同时加入。每个种子的四种运行从相同两个网络开始。只改变两个接口惩罚权重。

不完整形式是诊断消融，不是粘接杆的四种同样有效物理模型。它们揭示局部残差小为何仍不充分。为了分离这个问题，不需要比较不同网络大小或不同采样策略。

17. Put the same physical interface into the computation graph

uL, qL = left_trace(a)
uR, qR = right_trace(a)
jump_u = uL - uR
jump_q = qL - qR
loss = bulk + wu*jump_u**2 + wq*jump_q**2
loss.backward(); optimizer.step()

The code evaluates both networks at the same interface coordinate. Each q is computed from its own conductivity and automatic temperature derivative. The two jumps then connect the otherwise separate parameter sets through one joint objective.

Neither interface temperature nor interface flux is supplied as a reference label. Automatic differentiation propagates the coupling through both networks. The displayed code is a short teaching summary; the download contains the complete derivative and training implementation.

中文讲稿

代码在相同接口坐标上求两个网络。每个 q 都来自各自导热系数和自动温度导数。两种接口差随后通过一个联合目标，连接原本分离的参数集合。

接口温度和通量都没有作为参考标签给出。自动微分把耦合传到两个网络中。图中代码是简短教学摘要，下载包包含完整求导和训练实现。

The same saved checkpoints now show q rather than temperature. The analytic reference increases linearly because the rod generates heat internally. It remains continuous across the joint even though the temperature slope changes there.

A state-only penalty does not remove the flux mismatch. A flux-only penalty can reduce that mismatch while leaving an unacceptable temperature discontinuity. The fully coupled run must be checked against both requirements and the independent reference.

24. Separate the implemented idea from the unreproduced benchmarks

The original cPINN paper connects networks through conservative fluxes and average interface states for nonlinear conservation-law examples. We retain that coupling idea in an independently derived two-material steady heat problem. Our exterior hard constraints, fixed tanh, joint optimiser and penalty scaling are explicit changes.

We do not reproduce the paper’s Burgers, Korteweg–de Vries, Navier–Stokes or Euler benchmarks, adaptive activations or distributed execution. We also do not implement XPINNs, which is a distinct extension. This episode isolates one interface mechanism with an analytic solution and a small CPU budget.

没有复现论文的 Burgers、KdV、Navier–Stokes 或 Euler 基准、自适应激活和分布式执行。也没有实现另一种扩展 XPINNs。本篇用解析解和小型 CPU 预算分离一个接口机制。

26. After interfaces, why consider a weak formulation?

Domain decomposition lets us avoid differentiating a material jump as if it were smooth, but each local model still uses pointwise second derivatives. Rougher fields and conservation questions can motivate an alternative way of expressing the equation. That opens a natural discussion of weak or variational formulations.

A future paper presentation can compare integral constraints with the strong residuals used throughout this series. It will need its own test functions, quadrature controls and independent validation. Those choices deserve a separate experiment rather than being silently folded into this cPINN implementation.

后续论文讲解可以比较积分约束与本系列一直使用的强残差。它需要自己的测试函数、积分精度对照和独立验证。这些选择值得单独实验，不能悄悄混入当前 cPINN 实现。
