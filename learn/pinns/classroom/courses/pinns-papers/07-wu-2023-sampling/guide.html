1. Paper 07: Residual-adaptive sampling and boundary layers

The gPINN paper added information at each location, so this presentation asks where a limited set of locations should be placed. We will connect a steady thermal boundary layer to fixed, uniformly replaced and residual-adaptive point sets. Finally, twelve CPU runs will expose point histories, candidate-search costs, independent errors and cases where adaptation loses.

中文讲稿

gPINN 论文在每个位置增加信息，本篇追问有限位置应当放在哪里。先把稳态热边界层连接到固定、均匀替换与残差自适应点集。最后用十二次 CPU 运行展示点历史、候选搜索成本、独立误差，以及自适应不占优的情况。

2. From information at each point to the locations of points

The gPINN presentation changed what is enforced at a location, but the locations themselves stayed fixed. A limited CPU budget also makes it important to ask where those locations should be. This paper studies sampling rather than adding another derivative penalty.

We will keep the active training-point count fixed while comparing several placement policies. A boundary-layer problem gives the locations a clear physical meaning. The experiment will also retain cases where residual-based adaptation performs worse than a simple fixed grid.

中文讲稿

gPINN 一讲改变了每个位置约束什么信息，但位置本身保持固定。有限 CPU 预算也让我们必须追问这些位置应当放在哪里。这篇研究采样，而不是再加入导数惩罚。

保持活动训练点总数固定，比较几种位置策略。边界层问题让位置具有清楚的物理意义。也会保留基于残差的自适应比简单固定网格更差的情况。

3. First look at the thin layer near a hot outlet

Imagine a cold fluid moving to the right through a channel whose outlet is held warm. Advection carries heat along the flow, while diffusion spreads it against the sharp temperature variation. In steady balance, most of the temperature change can remain close to the hot end.

The gold curve and colour represent steady temperature u along position x. Moving markers only illustrate the flow direction; they are not a numerical transient simulation. The narrow changing region makes uniform and nonuniform point placement worth comparing.

中文讲稿

想象冷流体沿通道向右流动，出口保持较高温度。对流沿流向携带热量，扩散则抹平尖锐温度变化。达到稳态平衡后，大部分温度变化可能集中在热端附近。

金线与颜色表示沿位置 x 的稳态温度 u。移动标记只示意流向，不是数值瞬态模拟。这个狭窄变化区域，让均匀与非均匀选点的比较有了意义。

4. Describe the picture with steady advection-diffusion

ux−νuxx=0,x∈[0,1]u_x-\nu u_{xx}=0,\qquad x\in[0,1]ux​−νuxx​=0,x∈[0,1]

The steady physical picture becomes an equation with advection speed normalised to one. U is temperature, x is position, and nu is a known positive diffusivity. The first derivative describes advection and the second derivative describes diffusion.

We choose nu equal to zero point zero five. Smaller diffusivity creates a thinner transition near the warm outlet. This analytic one-dimensional problem is our teaching adaptation, not a reproduction of the original paper’s six benchmark problems.

中文讲稿

把对流速度归一化为一后，稳态物理图像变成这个方程。u 是温度，x 是位置，nu 是已知正扩散系数。一阶导数描述对流，二阶导数描述扩散。

选择 nu 等于零点零五。扩散越小，热出口附近的过渡越薄。这个可解析的一维问题是教学改编，不是原论文六个基准问题的复现。

5. How do endpoint temperatures select a boundary layer?

u(0)=0,u(1)=1u(0)=0,\qquad u(1)=1u(0)=0,u(1)=1

The differential equation alone needs boundary values to determine a temperature profile. We hold the left inlet at zero and the right outlet at one. Their imposed difference drives the steady layer in this normalised problem.

A network can satisfy these endpoint values and still predict the interior badly. That is why we evaluate the entire profile rather than celebrating boundary agreement alone. The hard endpoint treatment is identical across every sampling policy.

中文讲稿

微分方程需要边界值，才能确定温度曲线。左入口固定为零，右出口固定为一。在这个归一化问题中，两端给定差异产生稳态薄层。

网络即使满足端点值，内部也仍可能预测错误。因此要评估整条曲线，不能只因边界一致就认为成功。每种采样策略都使用相同硬端点处理。

6. Use an analytic exponential profile for validation

u∗(x)=ex/ν−1e1/ν−1u_*(x)=\frac{e^{x/\nu}-1}{e^{1/\nu}-1}u∗​(x)=e1/ν−1ex/ν−1​

This boundary-value problem has an exact exponential solution, so no large simulator is needed. U star denotes the analytic temperature reference. Its rapid variation near the right end explains where the physical difficulty is concentrated.

The implementation evaluates an algebraically equivalent expression with negative exponentials for numerical stability. Reference temperatures and slopes are only used for independent validation. They never determine the resampling probabilities or become interior training labels.

中文讲稿

这个边值问题有精确指数解，因此不需要大型模拟器。u 星表示解析温度参考。右端附近的快速变化，解释了物理困难集中在哪里。

实现使用代数等价的负指数表达式提高数值稳定性。参考温度与斜率只用于独立验证，既不决定重采样概率，也不作为内部训练标签。

7. Fix the endpoints by construction before comparing points

uθ=x+x(1−x)Nθ(x)u_\theta=x+x(1-x)N_\theta(x)uθ​=x+x(1−x)Nθ​(x)

The boundary conditions are built into the same surrogate for every policy. N theta is the trainable network output and theta denotes its parameters. The correction term vanishes at zero and one.

This makes endpoint agreement exact without using a boundary-loss weight. Sampling only changes which interior residual locations enter the objective. We use ordinary PINN residuals here, without the gPINN derivative penalty from the previous paper.

中文讲稿

所有策略都把边界条件写入相同替代表达式。N theta 是可训练网络输出，theta 表示网络参数。修正项在零与一处消失。

这样不需要边界损失权重，也能精确满足端点。采样只改变哪些内部残差位置进入目标。这里使用普通 PINN 残差，不加入上一篇的 gPINN 导数惩罚。

8. How do fixed grids and fixed random points differ?

We begin with two controls that never move their training locations. The grid uses thirty-two equally spaced midpoint locations. The random control draws thirty-two distinct locations from a fixed pool of five hundred and twelve candidates.

Our random control is therefore discrete-pool sampling rather than continuous uniform sampling from the original paper. It shares its initial points with the two resampling policies for each seed. The grid deliberately uses a different, deterministic placement while sharing network initialisation.

中文讲稿

先建立两个训练位置不移动的对照。网格使用三十二个等距中点。随机对照从五百一十二个固定候选位置中抽取三十二个不同位置。

因此，这里的随机对照是离散候选池采样，不是原论文的连续均匀采样。每个种子下，它与两种重采样策略共享初始点。网格则有意使用不同的确定位置，但共享网络初始化。

9. Keep 32 points but replace them periodically

The next control asks what changes simply by revisiting different locations. After each five hundred completed updates, uniform resampling draws a fresh set of thirty-two candidate positions. It replaces the previous set rather than growing it.

Network parameters and Adam moments are retained when the points change. This differs from the explicit optimiser resets in the coefficient-curriculum experiment. Across training, the policy sees more distinct locations even though its active batch size remains unchanged.

中文讲稿

下一个对照检验，仅仅访问不同位置会带来什么变化。每完成五百次更新，均匀重采样重新抽取三十二个候选位置。它替换旧集合，而不是不断增加点数。

点变化时保留网络参数与 Adam 动量。这不同于系数课程实验中明确进行的优化器重置。虽然活动批大小不变，训练过程中看到的不同位置会更多。

10. Which signal tells RAD a candidate is important?

ρj=∣rθ(xj)∣\rho_j=|r_\theta(x_j)|ρj​=∣rθ​(xj​)∣

Uniform replacement does not inspect the current physics mismatch, so RAD adds that information. X subscript j is a candidate location, and rho j is the absolute residual produced by the current network there. The residual is u x minus nu times u x x.

The paper uses epsilon for this magnitude, but we use rho to avoid confusing it with the previous presentation’s causality parameter. Rho is an error indicator, not the unknown true temperature error. An inaccurate indicator or an incomplete candidate pool can still misdirect the sampling.

中文讲稿

均匀替换不检查当前物理失配，因此 RAD 加入这类信息。x 下标 j 是候选位置，rho j 是当前网络在该处的残差绝对值。残差是 u x 减 nu 乘 u x x。

论文用 epsilon 表示这个幅值，这里改用 rho，避免与上一讲因果参数混淆。rho 是误差指标，不是真实温度误差。不准确的指标或不充分的候选池，仍可能使采样方向失准。

11. Turn residual magnitudes into nonnegative scores

The indicator is converted into a sampling score before drawing points. The exponent k controls how strongly large residuals are emphasised. The bar means the average powered residual over the candidate pool.

The nonnegative constant c adds background coverage to that score. We use the paper’s suggested default k equal to one and c equal to one. Those are stated hyperparameters rather than an assertion that the default is optimal for our boundary-layer adaptation.

抽点之前，先把指标转换成采样得分。指数 k 控制对大残差的强调程度。横线表示在候选池上对残差幂取平均。

非负常数 c 为得分增加背景覆盖。采用论文建议的默认值，即 k 为一、c 为一。这是明确的超参数，不代表默认值在这个边界层改编中最优。

12. Normalise scores into sampling probabilities

pj=aj∑m=1Mamp_j=\frac{a_j}{\sum_{m=1}^{M}a_m}pj​=∑m=1M​am​aj​​

A score is not yet a probability, so we divide it by the sum of all candidate scores. M is the number of candidates, here five hundred and twelve. The probabilities therefore add to one before the drawing operation.

We select thirty-two distinct candidates without replacement. These are proposal weights, not a claim that each point’s final inclusion chance is exactly thirty-two times p j. The finite-pool drawing convention is part of the implementation and is recorded explicitly.

中文讲稿

得分还不是概率，因此要除以全部候选得分之和。M 是候选点数，这里为五百一十二。因此在开始抽样前，概率之和为一。

采用不放回方式选择三十二个不同候选点。这些是提议权重，并不表示每点最终纳入概率恰好等于三十二乘 p j。有限池的抽样约定属于实现的一部分，会明确记录。

13. How does the default balance focus and coverage?

pj=12M+ρj2∑mρmp_j=\frac1{2M}+\frac{\rho_j}{2\sum_m\rho_m}pj​=2M1​+2∑m​ρm​ρj​​

For k and c both equal to one, the normalised proposal has a useful interpretation when total residual magnitude is positive. Half is a uniform background over candidates. The other half is proportional to the candidate residual magnitudes.

This gives every candidate a positive proposal weight without guaranteeing that every spatial region appears in one small sample. If all residuals are zero, the implementation falls back to uniform weights. The numerical denominator floor is also disclosed in the code.

中文讲稿

当 k 与 c 都为一，且残差幅值总和为正时，归一化提议有直观解释。一半是候选点上的均匀背景，另一半与候选残差幅值成正比。

这让每个候选点都有正的提议权重，却不能保证一次小样本覆盖每个空间区域。如果全部残差为零，实现退回均匀权重。代码也明确记录数值分母下限。

14. The code selects points using the current residual only

score = abs(r)/mean(abs(r)).clamp_min(1e-14) + 1
prob = score / score.sum()
idx = multinomial(prob, 32, replacement=False)
points = candidates[idx]

The proposal rule now connects directly to the selection code. Residual magnitudes are detached from the training graph before they are converted into scores and probabilities. The selected candidate coordinates become the next active point set.

There is no lookup of the analytic solution in these four lines. The following optimiser updates minimise the ordinary mean squared residual at the selected points. We do not apply an inverse-probability correction, so adaptive sampling also changes the empirical weighting of space.

中文讲稿

提议规则现在直接连接到选点代码。残差幅值先脱离训练计算图，再转换成得分与概率。选出的候选坐标成为下一组活动点。

四行代码没有查询解析解。随后的优化更新在选中位置最小化普通残差均方。没有做逆概率校正，因此自适应采样也改变了空间上的经验加权。

23. Why should this result not be extrapolated directly?

The original study compared ten sampling methods across multiple forward and inverse PDEs, while this episode isolates four policies on a different stationary problem. Our network, finite candidate pool, resampling interval and update budget are explicitly smaller choices. The local failures do not invalidate the paper’s original benchmark results.

They do show why residual magnitude is not an oracle for true solution error. Optimisation, finite candidate coverage and sampling-induced spatial weighting can all matter, but we have not isolated their individual causal contributions here. That calls for targeted follow-up experiments rather than an invented success narrative.

原研究在多个正反问题上比较十种采样方法，本篇只在另一个稳态问题上分离比较四种策略。网络、有限候选池、重采样间隔和更新预算都明确缩小。本地失败不能否定论文原始基准结果。

25. Why study how neighbouring regions are connected next?

Sampling changes where one model is tested, but some physical problems contain regions with different material properties. Simply placing more points does not specify how those regions exchange conserved quantities. That introduces a separate reason to study domain decomposition and interface conditions.

The next presentation examines a conservative domain-decomposition idea through a small composite-medium problem. It explains both state agreement and flux balance at an interface. This adds a new physical mechanism rather than treating adaptive sampling as a replacement for every modelling decision.
