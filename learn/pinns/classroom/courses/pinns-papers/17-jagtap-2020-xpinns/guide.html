1. Paper17: XPINNs and time-slab interfaces

First watch a cooling rod and split its time domain between two networks. Then connect average-solution and residual interface losses,including a counterexample to residual matching alone. Finally replay every CPU control,verify the handover and motivate adaptive pointwise weights.

中文讲稿

先观察冷却杆，把时间区域分给两个网络。再连接平均解值与残差接口损失，并给出只匹配残差不足的反例。最后回放全部 CPU 对照，验证交接，再引出逐点自适应权重。

2. From designing a rod to following its evolution in pieces

The design lesson checked whether a neural field described the actual physical system. Now ask a related question: what happens when several networks describe different parts of one evolving system?

XPINNs allow decomposition in space and time. We will use two time intervals and inspect the interface, keeping the entire experiment small enough for a CPU.

中文讲稿

设计这一讲检查神经场是否描述了真实物理。现在换一个相关问题：如果同一演化系统的不同部分由多个网络描述，会怎样？

XPINNs 允许在空间和时间上分区。我们采用两个时间区间，仔细检查交接接口，并让整个实验保持在小型 CPU 预算内。

3. First watch the same rod cool

This rod starts with a smooth warm hump. U is temperature, x is position, and t is time. Both ends stay at zero.

Watch heat leave through the cold ends. The curve comes from the exact heat-equation solution. It is a physical reference, not a trained prediction.

中文讲稿

这根杆最初有一个平滑的温度峰。u 是温度，x 是位置，t 是时间。两端温度保持为零。

看热量从冷端离开。这条曲线来自热方程的精确解，是物理参考，不是训练预测。

4. The physics stays the same across the partition

ut=αuxx,α=0.1u_t=\alpha u_{xx},\quad\alpha=0.1ut​=αuxx​,α=0.1

Alpha is the known diffusivity, here zero point one. Subscripts t and x x mean a time derivative and a second spatial derivative.

The initial profile is sine pi x. Its exact amplitude decays exponentially, and we reserve that full solution for validation. We do not train on interior temperature labels.

中文讲稿

α 是已知扩散率，这里为零点一。下标 t 与 x x 分别表示时间导数与空间二阶导数。

初始形状是 sin πx，精确振幅按指数衰减。完整解析解只用于验证，训练没有内部温度标签。

5. Split the model in time,not the material

The lower slab covers time zero to one half. The upper slab covers one half to one. Each gets its own network.

The pink line marks physical time. Is there a real material seam at the dividing line? No; the interface is how we organise the computation.

中文讲稿

下方时间片覆盖零到二分之一，上方覆盖二分之一到一。每片分配一个独立网络。

粉线表示物理时间。分界线那里，材料真的有接缝吗？没有，接口只是我们组织计算的方式。

6. Two networks need a reliable temperature handover

The first network receives the given initial profile at time zero. The second network does not receive the exact solution at the interface.

So where does its starting temperature come from? It must be transmitted by coupling the two predictions. Without that link, the later equation has missing initial information.

中文讲稿

第一个网络接收零时刻给定的初始形状。第二个网络不会得到接口处的精确解标签。

那它的起始温度从哪里来？必须通过耦合两个预测来传递。缺少这条联系，后半段方程就缺少初始信息。

7. Hard spatial boundaries do not replace the time interface

u1=u0+tx(1−x)N1,u2=x(1−x)N2u_1=u_0+t x(1-x)N_1,\quad u_2=x(1-x)N_2u1​=u0​+tx(1−x)N1​,u2​=x(1−x)N2​

U zero is the supplied initial profile, and N one and N two are separate trainable networks. Both fields have exact spatial endpoints. The first also has the exact initial profile.

But nothing here forces the second field to equal the first at time one half. This is the same lesson as before: a hard constraint guarantees only what we actually construct.

中文讲稿

u 零是给定初始形状，N 一和 N 二是独立待训练网络。两者空间端点准确，第一个还精确满足初始形状。

但这个构造没有强迫第二个场在二分之一时刻等于第一个场。和上篇一样，硬约束只保证我们实际写入构造的条件。

8. First make the two interface traces agree

uˉ=u1+u22\bar u=\frac{u_1+u_2}{2}uˉ=2u1​+u2​​

At the same interface location, we have two temperature predictions. U bar is their average, not an extra measurement and not the exact temperature.

Each prediction is penalised for differing from that average. The average moves with the networks, so the coupling transmits information without supplying an exact interface label.

中文讲稿

同一个接口位置，有两个温度预测。u 上横线是两者平均值，不是新增测量，也不是精确温度。

两个预测都因偏离平均值而受罚。平均值随网络一起变化，因此耦合能够传递信息，却没有提供精确接口标签。

9. Why does the average penalty reduce a jump?

(u1−uˉ)2+(u2−uˉ)2=12(u1−u2)2(u_1-\bar u)^2+(u_2-\bar u)^2=\tfrac12(u_1-u_2)^2(u1​−uˉ)2+(u2​−uˉ)2=21​(u1​−u2​)2

Substitute the average into both terms. The two deviations are equal in magnitude and opposite in sign. Their squares add to half the squared jump.

This identity also fixes the loss scaling. Our implementation differentiates through the shared average and updates both networks jointly; it does not claim a detached-neighbour parallel algorithm.

中文讲稿

把平均值代入两项。两边偏差大小相同、符号相反，平方相加就是跳变平方的一半。

这个恒等式也明确了损失尺度。实现对共同平均值求导，并联合更新两个网络，不声称实现了邻居梯度分离的并行算法。

10. Then compare the equation residuals on both sides

rq=uq,t−αuq,xx,LR=⟨(r1−r2)2⟩Ir_q=u_{q,t}-\alpha u_{q,xx},\quad L_R=\langle(r_1-r_2)^2\rangle_Irq​=uq,t​−αuq,xx​,LR​=⟨(r1​−r2​)2⟩I​

R q is the heat-equation residual from network q. The angle brackets average over sampled interface positions. L R penalises the difference between the two residuals.

Matching residuals is different from matching temperatures. XPINNs combine these interface ideas; whether the extra term helps this tiny optimisation is something we must measure.

中文讲稿

r q 是第 q 个网络的热方程残差。尖括号表示在采样接口位置上求平均，L R 惩罚两侧残差差值。

残差相同，不等于温度相同。XPINNs 组合这些接口思路；额外项是否改善这个小优化问题，需要实际测量。

11. Two zero residuals can still hide different temperatures

u1=sin⁡(πx)e−0.1π2t,u2=0u_1=\sin(\pi x)e^{-0.1\pi^2t},\quad u_2=0u1​=sin(πx)e−0.1π2t,u2​=0

Consider this analytic counterexample. The true cooling field and the identically zero field both satisfy the homogeneous heat equation.

Their residual difference is therefore zero. Yet their temperatures disagree at the interface. Residual continuity alone cannot manufacture the missing initial state.

中文讲稿

看这个解析反例。真实冷却场与恒为零的场，都满足齐次热方程。

因此两者残差差值为零，接口温度却不同。只匹配残差，无法制造缺失的初始状态。

12. Differentiate local time coordinates correctly

zt,1=4t−1,zt,2=4t−3z_{t,1}=4t-1,\quad z_{t,2}=4t-3zt,1​=4t−1,zt,2​=4t−3

Each half-interval is rescaled into its own minus-one-to-one time coordinate. The factor four comes from the half-unit interval length.

Our residual differentiates with respect to physical x and t. Automatic differentiation carries the coordinate factors through the network. Dropping them would change the equation.

中文讲稿

每个半区间都缩放到自己的负一到一时间坐标。因子四来自长度为二分之一的时间片。

残差仍然对物理 x 与 t 求导。自动微分把坐标因子带过网络；漏掉它们，就改变了方程。

13. Interface losses connect the two differentiable networks

u1, r1 = early_net(interface)
u2, r2 = late_net(interface)
shared = 0.5*(u1 + u2)
value_loss = mse(u1,shared) + mse(u2,shared)
loss = pde_loss + 50*value_loss + mse(r1,r2)

Evaluate both networks at the same interface positions. The shared value depends on both predictions, and both receive gradients from the coupling loss.

The final residual-matching term is present only in the XPINN control. The value-only control omits it, and the uncoupled control omits both interface terms.

中文讲稿

在同一批接口位置评估两个网络。共同值依赖两边预测，两边都从耦合损失接收梯度。

最后的残差匹配项只出现在 XPINN 对照中。仅解值对照删去这一项，不耦合对照则删去全部接口项。

14. Four settings,three starts and the same update budget

Every setting uses two thousand Adam updates and the same ninety-six interior points. The decomposed controls also share sixteen interface positions. There are three random starting identifiers: eleven, twenty-two and thirty-three.

The global network has one hundred and ninety-three parameters; the two smaller networks have one hundred and ninety-four combined. This is approximately matched capacity, not an exactly identical architecture or a parallel timing benchmark.

中文讲稿

每种设置都使用两千步 Adam 更新与相同的九十六个内部点。分区对照还共享十六个接口位置。随机起点编号为十一、二十二与三十三。

全局网络有一百九十三个参数，两个小网络合计一百九十四个。这是近似匹配容量，不是完全相同架构，也不是并行耗时基准。

17. Keep every start rather than selecting the best curve

Each group contains all three final field errors on a logarithmic scale. We compare fields on an independent two-hundred-and-one by one-hundred-and-one space-time grid, not just the interface values.

Coupling greatly improves the uncoupled controls. Residual matching does not improve every value-only run, and the global baseline is more accurate here. Decomposition is a useful mechanism, not a universal ranking advantage.

中文讲稿

每组在对数轴上保留全部三个最终场误差。在独立的二百零一乘一百零一时空网格上比较场，不只是接口数值。

耦合大幅改善不耦合对照。但残差匹配没有改善每一次仅解值运行，这里的全局基线还更准确。分区是一种有用机制，不是普遍排名优势。

18. Check jumps,residuals and field accuracy separately

A small interface jump checks agreement between networks. A small residual checks a differential equation at the evaluation points. Neither alone gives the full temperature error.

We retain all those quantities and evaluate residuals at seven hundred and seventy-eight held-out positions. The saved initial and final parameters let the independent verification script recompute the reported fields.

中文讲稿

小接口跳变检查网络之间是否一致，小残差检查评估点上的微分方程。任何一个单独指标都不等于完整温度误差。

我们保留全部指标，并在七百七十八个未训练位置检查残差。保存的初始与最终参数，让独立验证脚本能够重新计算报告的场。

19. A time interface is different from a spatial material interface

Here the interface cuts time. Matching the temperature trace supplies the later heat problem with its starting state. We did not introduce a new material boundary.

A spatial split can require additional conditions, such as heat-flux continuity. The original paper allows extra interface conditions; residual matching alone is not a substitute for deciding what the physical problem requires.

中文讲稿

这里的接口切开时间。匹配温度迹线，为后半段热问题提供起始状态，没有引入新材料边界。

空间划分可能还需要额外条件，例如热通量连续。原论文允许增加接口条件；残差匹配不能替代对物理问题所需条件的判断。

20. This experiment tests the handover mechanism,not large-scale parallelism

We use two time slabs, one-dimensional diffusion and a common joint optimiser. All runs use one CPU thread and finish within the stated cap.

The original framework allows richer space-time partitions and different networks in different regions. This small example does not establish its scalability, parallel speedup or a guarantee of better accuracy than a global model.

中文讲稿

这里采用两个时间片、一维扩散与共同联合优化器。所有运行使用一个 CPU 线程，并在规定上限内完成。

原框架允许更丰富的时空分区，以及不同区域采用不同网络。这个小示例不证明扩展性、并行加速，也不保证比全局模型更准确。

21. With only residual matching,can the later field be zero?

r1=r2=0  ⇏  u1=u2r_1=r_2=0\;\not\Rightarrow\;u_1=u_2r1​=r2​=0⇒u1​=u2​

Pause and use our analytic counterexample. The zero field satisfies the later homogeneous heat equation, as does the correctly cooling field.

Their residuals can match perfectly while their interface temperatures disagree. Add the missing state information, then check the entire field. More optimisation cannot replace an absent constraint.

中文讲稿

停一下，用刚才的解析反例来判断。零场满足后半段齐次热方程，正确冷却场也满足。

两者残差可以完全匹配，接口温度却不同。需要补上缺失状态信息，再检查完整场。更多优化不能替代缺少的约束。

22. Next:give difficult locations more training attention

The interface example used fixed loss weights, including a chosen value-coupling coefficient. But different locations may remain difficult during training. Should every point always receive the same emphasis?

The next paper introduces self-adaptive PINNs with trainable pointwise weights. We will distinguish changing a point’s weight from moving the point, and test the resulting min-max optimisation on a small CPU problem.

中文讲稿

接口例子使用固定损失权重，包括选定的解值耦合系数。但训练中，不同位置的难度可能不同。每个点是否应该一直获得相同强调？

下一篇介绍具有可训练逐点权重的自适应 PINNs。我们将区分改变点的权重与移动点的位置，并在小型 CPU 问题上检验相应的极小极大优化。

Teaching checks and reference answers

Ask for a prediction before running the exercise. Analytic expectations do not guarantee an optimiser outcome.

Can matching two zero residuals supply the missing temperature state?

No. The exact cooling solution and the identically zero field both solve the homogeneous heat equation and satisfy zero spatial endpoints. Their residuals and residual difference vanish,yet they disagree at the time interface. The later initial-value problem still needs its starting temperature trace. Value coupling provides that missing link without inserting exact interface labels. Finite sampled penalties must still be checked across the field;residual matching is not a general substitute for physical interface conditions.

r1=r2=0⇏u1=u2r_1=r_2=0\not\Rightarrow u_1=u_2r1​=r2​=0⇒u1​=u2​

匹配两个零残差，能提供缺失的温度状态吗？

不能。精确冷却解与恒零场都满足齐次热方程和零空间端点。两者残差及残差差值都为零，但在时间接口上不一致。后半段初值问题仍需要起始温度迹线。解值耦合提供这条缺失联系，不必插入精确接口标签。有限采样惩罚仍要在完整场上验证，残差匹配也不能普遍替代物理接口条件。
