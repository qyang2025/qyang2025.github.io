1. Paper 04: Transport and curriculum training

The gradient paper examined competing update signals, so we now ask how the difficulty of the equation affects the training path. First we will connect periodic transport, its analytic solution and a coefficient curriculum. Finally, paired CPU runs will compare direct training, equal-total-step continuation and an explicitly larger-budget diagnostic.

中文讲稿

梯度论文检查了相互竞争的更新信号，现在追问方程难度怎样影响训练路径。先连接周期输运、解析解和系数课程。最后用配对 CPU 运行比较直接训练、相同总步数延续，以及明确增加预算的诊断。

2. From update signals to problem difficulty

The previous presentation measured competing gradients and showed why a balancing rule must be checked rather than trusted automatically. A different question remains: should a difficult physical problem be learned immediately, or approached through easier ones. This paper studies that organisation of training.

The second box introduces coefficient continuation, sometimes called curriculum regularisation. We first solve a slower transport problem and carry the learned network into faster transport problems. We will also count the training steps carefully, because easier warm-up stages consume part of the budget.

中文讲稿

上一讲测量了相互竞争的梯度，并说明平衡规则需要检验，不能自动信任。还剩另一个问题：困难物理问题应当直接学习，还是先经过较容易的问题？这篇论文讨论训练任务的组织方式。

第二个框引入系数延续，也叫课程式正则化。先求解较慢的输运问题，再把已学习网络带入较快问题。同时仔细计算训练步数，因为容易阶段的预热也消耗预算。

3. First watch translation on a periodic domain

Before writing the transport equation, imagine a concentration pattern moving around a circular conveyor. Its height and shape remain unchanged as the whole pattern translates. The animation shows exact translation as time t increases, including wrap-around at the interval ends.

Gold is the current concentration u, and the dashed curve is its initial profile. The pink marker follows a crest rather than a fixed location. Later we will use that moving marker to explain why a particular combination of time and space derivatives vanishes.

中文讲稿

在写输运方程前，想象一个浓度分布沿环形传送带运动。整个图形平移时，高度和形状都不改变。动画展示随时间 t 增加的精确平移，包括区间两端的周期回绕。

金线表示当前浓度 u，虚线表示初始分布。粉色标记跟随一个波峰，而不是固定在某处。后面将用这个移动标记解释，为什么时间导数与空间导数的某个组合等于零。

4. Turn the moving picture into a transport equation

ut+βux=0u_t+\beta u_x=0ut​+βux​=0

The moving pattern gives meaning to the equation. U is the transported concentration, x is position, t is time, and beta is a known positive transport speed. U t measures change at a fixed position, while u x measures spatial slope.

The two effects cancel for pure translation at speed beta. There is no diffusion term in this model, so the pattern does not flatten. We vary the known speed to change the learning task, rather than trying to identify it from observations as in Part II.

中文讲稿

移动图像赋予方程具体含义。u 是被输运浓度，x 是位置，t 是时间，beta 是已知正输运速度。u t 描述固定位置的变化，u x 描述空间斜率。

以 beta 速度纯平移时，两种效应相互抵消。这个模型没有扩散项，因此分布不会变平。我们改变已知速度以调整学习任务，而不是像第二篇那样从观测中识别速度。

5. Follow a crest to connect the picture and derivatives

ddtu(x0+βt,t)=ut+βux=0\frac{d}{dt}u(x_0+\beta t,t)=u_t+\beta u_x=0dtd​u(x0​+βt,t)=ut​+βux​=0

The transport law becomes especially clear when we follow a moving point. X zero is its initial position, and its path is x zero plus beta times time. The vertical axis here is physical time and the horizontal axis is periodic position.

The chain rule says that the change measured along this path is u t plus beta times u x. A crest keeps the same concentration along that path, so the combination is zero. A path reappearing at the left edge is periodic wrap-around, not a jump across the interior.

中文讲稿

跟随移动点时，输运规律尤其直观。x 零表示初始位置，路径为 x 零加 beta 乘时间。这里纵轴是物理时间，横轴是周期位置。

链式法则说明沿路径测得的变化等于 u t 加 beta 倍 u x。波峰沿路径保持同一浓度，所以这个组合为零。路径从左边重新出现表示周期回绕，不是在内部跳跃。

6. What information does the initial pattern supply?

u(x,0)=sin⁡xu(x,0)=\sin xu(x,0)=sinx

The equation transports a pattern, but we still need to specify which pattern. At time zero, the prescribed concentration is sine of x. We provide forty-eight samples of that initial profile to the network.

We do not supply interior solution labels at later times. Instead, the residual constrains how the initial information moves. This distinction matters when we later evaluate a prediction far from the labelled initial line.

中文讲稿

方程会输运一个分布，但还需要指定是什么分布。零时刻给定浓度为 x 的正弦。向网络提供这条初始曲线上的四十八个样本。

不会提供后续内部时刻的解值标签。残差负责约束初始信息怎样移动。这一区别在检验远离有标签初始线的预测时很重要。

7. Why must the two interval ends connect?

u(0,t)=u(2π,t)u(0,t)=u(2\pi,t)u(0,t)=u(2π,t)

The conveyor picture requires a periodic boundary rather than two independent endpoint values. We display one period from zero to two pi. At a given time the concentrations at those endpoints must agree.

The training objective compares thirty-two matched pairs of boundary times. The chosen first-order transport model uses value periodicity; we do not add a separate derivative-periodicity penalty. These are soft constraints, so their final mismatch must still be measured.

中文讲稿

传送带图像要求周期边界，而不是两个独立端点值。显示的是零到二 pi 的一个周期。同一时刻两端浓度必须相等。

训练目标比较三十二对相同边界时刻。这里的一阶输运模型使用数值周期性，不另加导数周期性惩罚。这些都是软约束，因此仍需测量最终失配。

8. How does analytic translation provide validation?

u∗(x,t)=sin⁡(x−βt)u_*(x,t)=\sin(x-\beta t)u∗​(x,t)=sin(x−βt)

The periodic problem has a simple analytic solution, so no large simulation is necessary. U star denotes this reference solution. Its phase is position minus speed times time, expressing the same translation we watched in the animation.

We differentiate this formula independently to check the residual sign and use it on a separate space-time grid. Reference values inside the domain are never supplied as training labels. Their role is to reveal prediction error rather than make the optimiser look successful.

中文讲稿

周期问题有简单解析解，因此无需大规模模拟。u 星表示参考解。相位是位置减速度乘时间，表达的正是动画中的平移。

独立对这个公式求导以检查残差符号，并在单独的时空网格上使用它。内部参考解值从不作为训练标签。它们用于揭示预测误差，而不是让优化器显得成功。

9. Which three requirements form the objective?

L=L0+Lb+Lr\mathcal L=\mathcal L_0+\mathcal L_b+\mathcal L_rL=L0​+Lb​+Lr​

The physical and data requirements now become one network objective. L zero is initial-profile mismatch, L b is paired periodic-boundary mismatch, and L r is the squared transport residual. Each term is an average over its own point set.

All three terms have unit weight in this experiment. We keep that choice fixed across methods to isolate the training schedule. This does not claim that unit weighting is the best choice for every transport speed or equation.

中文讲稿

现在把物理与数据要求写成同一网络目标。L 零是初始曲线失配，L b 是配对周期边界失配，L r 是输运残差的平方。每项都在自己的点集上取平均。

本实验三项权重均为一。各方法保持这个选择不变，以单独检查训练日程。这不表示单位权重对所有输运速度或方程都是最优选择。

10. What changes when transport becomes faster?

u∗,t=−βcos⁡(x−βt)u_{*,t}=-\beta\cos(x-\beta t)u∗,t​=−βcos(x−βt)

The reference solution shows why increasing beta can change the task without adding more parameters. During the same time interval, the pattern travels further around the periodic domain. A fixed location therefore sees faster temporal variation, as the derivative shown here has amplitude beta.

This is a reason to investigate optimisation difficulty, not a proof that a particular speed must defeat every network. Our target is beta ten, which is smaller than some headline failure examples in the paper. The actual errors, rather than the paper’s reported errors, determine what we can conclude.

中文讲稿

参考解说明了为何增大 beta 即使不增加参数，也会改变任务。在同一时间区间，分布沿周期区域传播更远。因此固定位置观察到更快的时间变化，显示的时间导数振幅就是 beta。

这是研究优化困难的理由，不是证明某个速度一定能让所有网络失败。本实验目标为 beta 十，小于论文某些主要失效案例。结论要由实际误差决定，不能照搬论文报告的误差。

11. Learn slower transport before faster transport

β:1⟶3⟶6⟶10\beta:1\longrightarrow3\longrightarrow6\longrightarrow10β:1⟶3⟶6⟶10

The varying difficulty motivates a coefficient curriculum. We train at speeds one, three, six and ten in that order. The network weights at the end of one stage initialise the next stage, so learning is transferred rather than restarted randomly.

The physical time interval stays from zero to one throughout every stage. This is continuation across equations with different known coefficients. It is not sequence-to-sequence time marching, and it is not the temporal residual weighting introduced in the next paper.

中文讲稿

变化的难度引出系数课程。按一、三、六、十的顺序训练速度。前一阶段末尾的网络权重初始化下一阶段，因此迁移了学习结果，而不是重新随机开始。

每个阶段的物理时间区间始终是零到一。这是在不同已知系数的方程之间做延续，不是逐时间段推进，也不是下一篇中的时间残差加权。

12. How is the direct-training control defined?

A curriculum needs a control that makes its differences explicit. Direct training uses beta ten in all four stages. It receives the same initial network, residual points, initial labels and periodic pairs as the curriculum for each seed.

We reset Adam at each stage boundary in both policies while preserving network weights. That matching reset schedule removes one avoidable difference in optimiser state handling. The remaining deliberate difference is which equation is trained during the first three stages.

中文讲稿

课程式方法需要一个差别清楚的对照。直接训练四个阶段都使用 beta 十。对于每个种子，它与课程式方法使用相同初始网络、残差点、初始标签和周期配对。

两种策略都在阶段边界重置 Adam，同时保留网络权重。相同重置日程去掉了一个本可避免的优化器状态差异。剩下的有意差别，是前三阶段训练哪个方程。

13. Equal total steps do not mean equal target training

4000=4×1000,Ntarget∈{1000,4000}4000=4\times1000,\qquad N_{\rm target}\in\{1000,4000\}4000=4×1000,Ntarget​∈{1000,4000}

The control makes an important budget distinction visible. With four thousand total updates, the curriculum spends only its final thousand updates at beta ten. Direct training spends all four thousand updates on that final target.

Equal total steps is a useful compute-allocation comparison, but it is not equal target-equation training. If the curriculum performs worse, we must not immediately conclude that transfer learning itself is useless. A separate extra-budget diagnostic can examine the allocation issue.

中文讲稿

对照显示了一个重要预算区别。总计四千次更新时，课程式方法只有最后一千步训练 beta 十。直接训练的四千步都用于最终目标。

相同总步数是有用的计算分配比较，但不代表目标方程训练量相同。如果课程式表现较差，不能立即推断迁移学习本身无用。可以用单独的额外预算诊断检查分配问题。

14. The extra-budget control answers a different question

Ntotal=3000+4000=7000N_{\rm total}=3000+4000=7000Ntotal​=3000+4000=7000

To inspect the budget allocation, the extra-budget curriculum keeps the same three warm-up stages. It then receives four thousand target-speed updates instead of one thousand. This gives seven thousand total updates.

The comparison now matches the direct method’s target-stage steps but uses more total computation. Any improvement must be reported with that extra cost, and each run still has the ninety-second training cap. It is a diagnostic about warm starts and time allocation, not an equal-cost winner claim.

中文讲稿

为检查预算分配，额外预算课程保留相同的前三个预热阶段。随后给目标速度四千步，而不是一千步。总计七千次更新。

现在目标阶段步数与直接方法相同，但总计算量更多。任何改进都必须连同额外成本一起报告，并且每次运行仍受九十秒训练上限约束。这是预热与时间分配的诊断，不是等成本优胜主张。

15. The code transfers weights, not the previous equation

for beta in schedule:
    opt = Adam(model.parameters())
    residual = ut + beta*ux
    train_stage(model, opt, residual)

The continuation mechanism can now be connected to code. The same model object survives between stages, while the optimiser is recreated according to the declared reset policy. The residual uses the current stage’s known speed.

Initial labels and periodic constraints are unchanged because every stage starts from the same sine profile on the same periodic domain. We never mix a saved residual from the previous equation into the new objective. The full runnable script records each stage boundary and parameter checksum.

中文讲稿

现在把延续机制连接到代码。同一个模型对象在阶段之间保留，优化器按声明的重置策略重新建立。残差使用当前阶段的已知速度。

初始标签和周期约束保持不变，因为每阶段都从同一周期区域的正弦曲线出发。不会把旧方程保存的残差混入新目标。完整可运行脚本记录每个阶段边界及参数校验值。

The implementation is deliberately small enough for a local CPU. It has one thousand two hundred and ninety-seven parameters, two hundred and fifty-six residual points, forty-eight initial samples and thirty-two periodic pairs. Every run uses four CPU threads and double precision.

Seeds eleven, twenty-two and thirty-three are paired across four policies, including an easy speed-one control. We save full fields and final-time curves rather than only a scalar loss. The analytic reference is evaluated on a separate eighty-one by twenty-one grid, and residual validation uses five hundred and twelve new points.

实现刻意保持在本机 CPU 可运行的规模。它含一千二百九十七个参数、二百五十六个残差点、四十八个初始样本，以及三十二对周期点。每次运行使用四个 CPU 线程与双精度。

21. What does this small experiment not reproduce?

The controlled comparison teaches a mechanism and a budget trade-off. It does not reproduce every failure mode in the paper, its higher-speed headline cases, or its original optimisation settings. Our target speed ten is tractable for the direct baseline in the measured runs.

We also did not implement reaction-diffusion, time-window sequence-to-sequence learning, or a Hessian landscape study. A claim that continuation always improves accuracy would therefore go beyond the evidence. The useful conclusion is to validate both the transfer path and the computation it consumes.

中文讲稿

受控比较用于解释机制与预算取舍。它没有复现论文的所有失效模式、更高速度的主要案例，或原始优化设置。本地实测中，直接基线可以处理目标速度十。

也没有实现反应扩散、逐时间窗的序列学习，或 Hessian 地形研究。因此，声称延续总能提升精度会超出证据。更有用的结论是同时检验迁移路径及其消耗的计算量。

23. Why focus on temporal order next?

Coefficient continuation changes which physical equation is trained, but each stage still optimises residuals over the entire time interval together. That leaves a separate question: should later-time residuals receive full attention before earlier dynamics are learned. This motivates causal training.

Paper Five continues with temporal residual weights on an analytically checkable spring oscillator. It distinguishes physical time, optimiser updates and coefficient stages rather than treating them as the same axis.
