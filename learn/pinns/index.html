A connected reading route

Follow the numbered papers through forward solutions, inverse identification, gradient diagnostics, coefficient curricula, temporal weighting, residual derivatives, adaptive sampling. Each implementation includes controls and explicit limitations; advanced methods are tested rather than assumed to improve every problem.

What each presentation includes

Physical intuition → explained equations → implementation → controlled experiment → independent validation → limits → why the next paper is needed. English narration is cached with progressive reveals, professor gestures, highlights and laser guidance. Choose English or Chinese. Narration is enabled only when every clip for that presentation and language is cached; otherwise its slides and transcript remain readable. Published classrooms replay saved results without training on your device.

Local CPU budget

Default target: fewer than 5,000 parameters, at most 1,024 residual points, four CPU threads and a 90-second training cap per run. Each paper states its actual settings, seeds and departures from the original benchmark. Failures and engineering modifications are disclosed.
