Before you begin

Assumed background: calculus, linear algebra, introductory differential equations and basic neural networks. The two-hour plan includes student exercises, discussion and a ten-minute break; it is not two hours of uninterrupted recorded speech.

The classroom includes progressive reveals, a 3D Professor Vee, synchronized equation and schematic highlights, a laser pointer and animated heat flow. Computational demonstrations replay independent local Python experiments rather than execute code on the website.

English lectures use prepared British English Kokoro recordings. Chinese slides and transcripts are available throughout; incomplete Chinese narration is clearly marked in the classroom. Narration never starts automatically.

Sources and experiments

The teacher guide links the original PINN papers, training studies and official software examples. Teaching experiments use the dimensionless heat equation with a known sine-mode solution; their results are teaching runs, not reproductions of paper benchmarks.

Live questions

Generating a new explanation or supplementary slides with Codex requires the local Professor Vee application. This public course does not connect visitors to the author's computer or credentials.
