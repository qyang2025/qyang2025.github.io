What each presentation includes

Physical intuition → explained equations → implementation → controlled experiment → independent validation → limits → why the next paper is needed. English narration is cached with progressive reveals, professor gestures, highlights and laser guidance. Chinese slides and transcripts are available in reading mode. Published classrooms replay saved results without training on your device.

Local CPU budget

Default target: fewer than 5,000 parameters, at most 1,024 residual points, four CPU threads and a 90-second training cap per run. Each paper states its actual settings, seeds and departures from the original benchmark. Failures and engineering modifications are disclosed.
