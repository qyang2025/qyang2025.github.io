A curated 16-paper route

This teaching selection covers foundational methods, training mechanisms, variational formulations, multiscale representations, uncertainty and applications. It is an editorial learning route, not a citation ranking. Planned papers below do not yet have published presentations.

Local CPU budget

Default target: fewer than 5,000 parameters, at most 1,024 residual points, four CPU threads and a 90-second training cap per run. Each paper states its actual settings, seeds and departures from the original benchmark. Failures and engineering modifications are disclosed.
